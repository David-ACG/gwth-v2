# Marketing pages triage — 2026-06-12

Survey reshot in full (light + dark, all six pages) into `before/` as
`0N-<slug>-{light,dark}.png`. The May-8 shots (`01-pricing.png`,
`02-for-teams.png`) are kept alongside for history; `03-labs-light.png` was
re-shot in place.

**Dark-mode note:** `/pricing` and `/about` render byte-identical in dark mode.
This is by design, not a survey bug: both delegate to
`src/components/marketing/gwth-redesign/{pricing,about}-page.tsx`, whose CSS
module (`gwth-redesign.module.css`) hardcodes the editorial cream/ink palette
(`--gwth-bg`, `--gwth-ink`, etc.) rather than reading theme tokens. The
`PublicNav` is also fixed cream (0.95 alpha) on every page. Verified with a
computed-style probe (`probe.mjs`): the `.dark` class IS applied; the page
simply ignores it.
