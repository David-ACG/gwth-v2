Common questions

Pricing, billing, what's included.

Is the free tier really free forever?

Yes. Twelve lessons, no card needed, no expiry. You can read them as often as you like. The credential and the full curriculum sit behind the paid tiers.

What does a "seat" mean on the Team plan?

One seat is one learner — their own dashboard, their own score, their own credential. Seats are reassignable while someone is inactive but cannot be shared in real time.

Do I need to pay yearly?

No. The Member plan is available monthly at £14 or yearly at £11/month. The yearly price saves you about 16%. Team plans are typically yearly contracts but we offer monthly for smaller cohorts.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account settings — your access continues to the end of the period you've already paid for. We don't charge cancellation fees and we offer a 30-day refund on first-time Member subscriptions.

Will my credential stay valid if I cancel?

Yes. The credential you earn is yours. Its public verification URL stays live. Your score will continue to decay over time as the curriculum updates — that's the same for everyone, member or not.

Do you offer student or non-profit discounts?

Yes. We offer 50% off the Member plan for students with a valid .edu/.ac email, and bespoke pricing for registered charities. Write to hello@gwth.ai.

What payment methods do you accept?

Card and bank transfer. We use Stripe for card processing and never store card details ourselves. Team plans can be invoiced and paid by bank transfer with net-30 terms.
