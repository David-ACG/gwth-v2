# Gender pay gap, 5 April 2026

**Publication.** We must publish. The test is 250 or more relevant employees on 5 April 2026. Each person we employ that day counts as one, including people on leave and those who work part time. Agency workers do not, because their agency employs them. Hours do not change the count.

We had 262 relevant employees, so the duty applies. About 210 FTE matches the hours, not the legal test. Contracted hours in force that day total 7,857. Divided by 37.5, the most common weekly contract, that is 209.5 FTE.

**Hourly pay.** Figures use 248 employees on full pay. Fourteen were excluded because April pay was reduced: eight on statutory maternity pay, two on statutory sick pay, two on company sick pay at half pay, and two with an unpaid leave deduction. Mean hourly pay was £19.37 for men and £15.50 for women. The mean gender pay gap is 20.0% and the median gap is 5.0%. A positive figure means men are paid more.

The mean is wider than the median because the five highest hourly rates are the five directors, all men. Women are 69.0% of these 248, but 77.4% of the lower quartile, 71.0% of the lower middle, 74.2% of the upper middle and 53.2% of the upper quartile.

**Bonuses.** A bonus with a pay date from 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026 was received by 34.2% of relevant men and 28.4% of relevant women. Bonuses are not adjusted for hours. Among recipients, the mean bonus gap is 78.1% and the median gap is 73.5% (a median bonus of £1,700 for men and £450 for women).

**Ethnicity.** We cannot report an ethnicity pay gap. Ethnicity is not recorded in these files, and it cannot be worked out reliably from names or any other field.

**Data notes.** Sex was stored in several formats, including a trailing space, and was read as male or female. Fifteen identical payroll lines were counted once. Hours are those in force on 5 April, not later changes already agreed. Bonuses follow the pay date, so payments on 4 April 2025 are outside the year and payments on 2 April 2026 are inside it. One annual bonus paid in error was reversed in full and does not count. Five people in the April payroll joined after the snapshot and are not relevant employees.
