To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Date: Monday 5 October 2026

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% in a year, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter, from 16.8% to 12.6%. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires a business of our size to do. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, operatives, technicians, administrators and team leaders received a 5% pay rise. Managers received 3.5%, and the senior leadership team received 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%. Including long-term absence, sickness absence was 4.2%.

**Growing.** At the end of March, Hollinsford was our largest site, with 402 people, and Thorncliffe had 371. Our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles in the last financial year, and 58 of those were filled at the end of March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of those invited took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year, from 68% to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
