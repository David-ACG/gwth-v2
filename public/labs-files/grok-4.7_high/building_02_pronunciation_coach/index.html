      How the score is worked out

      Each word of the phrase is matched, in order, with what the listener heard. Capitals, accents and punctuation are ignored for the match, including apostrophes. Green is a match at 0.85 or more. Amber is a match at 0.60 or more, but below 0.85. Red is a weaker match, or a word that was missed.

      The score is the average confidence across the phrase’s words, multiplied by 100 and rounded to the nearest whole number. Each extra word takes off 5 points. The score never goes below 0.
