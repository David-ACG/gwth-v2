# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% in a year, almost halving, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as required by law for employers of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a 5% pay rise, with 3.5% for managers and 2% for senior leaders.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Thank you for staying with us and for helping new colleagues settle in. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe has grown to 371 people, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new roles, with 58 filled by March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: just under four in five of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, six points since 2024. Please keep telling us what matters so we can keep improving.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. Please speak to your line leader if you would like to do more training.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
