# Fernhill Kitchens — Four-Day Week Pilot Plan
For Nina Ashworth | 12 weeks: Mon 4 Jan – Sun 28 Mar 2027

## Does your proposal work as written?
No, not for the showroom. Mon–Thu / Tue–Fri with Saturdays "as now" gives contracted showroom cover of 6/12/12/12/6/0 from Mon to Sat. Saturday needs 6, so the shortfall is 6.

Even with overtime, only four showroom staff have volunteered (S02, S07, S09, S11). That lifts Saturday to 4, still short of 6, with one key-holder but only one designer against two required. Cost is £617.40 per Saturday, £7,408.80 for 12 Saturdays, and still non-compliant. Total contracted hours fall from 1,500 to 1,280 a week (-14.67%) on same pay.

The 87% is 13 of 15 respondents, not of staff. Only 15 of 40 replied (37.5%), via an optional WhatsApp link, skewed to Office (7 of 8 replied, but 3 of 12 Showroom and 3 of 14 Fitting). Yes is 32.5% of all staff. Treat as indicative only.

## Your options
A. Proceed as written. Simple and even, but Saturdays fall below your minimum cover; overtime does not close the gap.

B. Staggered showroom rota within 32 hours, below, with office, fitting and warehouse on your A/B patterns. Meets minima with no overtime.

C. Narrow or defer. Pilot non-showroom teams only, or pause to re-survey all 40 properly and resolve Saturday cover. Protects Saturday service but loses a whole-company test.

## A workable pilot — showroom
32 hours, four days each, Saturday as a contracted day for seven staff. S05 and S06 stay off Saturdays for childcare.

Rota: S01, S07: Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat. S02: Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat. S04, S11: Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat. S09: Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat. S10: Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat. S03, S05: Mon–Thu. S06, S08: Tue–Fri. S12: Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri. Full pattern in `out/numbers.json`.

Daily cover (minimum in brackets): Mon 7 (5), Tue 9 (5), Wed 8 (5), Thu 9 (5), Fri 8 (5), Sat 7 (6). Each weekday has at least one key-holder (S01, S02, S08) and one designer (S03, S04, S09, S10); Saturday has two key-holders and three designers, so one absence still meets the minimum on your busiest day.

Non-showroom: half Mon–Thu, half Tue–Fri. Tue–Thu full cover, Mon/Fri half cover; plan handovers. Bank holiday Fri 26 Mar: all closed. Easter Mon 29 Mar falls just after the pilot.

## How to judge success
Compare with Sep–Dec 2026: appointments kept/cancelled, Saturday conversion and sales, fitting lead-times and rework, complaints, sickness/absence, cover breaches, overtime spend, applications/resignations (especially fitters/designers), and a repeat survey of all 40. Set thresholds before 4 Jan, for example no rise in cancellations, breaches or sustained overtime.

## Review
Run all 12 Saturdays, collate data in week of 29 Mar, then decision meeting Wed 14 Apr 2027, where you decide to continue, amend and rotate the Saturday group, or revert.
