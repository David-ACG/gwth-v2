# Four-day week pilot: plan for Nina

## Does the proposal work as written?
**Not for the showroom.** Patterns A and B (Mon–Thu, Tue–Fri) put all 12 showroom staff on weekdays, so **nobody is in on Saturday**: cover is 0 against a minimum of 6. Weekdays are fine (Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6).

Overtime doesn't rescue it. Only four showroom volunteers exist (S02, S07, S09, S11), giving 4 of 6, with one designer where two are needed. It would also cost £617.40 a Saturday, £7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays (gross, 1.5× for 7 hours).

Two other points:
- **The 87% figure is weak.** 15 of 40 staff replied (37.5%) via a WhatsApp group, so it is self-selected. Yes = 13, or 32.5% of all staff. Only 3 of 12 showroom staff replied, and one comment doubts Saturdays will work.
- **Hours:** 1,500 to 1,280 a week, a 14.67% cut. Good Friday (26 March 2027) is the only bank holiday in the pilot. Decide how it affects a 32-hour week for Tue–Fri staff.

## Options (your decision)
1. **Proposal as written, with overtime.** Cheapest to describe, but leaves Saturday short and costs about £7.4k.
2. **Four-day week with Saturdays built into the showroom pattern** (below). No overtime, but showroom staff lose the fixed A/B pattern, and Saturday workers have a different day off.
3. **Pilot office, fitting and warehouse only (A/B patterns); showroom stays at five days.** Simplest, but excludes the team with the hardest recruitment story and gives no showroom evidence.
4. **Delay** until the showroom pattern is agreed with the team.

## Pilot (option 2)
Starts Mon 4 Jan 2027, 12 weeks, ending Sun 28 Mar. Non-showroom staff use A/B, balanced half and half.

**Showroom days:**
- **Mon (7 staff):** S02, S03, S05, S08, S09, S11, S12
- **Tue (8):** S02, S03, S05, S06, S07, S08, S09, S10
- **Wed (9):** S01, S02, S04, S05, S06, S07, S09, S10, S11
- **Thu (9):** S01, S02, S04, S05, S06, S08, S09, S10, S12
- **Fri (9):** S01, S03, S04, S06, S07, S08, S10, S11, S12
- **Sat (6):** S01, S03, S04, S07, S11, S12

Every day has a key holder and a designer, and Saturday has S01 as key holder with designers S03 and S04. Weekdays have 2+ key holders and 2+ designers, so one absence is absorbed. **Saturday has no slack**: if any of the six is off, call volunteers S02 (key holder) or S09 (designer) as paid overtime, which should be a rare cost. Mon–Fri days off vary by person, so check this with the team. Fixed Saturday staff may find it unfair, so consider rotating after week 6.

## Measuring success
Compare with Oct–Dec 2026 where possible:
- Saturday and weekday cover met (target 100% of days at minimum).
- Overtime hours and cost (budget: under £1,000).
- Design appointments and sales, with Saturday appointments tracked.
- Fitting completions and delivery lead times.
- Sickness absence and staff pulse survey at weeks 0, 6 and 12 (aim for responses from all 40 this time).
- Applications for open fitter and designer roles.
