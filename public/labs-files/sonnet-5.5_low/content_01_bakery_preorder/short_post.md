We sold out by 9.40am again. Some of you walked a long way and went home with nothing.

I hate that. You got up early for bread and we had none left to sell you.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Here is how it works. You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. You pay when you order. Then you collect your bread on Saturday between 8am and 11am.

That's the whole change. Weekday sales stay walk-in, just as they are now.

Why are we doing it? There is one oven and one me. We can bake 64 loaves on a Saturday, and that is the most we can do. Pre-orders mean the 64 go to people who asked for them, not to whoever got here first.

It also helps with waste. On a quiet week we throw away about 6 loaves.

If you can't make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday and you get a full refund. After that we can't refund you, because the bread has been baked for you.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. So it won't be wasted, but I'd rather it ended up with you.

We haven't tried this before, so we may get things wrong. I'll tell you if we do, and we'll fix it. I'd rather you heard it from me than guessed.

Questions about how it works? Ask in the comments or send me a message. I read every one.
