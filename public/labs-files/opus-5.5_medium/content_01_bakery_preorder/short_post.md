We keep selling out by 9.40am on Saturdays.

That sounds like a nice problem. It isn't, if you've walked across Leeds and found an empty shelf. You got up early for bread, and we let you down. I hate that part.

Here's the thing I can't change. I have one oven, and it bakes 64 loaves on a Saturday. I've asked it nicely for more. It doesn't listen.

On quiet weeks it goes the other way. We throw away about 6 loaves. That's flour, time and money in the bin, and it bothers me too.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

How it works:
- Order on our website by 8pm Thursday.
- Pay when you order.
- Collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday.

Paying upfront might feel a bit formal for a loaf of bread. It means your loaf is waiting for you, not gone by 9.40am.

If your plans change, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that we can't refund, because the bread is baked for you. This doesn't change your rights if something is wrong with the loaf itself.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Your loaf still gets eaten, just by someone else.

Weekdays stay the same. You can walk in and buy bread as you do now, no ordering needed.

We haven't done this before. I expect to get a few things wrong in the first weeks, and I'd rather hear about it than guess. I read every comment and message.

If you want bread on Saturday 7 November, order on our website by 8pm on Thursday 5 November.
