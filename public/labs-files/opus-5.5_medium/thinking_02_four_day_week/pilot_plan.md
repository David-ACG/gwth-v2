# Four-day week pilot: plan for decision (board, 15 October 2026)

## Does the proposal work as written?

**No, because Saturdays in the showroom aren't covered.** Patterns A (Mon–Thu) and B (Tue–Fri) leave nobody rostered on a Saturday. Showroom cover would be Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6, **Sat 0**, against a Saturday minimum of 6 (a key holder and 2 designers) on our busiest day.

Overtime doesn't fix it. Only four showroom staff have volunteered (S02, S07, S09, S11). That's **2 short, with 1 designer instead of 2**. It would also cost **£617.40 a Saturday (£7,408.80 over the pilot)**, with the same four people working six days a week.

Also note:
- **The 87% is a share of the people who replied.** There were 15 responses from 40 staff (37.5%). The 13 "Yes" answers are **32.5% of all staff**. Only 3 of 12 showroom staff replied. The link could be forwarded, so we can't check who answered.
- **Contracted hours fall 14.67%** (1,500 to 1,280 a week) at the same pay. Output per hour must rise about 17% to stand still. Fitters on Mondays and Fridays halve, from 14 to 7.

## Realistic options

1. **Pilot with a separate showroom rota (below).** Four days each, with no overtime needed.
2. **Pilot without the showroom.** Simpler, but it creates a fairness problem.
3. **Delay to April.** Run a proper survey and collect baseline data first.
4. **Don't go ahead.**

## Pilot (option 1)

**Dates:** Mon 4 January to Sun 28 March 2027 (12 weeks, 12 Saturdays). Good Friday, **26 March**, is a bank holiday. Other teams use Patterns A/B as proposed.

**Showroom pattern:**

- S01, S05, S09: Mon–Thu
- S02, S06, S11: Tue–Fri
- S03: Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat
- S04: Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat
- S07, S08: Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat
- S10: Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat
- S12: Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat

**Cover each day:**

| Day | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Staff (min) | 8 (5) | 9 (5) | 9 (5) | 8 (5) | 8 (5) | 6 (6) |
| Key holders | 2 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| Designers | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 |

- **Saturday has no slack.** Volunteers are kept off the Saturday rota so they can cover absences as overtime (£138.60–£176.40 per person). S02 covers the key holder, S09 a designer, and S07 or S11 anyone else.
- S05 and S06 stay on weekdays only, in line with their childcare arrangements. Check with them before confirming.
- Like-for-like swaps are allowed if the manager approves.

**Settle first:**
- Saturday shifts are 7 paid hours, so Saturday staff work 31 hours, not 32.
- Good Friday's effect on pro-rata leave for Friday workers.
- Written, temporary contract changes with a right to revert.

## Measuring success

Compare against a baseline from Jan–Mar 2026 and Oct–Dec 2026. **Fix the pass/fail thresholds before 4 January.**

- Revenue, plus design appointments booked and converted (Saturdays tracked separately)
- Fitting jobs on schedule, on-time deliveries, complaints
- Days below minimum cover (target: zero), and overtime cost
- Sickness absence, staff leaving, job applications
- An anonymous survey of all staff, one response per person, in December and at weeks 6 and 12
