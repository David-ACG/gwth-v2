# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has almost halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. As an employer of our size, we publish our gender pay gap every year, as the law requires. In March, women held 9 of our 22 filled senior leadership posts (41%), compared with 8 of 23 (35%) a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, everyone in grades 1 to 6 received a 5% pay rise, as agreed with the union. Managers received 3.5% and the senior leadership team 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. That puts us below the median for food manufacturers in the North of England. Sickness absence fell from 4.5% to 4.2%, though that is still above the sector average of 3.6%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is now our second-largest site, and our Line 4 investment there has created 64 new permanent jobs.

**Your voice.** Thank you to the 972 people who filled in the PulsePoint survey, almost four in five of those invited. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, and is up six points since 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
