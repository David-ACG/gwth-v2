We keep selling out by 9.40am on Saturdays. So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Selling out sounds like a nice problem. It isn't, if you've walked over for nothing. I heard from 3 people who'd walked from Headingley to an empty shelf. You made the effort, and we let you down. I'm sorry.

The other problem is the opposite. On a quiet week, we throw away about 6 loaves. That's bread baked for nobody. Neither is good enough, and pre-ordering is the fairest idea I've got.

You order and pay on our website by 8pm on the Thursday before. By then, I'll know exactly how much to bake. That should mean less waste.

You collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. There won't be spare loaves to buy on the day.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Better eaten than binned.

Can't make it? You can cancel by 8pm on Thursday for a full refund. After that, there's no refund, because your bread is baked for you. None of this changes your legal rights if we get something wrong.

Pre-ordering doesn't make the oven any bigger. I can still bake 64 loaves on a Saturday, and no more. Once they're all ordered, that Saturday is full. The difference is you'd find out when you order, not at our door.

I know this asks more of you. You'll need to order by Thursday, not just turn up. If that doesn't suit you, weekdays stay walk-in, as now.

We haven't done this before, so 7 November might wobble a bit. If it does, I'll say so.
