# Four-day week: pilot plan

## Does it work as written?

**Not for the showroom.**

- **Saturdays are empty.** Patterns A (Mon–Thu) and B (Tue–Fri) roster nobody on Saturday: 0 against a minimum of 6, on the day with a third of design appointments. Midweek gets 12 (minimum 5).
- **Overtime can't fill it.** Only four volunteers can cover the showroom (Imran, Callum, Owen, Ben; Lewis is a fitter): 4 staff, 1 designer, still 2 people and a designer short, for £617.40 a Saturday (£7,408.80 over 12), with those four working 39 hours over five days.
- **"87%" is 13 of 15 replies**: 32.5% of all 40 staff, on a 37.5% response rate. 7 of 8 office staff replied; only 3 of 12 showroom staff and 3 of 14 fitters. The anonymous link could be forwarded, so replies can't be verified. 11 of 15 prefer Fridays off, which a 50/50 split can't give.
- **Hours fall 14.67%** (1,500 to 1,280 a week) on the same pay: each hour must produce about 17% more to hold output. Days lengthen to 8 hours, a concern raised for fitters.

## Options

1. **As written:** Saturday falls short every week, even with every volunteer.
2. **Amended pilot (below):** whole company; showroom patterns include Saturday. Meets every minimum without overtime, but more complex, and Saturday staff must agree.
3. **Pilot without the showroom:** simpler, but two-tier, and leaves out the designers you most want to recruit.
4. **Delay:** consult all 40 staff on real patterns, then start later or not at all; recruitment benefits wait.

## Pilot (option 2)

**Monday 4 January to Sunday 28 March 2027:** 12 weeks, 12 Saturdays. Good Friday (26 March) is the only bank holiday.

- C and D each have a key holder, designer and consultant. Two key holders every day, and every weekday survives one absence.
- **Saturday absence:** Rachel, Hannah, Tomasz or Amira swaps a weekday for that Saturday (Hannah or Tomasz for a designer). Not overtime; weekdays still meet the minimum.
- Ella and Joy stay off Saturdays (childcare); confirm their weekdays.
- C and D total 31 hours (Saturday is 7 paid hours): accept that or add an hour.
- Saturdays can rotate by swapping whole patterns between same-role colleagues; cover stays identical.
- **Other teams:** A or B by preference where cover allows; plan around days off for Karen and Helen (sole warehouse/office key holders) and our only electrician and plumber.
- Agree temporary terms in writing; count leave and bank holidays in hours (Good Friday is Pattern A's day off).

## Measuring success

Set pass marks before January against the same weeks of 2026:

- **Business:** orders (number and value), design appointments and wait for the next slot, kitchens fitted and deliveries made on time, complaints.
- **Cover:** Saturdays at minimum without overtime (12 of 12).
- **People:** survey all staff (one reply each) in December and week 11; sickness; leavers; applicants for fitter and designer jobs.

## Review

- **Weekly:** Rachel logs cover, swaps and overtime.
- **Mid-point check, Thursday 18 February 2027,** against stop triggers agreed in advance.
- **Decision meeting, Thursday 15 April 2027:** after Easter, with final figures and survey in; pilot patterns continue until then.
