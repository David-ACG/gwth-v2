# Fernhill Kitchens: four-day week decision and pilot

**Does the proposal work?** No. Basic pay stays the same while contracted hours fall from 1,500 to 1,280 a week (14.67%). Showroom staffing Monday–Saturday would be 6/12/12/12/6/0; Friday's sole key holder, S08, needs break and absence relief.

Even if all four showroom overtime volunteers attend, that gives four Saturday staff, including one designer, against a minimum of six people and two designers. Seven-hour shifts cost £617.40 gross per Saturday, or £7,408.80 for 12. Extra staff and break cover would still be needed.

The survey's 87% is 13 of 15 respondents: 37.5% of staff responded and yes answers were 32.5% of all staff. It was voluntary and shared through WhatsApp; three showroom staff responded.

**Options.** Keep the current week; retain the proposed patterns with extra qualified Saturday staff, overtime and Friday relief; close Saturdays, risking a third of design appointments and still needing Friday relief; or use the rota below.

**Possible pilot: 4 January–28 March 2027.** This fixed weekly showroom rota uses staff IDs. S05 and S06 retain their no-Saturday arrangements. Seven staff would work all 12 Saturdays rather than rotate as now; consult them and obtain agreement.

Weekdays are 09:00–17:30 with a 30-minute unpaid lunch: eight paid hours. Saturday's 09:00–16:30 shift, including lunch, is seven paid hours, followed by one paid hour of administration after closing. Confirm useful work and agreement to the later finish. Each person works four eight-hour days. Stagger breaks one at a time to maintain minimum cover.

Cover absence or leave by agreed swaps or voluntary overtime; check skills and breaks. S02 (key holder), S09 (designer) and S11 (sales) are off Saturday and have volunteered: confirm availability. Overtime pays 1.5 times the hourly rate. Delay opening or close affected hours if cover fails.

Split fitting, warehouse and office teams 7/7, 3/3 and 4/4 between Monday–Thursday and Tuesday–Friday. Assign the sole electrician F08 to the former and plumber F09 to the latter; schedule joint jobs Tuesday–Thursday. Check longer eight-hour days with fitters.

All teams close on Good Friday, 26 March. Give anyone normally off Friday eight hours' paid leave in lieu; preserve contractual annual leave and bank-holiday benefits, and schedule leave with cover.

**Measure and review.** Agree an overtime budget and baselines before launch. Suggested targets: full cover during opening hours; maintain comparable-season sales, appointments and completed fittings per open day; no increase in rework per job; keep overtime within budget. Track complaints, absence, overtime and qualified applications weekly; survey all staff anonymously on workload and wellbeing at start and end. Review on 15 February and decide whether to continue, amend or stop on **30 March 2027**. Arrange that day's cover in advance; Easter Monday, 29 March, is closed.
