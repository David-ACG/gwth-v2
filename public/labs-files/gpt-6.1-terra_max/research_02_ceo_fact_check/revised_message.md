# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% in the 2025 report, and our mean gap fell by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap reports for the last two years, in line with the legal requirement for businesses of our size. At 31 March 2026, women held 9 of 22 filled senior leadership posts (41%), up from 8 of 23 (35%) a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, basic pay rose by 5% for grades 1–6, 3.5% for grades 7–8 and 2% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% in 2024/25 to 18.1% in 2025/26. Sickness absence, including long-term absence, fell from 4.5% to 4.2% in 2025/26, compared with 3.6% in a North of England food-and-drink manufacturing survey for calendar 2025.

**Growing.** At 31 March 2026, Hollinsford was our largest site. Our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 permanent roles in 2025/26, of which 58 were filled by 31 March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 972 of the 1,231 employees and long-term agency workers invited took part, a 79% response rate. Our engagement score rose four percentage points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. Thank you to everyone who helps welcome our new starters.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
