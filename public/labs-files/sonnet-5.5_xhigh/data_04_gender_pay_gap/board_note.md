# Gender pay gap: board note (snapshot 5 April 2026)

## Must we publish? Yes

The threshold of 250 counts people, not full-time equivalents. Everyone employed on 5 April 2026 counts, including part-timers and people on leave. We had **262** relevant employees (183 women, 79 men), so we must publish. Graham's figure of about 210 FTE is right, but it is not the test. Agency workers (14), leavers before 5 April and joiners after it are excluded. Publication is due within 12 months of the snapshot, by 4 April 2027.

## Figures

248 employees were on full pay in April (171 women, 77 men). The other 14 were on maternity, sick or unpaid leave and are left out of the pay figures. A positive gap means men are paid more.

| | Men | Women | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mean hourly pay | £19.37 | £15.50 | 20.0% |
| Median hourly pay | £14.63 | £13.91 | 5.0% |
| Mean bonus | £3,857 | £846 | 78.1% |
| Median bonus | £1,700 | £450 | 73.5% |

34.2% of men and 28.4% of women received a bonus. Women are 69% of full-pay staff but 77.4%, 71.0%, 74.2% and 53.2% of the lowest to highest pay quartiles (see chart).

**What it means:** the five directors, all men, are the five highest paid and received the five largest bonuses (£9,250 to £19,650). Without them the mean pay gap would be about 7.5%. The median gap of 5.0% is the better guide to typical pay. Fewer women reach the top quartile.

## Corrected in the data

- 15 payroll lines appeared twice with identical line numbers. I counted each once. Three were bonuses that would have been doubled.
- Sex was recorded in eight formats. I standardised it to M or F.
- A £1,500 bonus paid in November 2025 was reversed in December. It nets to zero, so that person is not a recipient.
- Four bonus recipients left before 5 April and are not counted.
- Hours changes agreed for after 5 April were ignored.

## Could not work out

- **Ethnicity pay gap: null.** No file records ethnicity, so no figure is possible. We would first need to collect it, voluntarily and with consent.
- Sex is as recorded at onboarding, so it may not match anyone's current legal sex.
