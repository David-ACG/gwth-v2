# Four-day week pilot: plan for your 15 October decision

## Does the proposal work as written?

**Not for the showroom.** I have no cover data for fitting, warehouse or office, so I can't test half-strength Mondays and Fridays there.

- **Hours:** 1,500 a week now, 1,280 in the pilot (down 14.67%) on the same pay. Days lengthen from 7.5 to 8 hours, which a survey comment warned against for fitters.
- **Saturday:** Patterns A and B put nobody in. Cover would be Mon 6, Tue-Thu 12, Fri 6, **Sat 0 against a minimum of 6**.
- **Overtime doesn't close the gap.** Only four showroom staff volunteered (S02, S07, S09, S11); the fitter volunteer can't cover the showroom. That gives 4 on Saturday: two short, with one designer against two required. It also costs £617.40 a Saturday, £7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays, and relies on overtime that happened twice in 2026.
- **Survey:** 87% is of 15 respondents. That is 37.5% of staff; the 13 "yes" answers are 32.5% of all 40. It went via a WhatsApp group and only 3 of 12 showroom staff replied. Encouraging, not a mandate.
- **Good Friday (26 March)** is the only bank holiday in the pilot. Anyone with Friday in their pattern loses a working day that week; Mon-Thu staff don't. Decide how to treat that beforehand.

## Your options

1. **As proposed, plus overtime.** Fails the Saturday minimum even with every volunteer.
2. **Saturday inside the four days** (pilot below). No overtime, every minimum met. Cost: seven people work every Saturday (now about three in five), and a 7-hour Saturday leaves them 1 hour short of 32 unless weekdays gain 20 minutes.
3. **Everyone except the showroom** (28 staff). Lowest risk, but it excludes 12 colleagues and your designers, a hard-to-recruit group.
4. **Wait.** Gather baseline data and run a proper all-staff survey first; the January start goes.

## Pilot: Mon 4 January to Sun 28 March 2027 (option 2)

**Showroom pattern** (S05 and S06 never Saturday):
- Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat: S01, S03, S04, S11
- Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat: S02, S07, S09
- Mon-Thu: S05, S06, S08, S10
- Tue-Fri: S12

Everyone else: Mon-Thu or Tue-Fri, half each.

**Cover by day:**

| | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Staff (min 5; Sat 6) | 8 | 9 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 7 |
| Key holders (min 1) | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Designers (min 1; Sat 2) | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 |

Losing any one person on any day still meets every minimum. Beyond that, ask S08, S10 or S12 for paid Saturday overtime.

**Measures** (against the same weeks of 2026; you set thresholds):
1. Minimum cover met every open day; two breaches in a month pauses the showroom.
2. Appointments held (including Saturdays) and quote-to-order conversion no worse than baseline.
3. Fitting jobs on time, deliveries and complaints no worse than baseline.
4. Anonymous survey of all staff before and at week 12 (aim for 80% replies), plus sickness, leavers, applications per vacancy.
5. Overtime spend.

**Review:** checks after weeks 4 and 8. Final review **Tuesday 13 April 2027**, two weeks after the pilot, with full data. Then keep, adjust or stop.

**Before January:** agree 32-hour terms in writing with a right to revert; check S05's and S06's childcare arrangements.

The decision is yours.
