# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% last year, and our mean gap fell by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap reports for the last two years, as required by law for employers of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a pay rise of 5%, grades 7 to 8 received 3.5%, and our senior leadership team received 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Our short-term sickness absence rate was 3.1%; including long-term absence, it was 4.2%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is now our largest site, and Line 4 there has created 64 new permanent roles. A further 56 roles depend on a second customer contract.

**Your voice.** Thank you to the 972 people who took part in the PulsePoint survey, a 79% response rate among invitees. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, six points higher than in 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. In our last onboarding survey, 87% of respondents rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
