At 9.40am on Saturday, the bread was gone. I was still in my coat when the oven hit 250 at 4.10am, so I know what went into making it.

From Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread will be pre-order only. You can order on our website and pay by 8pm on Thursday. Collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday.

We sell out early. People walk a long way and sometimes find an empty shelf. We also throw away about 6 loaves on a quiet week. I want to make the number we bake closer to the number you need.

There is a limit. One oven gives us room for 64 loaves on Saturday. Pre-orders help us plan, but they don't make the oven bigger. If Saturday bread is what you came for, you will need to order ahead. Weekday bread stays walk-in as usual.

If you can't collect, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that, we can't refund the order. The bread is baked for you. Anything not collected by 11am Saturday goes to the Chapeltown food bank.

I haven't tried this before, so I don't know yet how it will work in practice. I don't know how many people will order, or whether the collection window will suit you. We'll pay attention and adjust if we need to. The numbers tell me there is a problem, but they don't tell me exactly what will fix it.

Please place your Saturday order on our website by 8pm Thursday.
