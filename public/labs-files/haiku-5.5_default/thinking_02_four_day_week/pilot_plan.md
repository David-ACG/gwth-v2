# Four-day week: review of the proposal and a pilot

**Board date:** the 15 October board meeting has passed. Confirm the decision is still open before the 4 January 2027 start.

## 1. Does the proposal work as written? No.

- **Hours:** 1,500 a week falls to 1,280 (down 14.67%).
- **Weekdays work.** Showroom cover under your patterns is Mon 6, Tue 12, Wed 12, Thu 12, Fri 6. Each day meets the minimum of five, with a key holder and a designer.
- **Saturday fails.** Nobody works Saturday on their four pilot days, so cover is 0 against a minimum of 6.
- **Overtime does not close the gap.** Four showroom staff volunteer (S02, S07, S09, S11). All four in gives 4 of 6, with one designer where two are required. Cost: £617.40 per Saturday, £7,408.80 over 12.
- **The rules conflict.** "Saturdays carry on as now" means a rota Saturday on top of four days: five days, which the handbook treats as overtime.
- **The survey is weak evidence.** The 87% is 13 of 15 responses. Only 32.5% of all staff said yes. It was shared through a WhatsApp group, and 7 of 15 responses came from the office (8 staff), against 3 of 14 fitters.

## 2. Options

- **A. Run as written.** Not viable, because Saturday fails every week.
- **B. Four-day pilot with fixed showroom patterns (set out below).** Saturday meets the minimum with no overtime. The cost is that six people work Saturdays permanently. Four who work Saturdays now stop (S01, S09, S10, S12), and S05 and S06 never work Saturdays.
- **C. Four-day week for fitting, warehouse and office only (28 staff).** The showroom stays on 37.5 hours. Hours fall by 154 a week (10.27%), with the least trading risk.
- **D. Change Saturday opening.** Saturday is about a third of design appointments, so this is a commercial call.

I lean towards C for low risk, or B to test the full idea.

## 3. Pilot (option B)

**Showroom patterns, fixed for 12 weeks** (full list in `numbers.json`):
- Saturday group (S02, S03, S04, S07, S08, S11): Saturday plus three weekdays.
- Weekday group (S01, S05, S06, S09, S10, S12): four weekdays.

**Cover by day:** Mon 8, Tue 7, Wed 9, Thu 9, Fri 9, Sat 6. Every day meets the minimum. Saturday has two key holders (S02, S08) and two designers (S03, S04).

**Settle before launch:**
- Saturday-group staff would total 31 paid hours (three 8-hour days and a 7-hour Saturday). Decide how to make up the hour.
- Good Friday, 26 March 2027, is a bank holiday: the showroom closes and Friday-pattern staff lose a day.
- Each person must agree the change to 32 hours in writing. Pay stays the same.

**Measures:**
- Showroom minimum met on every trading day (target 100%).
- Showroom appointments and design sales against the same weeks of 2026, with a threshold agreed now.
- Fitting jobs finished to programme; warehouse on-time deliveries.
- Staff sickness absence, resignations and overtime spend.
- HR-run anonymous survey at weeks 0, 6 and 12, aiming for 70% response.

**Suggested decision rule:** continue only if the showroom minimum holds every trading day, sales stay within your threshold, absence and resignations do not rise, and a majority of all 40 staff support continuing.

**Timeline:**
- Mid-December 2026: decide, obtain written agreements, brief staff.
- Wed 10 February 2027: mid-point check (week 6).
- Mon 4 January to Sun 28 March 2027: pilot (12 Saturdays).
- Tue 6 April 2027: review meeting.
