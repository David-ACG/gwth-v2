# Gender pay gap: 5 April 2026 snapshot

## Do we have to publish?

Yes. The duty applies to employers with 250 or more relevant employees on the snapshot date. We have 262. The chair's figure of about 210 is full-time equivalent (209.5 FTE), but the threshold counts people, not FTE.

## The figures

Pay figures use 248 full-pay relevant employees (77 men, 171 women). Bonus figures use all 262.

- **Mean hourly pay:** men £19.37, women £15.50.
- **Mean gender pay gap:** 20.0%. **Median gap:** 5.0%. Men are paid more on both.
- **Mean bonus gap:** 78.1%. **Median bonus gap:** 73.5%.
- **Receiving a bonus:** 34.2% of men (27 of 79), 28.4% of women (52 of 183).
- **Women in each quartile** (lowest paid first): 77.4%, 71.0%, 74.2%, 53.2%.

What this means: women make up most of the lower-paid groups and just over half of the top quartile. The top five hourly rates are all men; excluding them lowers the mean gap from 20.0% to 7.5%. Men are also more likely to receive a bonus, and the amounts are much larger.

## Corrections to the data

- Removed 15 duplicate payroll lines.
- Standardised eight recorded sex formats (for example "Woman", "female", "male " with a trailing space) to F or M.
- Excluded 14 agency workers, 18 people who left before the snapshot and 5 who joined after it.
- Ignored 33 contracted-hours changes that take effect after 5 April 2026.
- Pay uses the April 2026 monthly run only. Off-cycle bonuses count if paid within the 12 months to 5 April 2026, so the 2 April 2026 long-service bonuses are included and the April 2025 ones are excluded.
- One employee (TC-0335) had a £1,500 bonus in November 2025 reversed in December. The net is zero, so they are not a bonus recipient.
- Fourteen employees on leave in April (maternity, sick or unpaid) are excluded from pay figures.

## What we could not work out

- **Ethnicity pay gap: not calculated (null).** The pack does not include ethnicity, so we cannot give a figure or compare it with the gender gap.
- **Low hourly rates to check.** Fifteen full-pay employees have rates between £12.22 and £12.70. The April 2026 National Living Wage for adults is £12.71 (from outside the pack). Overtime is excluded from these rates, so payroll should check them against actual pay.
