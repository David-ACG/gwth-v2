# People update from Martin

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has almost halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires for employers of our size. At 31 March, women held 41% of our filled senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, everyone on grades 1 to 6 received a pay rise of 5%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Our voluntary turnover is below the North of England food manufacturing median. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe has grown by 59 people in a year and is now our second-largest site. Our Line 4 investment there has created 64 new permanent roles, and 58 were filled by 31 March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: nearly four in five of those invited took part. Our engagement score rose to 72%, up six points since 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
