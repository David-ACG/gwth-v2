**The proposal does not work as written.** Contracted hours fall from 1,500 to 1,280 weekly (14.67%) at unchanged pay. The A/B showroom patterns give Mon–Sat cover of **6, 12, 12, 12, 6, 0**. Weekdays meet headcount and skill minima; Saturday needs six staff, including a key holder and two designers. Continuing the existing Saturday rota on top would mean five working days for those staff and paid overtime.

Only S02, S07, S09 and S11 are eligible Saturday overtime volunteers: four staff, one key holder and one designer. F03 is untrained for showroom cover. All four volunteers cost **£617.40 per Saturday; £7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays**, gross pay only. They still leave a two-person shortfall and one missing designer; willingness is not guaranteed attendance.

**Options:** stagger showroom days across Monday–Saturday within 32 hours; retain A/B but secure and cost additional trained Saturday cover; or change Saturday opening/service, accepting the commercial impact (a third of design appointments occur then). Compressing 37.5 hours into four days requires longer days and feasibility checks.
