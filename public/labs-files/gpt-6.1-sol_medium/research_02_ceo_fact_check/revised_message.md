# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of the progress these numbers show, and they are yours. There is more to do.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% between the 2024 and 2025 snapshots, and our mean gap fell by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires. At March 2026, women held 41% of our filled senior leadership posts, up from 35% in March 2025.

**Pay.** In April, basic pay rose by 5% for grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for grades 7 to 8, and 2% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% in 2024/25 to 18.1% in 2025/26. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%. Including long-term sickness, absence was 4.2%, above the sector average of 3.6%.

**Growing.** At March 2026, Hollinsford remained our largest site, with 402 colleagues, compared with 371 at Thorncliffe. Our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles in 2025/26, with 58 filled at year end.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 972 people took part, 79% of those invited, including employees and long-term agency workers. Our engagement score rose four percentage points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development in 2025/26.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
