# Thistlecombe Care: 5 April 2026 gender pay gap

**We must publish.** The provider's method note sets the threshold at 250 relevant employees on the snapshot date, counted as people, not full-time equivalents. We had **262**: 183 women and 79 men. Part-time employees and employees on leave count. Agency workers, people who had already left and people who joined after the snapshot are excluded. The approximately 210 FTE figure does not remove the publication requirement.

Hourly pay and quartiles cover **248 full-pay relevant employees** (171 women, 77 men). Fourteen employees receiving reduced pay because of leave in the April monthly run are excluded from these figures, but remain included in the headcount and bonus calculations.

Men's mean hourly pay was **£19.37**, compared with **£15.50** for women. The **mean gender pay gap is 20.0%**: women's average hourly pay was 20.0% below men's. The **median gap is 5.0%**, comparing the middle hourly rate for each sex. These describe workforce pay differences; they do not establish whether women and men receive equal pay for the same work.

The **mean bonus gap is 78.1%** and the **median bonus gap is 73.5%**, among recipients. **34.2% of men** (27 of 79) and **28.4% of women** (52 of 183) received a positive net bonus. Bonuses cover payment dates from 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026 inclusive and are not adjusted for part-time hours.

Each quartile contains 62 employees. Women's shares, from lowest to highest pay, are **77.4%, 71.0%, 74.2% and 53.2%**; men's shares are **22.6%, 29.0%, 25.8% and 46.8%**. Women are less represented in the highest-paid quarter than in the other quarters.

We removed **15 exact duplicate payroll lines** (12 April pay lines and three bonus lines), standardised sex labels, matched payroll IDs to employee references, and used contracted hours effective on the snapshot date. Salary-sacrifice deductions retain their negative sign; the bonus reversal cancels the erroneous payment. Hourly pay uses the specified ordinary-pay elements and April's 30 days, excluding overtime, expenses and bonuses. Calculations use unrounded rates; reporting values are rounded.

**Ethnicity pay gap: unavailable (`null`).** The supplied files contain no ethnicity data or defined comparison groups. We cannot reliably calculate it or compare it with the gender gap.
