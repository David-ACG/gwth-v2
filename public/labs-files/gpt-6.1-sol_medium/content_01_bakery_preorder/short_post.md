We’ve been selling out by 9.40am on Saturdays. You can walk a long way for an empty shelf. We’ve let you down, and we need to change that.

From Saturday 7 November 2026, our Saturday bread will be pre-order only. If you usually walk in for a loaf, this changes your Saturday routine.

You’ll order on our website. You’ll pay when you order, so there’s no payment to make at collection. That’s the booking part sorted.

Orders close at 8pm Thursday. That gives us a number to bake towards. It also means deciding on your Saturday bread a little earlier.

Collection is between 8am and 11am on Saturday. That’s the window to plan around if you’re ordering bread for breakfast or later.

We have 1 oven. It does a fair amount of work, but it cannot make every possible Saturday loaf. I have considered staring at it harder.

Our Saturday limit is 64 loaves. Pre-ordering won’t increase that limit. We’re trying to make the bread we can bake fit the orders we receive.

On a quiet week, we throw away about 6 loaves. An empty shelf and wasted bread both happen here. Neither feels like a good way to run a bakery.

If your plans change, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that, there’s no refund for a change of plans. The bread is baked for you.

Anything uncollected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. That means collection has a finish time as well as a start time.

Weekday sales stay walk-in. We haven’t tried Saturday pre-orders before, so we can’t yet say how well they’ll work. The aim is less waste and fewer wasted walks.

Pre-order your Saturday bread on our website by 8pm Thursday.
