# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter, from 16.8% to 12.6%. We have published our gender pay gap voluntarily for the last two years, even though the law does not require a business of our size to do so. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a pay rise of 5%. Managers received 3.5%, and the senior leadership team received 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Our recruitment partner ranks us lowest for turnover among the food manufacturers in its portfolio. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%; including long-term absence, our overall rate was 4.2%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe grew by 59 people last year and is now our second-largest site. Our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new roles, with 58 filled.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of invited people took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. In our most recent onboarding survey, 87% of respondents rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
