# Fernhill Kitchens: four-day week pilot plan

## Does the proposal work as written?

No, not for the showroom. Moving S01–S06 to Monday–Thursday and S07–S12 to Tuesday–Friday leaves **zero** contracted showroom staff on Saturday. The Saturday minimum is six, including one key holder and two kitchen designers, so there is a shortfall of six. Even bringing in every showroom volunteer who currently works Saturdays would add only S02, S07, S09 and S11: four people, still below the minimum. Their gross Saturday overtime cost would be £617.40 per week and £7,408.80 across 12 pilot Saturdays.

The 32-hour, same-pay proposal reduces contracted hours from 1,500 to 1,280 per week: **14.67%**. The survey is not strong evidence: 15 of 40 staff responded (**37.5%**), and 13 "Yes" answers are **32.5%** of all staff.

## Realistic options

1. **Do not pilot yet.** Keep 37.5-hour contracts and design a showroom rota and business measures first.
2. **Hybrid pilot.** Office, fitting and warehouse staff trial 32 hours over four days; showroom staff keep their current 37.5-hour pattern. This is lower risk but gives less showroom evidence.
3. **Full pilot with a purpose-built showroom rota.** Keep 32 hours for all staff, but use fixed four-day patterns that place six trained showroom staff on Saturday. This is the pilot below.
4. **Increase Saturday capacity.** Recruit or contract trained weekend showroom staff before any four-day pilot. This adds lead time and cost but improves fairness and resilience.

## Proposed 12-week pilot

- **Dates:** Monday 4 January to Sunday 28 March 2027. The showroom closes on Good Friday, 26 March.
- **Non-showroom staff:** a balanced mix of Monday–Thursday and Tuesday–Friday, 32 hours at current pay.
- **Showroom:** six rostered staff work Saturday plus three weekdays; the other six work four weekdays. The fixed pilot rota is shown in `numbers.json`.
- **Daily cover:** Monday 8, Tuesday 9, Wednesday 10, Thursday 8, Friday 7, Saturday 6. Every day has at least one key holder and one designer; Saturday has two key holders and two designers.
- **Saturday rota:** S02, S03, S04, S07, S08 and S11 are rostered each pilot Saturday. If one is absent, use a qualified volunteer first; otherwise move a qualified weekday showroom employee to Saturday and backfill their weekday shift. Treat Saturday hours as overtime for anyone already working four contracted days.
- **Baseline:** compare against the equivalent 12 weeks in 2026, adjusted for seasonal promotions, holidays and known one-offs.

## Success measures and review

- **Cover and service:** no shift below the stated minimum; no unplanned closure; completed appointments, quote conversion, orders, revenue, complaints and response times no worse than agreed thresholds.
- **Operations:** installations, deliveries and warranty work completed on schedule; overtime held within budget.
- **People:** sickness, unplanned absence, resignations, a staff pulse survey and a short manager log.
- **Review:** weekly operational check-ins; a formal review in week 11; board decision meeting on **Wednesday 7 April 2027**. Continue, revise, or stop only after reviewing all measures.
