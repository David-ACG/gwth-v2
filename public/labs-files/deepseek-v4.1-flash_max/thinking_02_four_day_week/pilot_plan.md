# Four-day week: pilot plan

**For:** Nina Ashworth, Fernhill Kitchens · **Pilot window:** Mon 4 Jan – Sun 28 Mar 2027

## Does your proposal work as written?

**Not for the showroom.** The fixed Mon–Thu / Tue–Fri patterns leave **no one on duty on Saturday**, against a minimum of 6, and weekday cover is lopsided (Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6). Overtime cannot close the gap: only four showroom staff have volunteered, and even if all four worked every Saturday you would have 4 people — one designer, not the two required — at **£617.40 per Saturday (£7,408.80 over the 12 Saturdays)**.

The other 28 staff (fitting, warehouse, office) can run your A/B patterns as proposed; they do not work weekends.

Two more things to weigh:
- **32 hours for the same pay cuts contracted hours by 14.67%** (1,500 → 1,280 hours a week). That is 17.2% more per hour worked, so output must hold.
- **The survey overstates support.** 87% is 13 of 15 responses. Only 15 of 40 staff answered (37.5%), so “yes” is **32.5% of all staff**. It was self-selected, and only 3 of 12 showroom staff replied.

## Your options

1. **Staggered showroom rota (the pilot below).** All 40 on 32 hours over four days; showroom rest days are spread across Mon–Sat so every day is covered.
2. **Non-showroom teams only.** The other 28 staff trial the four-day week; the showroom stays five days. Easy cover, but two tiers of staff, and skips the showroom designers.
3. **Pause and redesign.** Recruit or train more Saturday-capable staff first (at least two designers, one key holder), or keep 37.5 hours and trial compressed hours.

## The pilot

- 12 weeks, 4 Jan – 28 Mar 2027. All staff move to 32 hours over four 8-hour days, same pay.
- **Non-showroom:** Pattern A Mon–Thu / Pattern B Tue–Fri, split half and half per team.
- **Showroom:** staggered patterns below. S05 (Ella Marsh) and S06 (Joy Adeyemi) keep their agreed no-Saturday arrangements; the other ten work Saturdays, so Saturday duty is shared equally. Saturday is a 7-paid-hour shift (09:00–16:30); one weekday shift is extended so each week totals 32 hours. No Saturday overtime is needed.
- **Closed:** Good Friday, 26 March 2027.

Showroom working days:

- Wed–Sat: S03, S07, S09
- Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat: S01, S10
- Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat: S02, S11
- Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat: S04, S08, S12
- Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri: S05
- Tue–Fri: S06

Daily cover (staff on duty / key holders / designers): **Mon 8/3/2 · Tue 7/2/1 · Wed 7/1/3 · Thu 7/1/3 · Fri 9/2/3 · Sat 10/3/4.** Every day meets the minimum (5 on Mon–Fri with 1 key holder and 1 designer; 6 on Saturday with 1 key holder and 2 designers), with buffer for sickness.

## Measuring success

Baseline: the same 12 weeks in 2026.

- **Cover:** no day below minimum; no missed design appointments.
- **Output:** enquiries, quotes, orders and conversion at or above baseline; installations on time.
- **Customers:** appointment availability, response times, complaints.
- **People:** all-staff pulse at weeks 4, 8 and 12 (target 80%+ response); absence, overtime, resignations.
- **Cost:** overtime or temporary cover, and pay per hour worked, against the 14.67% hours cut.
- **Mid-pilot checkpoint:** Mon 15 Feb 2027 — adjust or stop if cover fails or output drops materially.
