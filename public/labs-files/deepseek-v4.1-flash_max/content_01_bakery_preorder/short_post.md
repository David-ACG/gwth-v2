Three people told me they walked from Headingley and found an empty shelf. We had sold out by 9.40am. I felt awful.

You got up early and walked here, and we had nothing left. That is on us.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. Pay when you order. Collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank.

Weekday sales stay the same. Walk in as normal.

Why the change? I have one oven, and it bakes 64 loaves on a Saturday. Not one more. There is no second oven out the back. I have looked. On a quiet week we also throw away about 6 loaves. I hate that. Pre-orders mean we bake what people want, and your loaf is baked for you.

Pre-orders also help us plan the bake. I know on Friday how much flour to use. Saturday morning is for mixing, proving and baking. Your loaf is set aside until 11am.

I will not pretend this is perfect. It is a change, and change is annoying. A wasted walk is worse.

If you cannot make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday for a full refund. After that there is no refund, because your bread is already baked for you. This does not affect your statutory rights.

I have not tried this before. I do not know how the first Saturdays will go. I will tell you honestly what happens, good or bad.

The first pre-order Saturday is 7 November. Orders close at 8pm on Thursday 5 November.

Order your Saturday loaf on our website before 8pm on Thursday.
