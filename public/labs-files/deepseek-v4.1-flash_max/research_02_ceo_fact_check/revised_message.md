# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has almost halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter, from 16.8% to 12.6%. We publish our gender pay gap every year, as the law requires for a business of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a pay rise of 5%. Managers in grades 7 to 8 received 3.5%, and our senior leadership team received 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%. The sector average of 3.6% includes long-term absence; on that same basis our figure was 4.2%, so we still have work to do.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is now our second-largest site, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new permanent roles.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of those invited took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, up six points since 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year, and in our last induction survey 87% of new starters rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
