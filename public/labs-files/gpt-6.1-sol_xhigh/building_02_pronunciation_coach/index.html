    How your score works

    We match words in order, ignoring capitals, accents and punctuation. Each phrase word gets the listening service’s confidence value, or zero if missed. We average those values, multiply by 100 and round, then subtract 5 for every extra word heard. Scores stop at zero.

    The colours and score reflect the service’s confidence in your words. A lower attempt never lowers your best score. You can try as many times as you like.
