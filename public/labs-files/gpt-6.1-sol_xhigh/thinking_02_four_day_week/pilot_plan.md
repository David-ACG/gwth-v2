# Fernhill Kitchens: four-day week pilot for consideration

Your proposal does not work as written. Weekly contracted hours fall from **1,500 to 1,280 (14.67%)**, with pay unchanged. Showroom cover becomes Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6, Sat 0. Saturday needs six staff, including a key holder and two designers. The four eligible overtime volunteers (S02, S07, S09, S11) provide only four staff and one designer. Their gross overtime costs **£617.40 each Saturday; £7,408.80 over twelve Saturdays**, without solving the shortage. Other teams’ volunteers cannot count as showroom cover.

The survey shows support among respondents, not 87% of all staff: 15/40 responded (**37.5%**); 13/40 said yes (**32.5%**). Optional WhatsApp recruitment and uneven team participation limit its representativeness. Invite all 40 staff directly to a confidential follow-up before launch.

Your options are to retain 37.5 hours with more flexible rotas; compress 37.5 hours into four longer days; or trial 32 hours on unchanged pay using staggered days including Saturdays. Keeping A/B would require additional qualified Saturday staffing or changed opening, risking a day carrying about one-third of appointments. Compression preserves hours but requires longer shifts. The choice remains yours at the **15 October 2026 board meeting**.

If you choose reduced hours, one viable pilot runs **4 January–28 March 2027**. It includes twelve Saturdays and one England bank holiday, **Good Friday, 26 March**, when everyone is closed. Easter Monday, 29 March, falls after the pilot.
