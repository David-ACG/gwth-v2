Last Saturday, we sold out by 9.40am. Three people had walked from Headingley and found an empty shelf. I felt awful.

From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread will be pre-order only. Order on our website by 8pm Thursday and pay when you order. Collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. Anything not collected by 11am goes to Chapeltown food bank.

Weekday bread stays walk-in, as it is now. This change is only for Saturdays.

We have one oven, and 64 loaves is our Saturday limit. Another coffee won't make it 65.

On a quiet week, we throw away about 6 loaves. Meanwhile, people have been making the trip and finding nothing by 9.40am. I want more of the bread to reach people who came for it, and fewer loaves to go spare.

The empty shelf is the part people see. The unsold loaves are the part I see at the end of a quiet week. I would like those two numbers to sit closer together, though I don't know yet if they will.

If your plans change, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that, our policy is no refund, because the bread is baked for you. The cutoff gives us time to plan Saturday's bake.

We haven't tried this before. I don't know how many people will pre-order, or whether the new collection window will work for you. It is a change, and we will have to learn as we go.

If you want Saturday bread from 7 November, place your order on our website by 8pm Thursday.
