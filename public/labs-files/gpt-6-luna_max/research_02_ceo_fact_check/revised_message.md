# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% in a year, a reduction of about 48%, and our mean gap fell by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap reports for the last two years, as required for employers with 250 or more employees. Women now hold 41% of our filled senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, the basic-pay award was 5% for grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for grades 7 to 8, and 2% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last financial year. TalentHarvest says our turnover is the lowest among its food-manufacturer clients. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%; total sickness absence, including long-term cases, was 4.2%.

**Growing.** Hollinsford remains our largest site, with 402 people; Thorncliffe has 371. Line 4 created 64 new permanent roles in 2025/26, with 58 filled by 31 March. A further 56 roles depend on a second customer contract.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 972 of 1,231 invitees took part (79%); invitations included employees and long-term agency workers. Our engagement index reached 72%, up four points from 2025.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in learning and development last year. Our last induction pulse, in 2024, found 87% of respondents rated induction good or excellent (46 responses; 38% response rate).

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
