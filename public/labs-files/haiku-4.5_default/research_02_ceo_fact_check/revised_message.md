# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen significantly in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years as required by law. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, all frontline staff received a pay rise of 5%, reflecting their essential contribution to our business.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year, well below the sector median of 20.4%. Sickness absence was 4.2%, slightly above the sector average of 3.6%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is now our fastest-growing site, and our Line 4 investment there has created 64 new jobs so far, with further expansion planned.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: four in five of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
