We sold out by 9.40am last Saturday. A customer came in from Headingley right after. The shelf was empty. I felt awful.

This happens most weeks. The bread sells fast, which is brilliant. But it means people leave empty-handed. Three customers have told me they've travelled across Leeds hoping to buy from us and found nothing. You got up early. You travelled. We let you down.

Here's why. I have one oven. On a Saturday I can bake 64 loaves maximum. That's it. Not 65, not 70 if I stay later. One oven, two hands, one Saturday morning.

When sales are quiet we throw bread away. About 6 loaves on a slow week. Bread made but unsold. That bothers me. There are people who need food.

So from 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Here's what it means. You order on our website by 8pm Thursday and pay when you order. On Saturday morning come in between 8am and 11am to collect your bread. We've made it, it's ready, you take it home.

Weekday bread doesn't change. Walk in Monday to Friday, buy what we've got.

What if Saturday doesn't work for you this week?

Cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. We haven't started baking yet.

Cancel after 8pm Thursday and there's no refund. Why? The bread is already baked for you. You've paid for it.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to Chapeltown food bank. No waste.
