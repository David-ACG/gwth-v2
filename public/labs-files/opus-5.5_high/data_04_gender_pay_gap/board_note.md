# Gender pay gap: snapshot date 5 April 2026

## Must we publish? Yes.

The 250 threshold counts **people**, not full-time equivalents. On 5 April 2026 we had **262 relevant employees**. Part-timers count as one each, and staff on leave are included. About 210 is our full-time equivalent, which is not the test. Agency workers are excluded. Our report is due by 4 April 2027.

## Figures

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Mean gender pay gap | **20.0%** |
| Median gender pay gap | **5.0%** |
| Mean hourly pay, men / women | £19.37 / £15.50 |
| Mean / median bonus gap | 78.1% / 73.5% |
| Received a bonus, men / women | 34.2% / 28.4% |
| Women in each pay quartile, lowest to highest | 77.4%, 71.0%, 74.2%, 53.2% |

The hourly figures cover the 248 employees on full pay in April 2026.

**What this means.** A positive gap means men are paid more. At the median, men earn 5% more per hour. The mean gap is larger because about 70% of staff are women, but women hold only 53% of the top-paid quarter of jobs, and all five directors are men. The bonus gaps are large mainly because men's bonuses were bigger. Bonuses are not adjusted for part-time hours, and 78% of our part-timers are women.

**Ethnicity pay gap: cannot be calculated.** None of the exports record ethnicity. To report it in future, we would need to collect ethnicity data from staff on a voluntary basis.

## Corrections and decisions

- **Duplicate payroll lines:** 15 lines (12 April pay lines, 3 March 2026 bonuses) appeared twice. We counted each once.
- **Sex** was recorded in eight formats (e.g. "F", "Woman", "male "). We standardised them.
- **Bonus paid in error:** one November 2025 bonus was reversed in December. The two cancel out.
- **Bonus period:** long-service bonuses paid on 4 April 2025 fall outside the 12-month period. Those paid on 2 April 2026 are included.
- **Hours:** we used the hours in force on 5 April. We ignored changes agreed for later dates.
- **Leave:** 14 employees on maternity, sick or unpaid leave are excluded from the hourly figures but still count in the headcount and bonus figures.
- **Joiners and leavers:** we excluded five people who started after 5 April and anyone who left before it.
