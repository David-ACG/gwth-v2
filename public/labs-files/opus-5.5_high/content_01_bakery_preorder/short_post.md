Most Saturdays, our shelf is empty by 9.40am.

Some of you walk all the way from Headingley for a loaf. You get here and there's nothing left. I hate that, and it keeps happening.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Here's how it works. You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. You pay when you order. Then you collect your bread between 8am and 11am on Saturday.

That means no walk-in bread on Saturdays from 7 November. Every Saturday loaf will already belong to someone.

Weekdays stay the same. You can still walk in and buy bread, just as you do now.

Why the change? We have a single oven. On a Saturday it bakes 64 loaves, and not one more. I've tried. The oven doesn't care how early I get up.

Right now, those 64 loaves go to whoever arrives first. That's fine if you live round the corner. It's not fine if you've come on 2 buses. Pre-orders mean the loaves go to the people who asked for them.

It helps with waste too. On a quiet week, we throw away about 6 loaves. I'd rather bake what you've actually ordered.

If you can't make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday. You'll get a full refund. After that, we can't refund you, because your loaf is baked for you.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank.

We've never done this before. Some of it will probably need fixing. I'll tell you honestly what isn't working.

To get bread on Saturday 7 November, order on our website by 8pm on Thursday 5 November.
