# Four-day week pilot: plan for the 15 October board

## Does the proposal work as written?

**Not in the showroom.**

- Neither pattern includes Saturday, so Saturday cover is **0 of the 6 needed**.
- Only four showroom staff volunteer for overtime (S02, S07, S09, S11). With all four every week, Saturday has **4 staff and 1 designer, against a minimum of 6 staff and 2 designers**. That costs **£617.40 a Saturday (£7,408.80 over 12)** and still falls short of minimum cover.
- **The survey doesn't show 87% support.** 15 of 40 staff replied (37.5%). The 13 "Yes" answers are **32.5% of all staff**. Only 3 of the 12 showroom staff replied, and the link could be forwarded. Friday was the preferred day off by 11 to 4.
- Hours fall from 1,500 to 1,280 a week (**−14.67%**) on the same pay. Days get longer, from 7.5 to 8 hours.
- **Good Friday (26 March 2027)** falls in the pilot and only affects staff who work Fridays, so you need a pro-rata rule.

## Realistic options

1. **Pilot with a separate showroom pattern** (below). Other teams use A and B. Cover is met every day with no planned overtime.
2. **Pilot outside the showroom only** (28 staff). This creates two tiers and leaves out the designers you struggle to recruit.
3. **Delay to April 2027** and survey all staff properly first.
4. **Don't pilot.**

## Pilot showroom pattern

Everyone works four 8-hour days. A fixed Saturday team of six who already work Saturdays leaves the four overtime volunteers free to cover absences.

| Staff | Days | Staff | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01 (KH) | Wed Thu Fri Sat | S07 | Mon–Thu |
| S02 (KH) | Mon–Thu | S08 (KH) | Mon Tue Fri Sat |
| S03 (D) | Mon Tue Fri Sat | S09 (D) | Mon–Thu |
| S04 (D) | Wed Thu Fri Sat | S10 (D) | Mon Thu Fri Sat |
| S05 | Mon–Thu | S11 | Tue–Fri |
| S06 | Tue–Fri | S12 | Mon Wed Fri Sat |

KH = key holder, D = designer.

**Cover:** Mon 8, Tue 8, Wed 9, Thu 9, Fri 8, Sat 6. Two key holders and at least two designers every day.

**To settle first:**
- The Saturday six would go from about 3 Saturdays in 5 to every Saturday. This needs their agreement.
- Saturday is 7 paid hours. Either extend it to 8 or accept a 31-hour week for those staff.
- Check that S05 having Friday off and S06 having Monday off fit their childcare arrangements.
- Cover Saturday absence like-for-like with a volunteer: S02 (KH), S09 (D), S07 or S11 (£138.60–£176.40 a shift).

## Measuring success

Set targets in advance and compare against January–March 2026:

- Showroom minimum cover met on all 71 open days; design appointments and sales
- Installs completed on schedule; call-backs
- Calls answered; complaints
- Overtime cost; sickness absence
- An HR-run survey of all 40 staff at the start and end, one response each, aiming for 80%+
- Applications per fitter and designer vacancy
