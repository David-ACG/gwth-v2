Last Saturday a man walked from Headingley. He got to the shop at 9.20am and the shelf was empty. I had nothing to give him. He just said, "Oh," and that was it. I went back to the oven and thought about it for the rest of the morning.

That's why, from Saturday 7 November, our Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Most Saturdays we sell out by 9.40am. On a quiet week we throw away about 6 loaves. Either you walk a long way for nothing, or I bake bread no one eats. Both are wrong, and one of them is on me.

I tried a few small things first. None of it shifted the basic problem. The oven holds 64 loaves, and that's the lot.

So here's the new way. Order on our website by 8pm Thursday. Pay when you order. Collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. We bake 64 loaves and not one more.

If you can't come, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After 8pm Thursday, no refund. By then the bread is in the mixer, baked for you and no one else.

Anything still on the shelf at 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Nothing wasted if we can help it. I'd rather it fed someone than sat there.

Weekday sales stay walk-in, same as now. Only Saturday changes.

The first collection is Saturday 7 November. The order page is on our website now. If you want a loaf, order by 8pm on Thursday 5 November.
