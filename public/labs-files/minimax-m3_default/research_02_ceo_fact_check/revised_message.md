# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Sent: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen from 14.2% to 7.4% in the latest year, close to halving. Our mean gap has fallen by a quarter, from 16.8% to 12.6%. We have published our gender pay gap figures for the last two years, in line with the legal requirement on employers of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, all union-graded colleagues (grades 1 to 6) received a 5.0% pay rise, in line with the offer accepted by union members in December. Awards for other grades were set separately by the company.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Our recruitment partner, who works with 14 food manufacturers in Yorkshire and the North East, says we have the lowest staff turnover of any client in their portfolio. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%; our overall sickness absence rate for the year, including long-term absence, was 4.2%, and we know there is more to do.

**Growing.** Hollinsford remains our largest site, and Thorncliffe is our fastest-growing, adding 59 colleagues over the year. Our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles in its first year, of which 58 were filled by the end of March; a second phase, dependent on a new customer contract, would take us towards the planned 120 over three years.

**Your voice.** Thank you to the 972 colleagues who filled in this year's PulsePoint survey – 79% of those invited. Our engagement score is 72%, up 4 points on last year and 6 points since 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. In our last onboarding survey, run in 2024, 87% of new starters rated their induction good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
