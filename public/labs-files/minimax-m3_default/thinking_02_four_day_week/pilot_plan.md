# Four-day week pilot: a plan for Nina

**Does your proposal as written work?**

Mostly, but Saturday does not.

- **Hours.** 40 staff × 32 hours = **1,280 hours a week**, down from **1,500** — a **14.67% cut** on the same pay.
- **Monday to Friday is fine.** Your split (S01–S06 Mon–Thu, S07–S12 Tue–Fri) gives 6 staff each weekday, above the 5-staff minimum, with key holders and designers covered.
- **Saturday is the gap.** With everyone on a 4-day Mon–Thu or Tue–Fri pattern, no one is contracted to work Saturdays, so the line in your proposal that "Saturdays carry on exactly as now" cannot work as written. Only four staff have volunteered for Saturday overtime (S02, S07, S09, S11). The most we can field on a Saturday is **4**, against a **minimum of 6** (1 key holder, 2 designers). Saturday is your busiest day, so this is the part to fix.

**Survey note.** "87% in favour" is 13 of 15 respondents. **15 of 40 staff responded (37.5%)** and 13 of 40 said yes (32.5%). Support is real, but the survey went out via the Wellbeing Champions WhatsApp group, so it over-represents engaged staff. Only 3 of 12 showroom staff responded — the team most affected.

**Your three realistic options**

1. **Run as written, accept 4-staff Saturdays.** Cheapest, but Saturday service runs at 67% of minimum for 12 weeks.
2. **Add a small Saturday pattern.** Put 2 staff on a Wed–Sat pattern (4 days, 8 hours, normal pay) — one key holder, one designer. Combined with the 4 overtime volunteers, Saturday cover reaches 6. This is the pilot I would run.
3. **Recruit more Saturday volunteers** before the pilot starts.

**The pilot: option 2**

Showroom pattern (4 days × 8 hours, normal pay):

| Pattern | Days | Staff |
|---|---|---|
| A | Mon–Thu | S01, S03, S04, S05, S07 |
| B | Tue–Fri | S02, S06, S09, S11, S12 |
| C | Wed–Sat | S08, S10 |

A and B follow your two-pattern logic, 5 staff each. Pattern C anchors Saturday with a key holder (S08) and a designer (S10) at normal pay.

**Cover each day (Mon–Fri min 5, Sat min 6):**

- Mon: 5 — 1 key holder, 2 designers ✓
- Tue–Thu: 10–12 ✓
- Fri: 7 — 2 key holders, 2 designers ✓
- Sat: 2 (C) + 4 overtime (S02, S07, S09, S11) = **6** — 2 key holders, 2 designers ✓

**Overtime cost.** Each Saturday costs **£617.40** (7 paid hours × 1.5× rate for 4 volunteers). Over 12 Saturdays: **£7,408.80** gross, per the handbook.

**Other teams.** Fitters, warehouse and office move to 32 hours over 4 days, same pay. Let each team pick its own A/B split to match demand — the survey preference is Friday (11) then Monday (4).

**Bank holiday.** Good Friday, **26 March 2027**, is in the pilot. Showroom closed, no cover needed.

**Measuring success** (vs the same 12 weeks a year earlier)

- Sales and orders by week, Saturday split out
- Showroom appointments: booked, kept, conversion rate
- Customer complaints and Saturday wait times
- Sickness days, voluntary turnover, time-to-fill
- Pulse survey at week 6 and week 12

Pass = no fall in sales/orders, no rise in complaints, Saturday appointments still hit, staff mood improves or holds.
