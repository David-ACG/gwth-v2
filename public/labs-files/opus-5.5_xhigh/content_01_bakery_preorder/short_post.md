A while back, 3 people told me they'd walked from Headingley and found an empty shelf. I still think about it. They'd got up early for bread, and we let them down.

We keep selling out by 9.40am on Saturdays. Saturday bread goes to whoever gets here first. If you've walked a long way, that means going home with nothing.

And on a quiet week, we throw away about 6 loaves. Too little some weeks, too much on others. Neither is good enough.

The oven doesn't get any bigger when I get up earlier. I've checked. We can bake 64 loaves on a Saturday, and that's the limit.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only. Weekday bread stays walk-in, same as now.

You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday, and you pay when you order. You collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. With orders in by Thursday, we bake for real people, not a guess.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. I'd rather an uncollected loaf gets eaten than binned.

If you can't make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday for a full refund. After that there's no refund, because your loaf is baked just for you. None of this changes your legal rights.

We can still only bake 64 loaves, so orders might run out. The difference is that you'll know before you set off.

We've never tried this before. Some of it might wobble on 7 November. If it does, I'll say so.

The first order deadline falls on Bonfire Night. I didn't think that through.

Put 8pm on Thursday 5 November in your diary.
