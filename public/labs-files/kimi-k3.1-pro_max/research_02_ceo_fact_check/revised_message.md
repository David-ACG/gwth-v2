# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has nearly halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires for a business of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, everyone at Hollins Brook received a pay rise: 5.0% for grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for grades 7 to 8 and 2.0% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. TalentHarvest says we have the lowest staff turnover in its food-manufacturing client portfolio. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1% in 2025/26; our all-absence rate was 4.2%, while the calendar-2025 sector average was 3.6%.

**Growing.** Hollinsford remains our largest site, and our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles in 2025/26, 58 of which were filled by 31 March 2026.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of those invited took part. Our engagement score rose by four points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. In our January–June 2024 onboarding survey, 87% of respondents rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
