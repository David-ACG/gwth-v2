# Board note: gender pay gap at 5 April 2026

## Publication duty

Thistlecombe Care **must publish** because it has **262 relevant employees** on the snapshot date. The 250-person threshold is an employee headcount, not an FTE measure; part-time staff and employees on leave count. People not employed on the snapshot and 14 agency workers are excluded. Hourly-pay figures use **248 full-pay relevant employees**, after 14 leave-related exclusions specified in the method note.

## Results and interpretation

- Men's mean hourly pay is **£20.07** and women's **£15.91**. Women's mean hourly pay is **20.8% lower** and median hourly pay **4.1% lower** than men's.
- The mean and median bonus gaps are both **77.5%**: women's mean and median bonus amounts are that much lower than men's among recipients. Bonuses are not hours-adjusted. **34.2% of men** (27/79) and **28.4% of women** (52/183) received a bonus.

| Pay quartile | Women | Men |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lower (1) | 79.0% | 21.0% |
| Lower middle (2) | 66.1% | 33.9% |
| Upper middle (3) | 72.6% | 27.4% |
| Upper (4) | 58.1% | 41.9% |

Each quartile has 62 employees; quartile 1 is lowest paid. Women form a majority in every quartile, but their concentration is highest in the lower quartile.

## Data treatment and limitation

Sex labels were standardised. Calculations use contracted hours effective on the snapshot, ignoring later changes. Ordinary pay includes only the specified April monthly elements, deducts salary sacrifice, and excludes overtime, mileage and bonuses. The bonus window uses pay dates from 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026 inclusive: 4 April 2025 payments are excluded, 2 April 2026 off-cycle payments included, and the December reversal netted off. A fully reversed bonus does not count as receipt.

**Ethnicity pay gap: not calculable.** None of the source files contains ethnicity data or categories, so no reliable figure or like-for-like comparison can be produced; the JSON value is `null`.
