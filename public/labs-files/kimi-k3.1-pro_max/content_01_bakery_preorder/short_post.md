We sold out by 9.40am on Saturday. That's good for a small bakery. It isn't good if you've walked from Headingley to find an empty shelf. Three people told me that's what happened. I felt awful.

So we're changing Saturday bread. From Saturday 7 November 2026, it will be pre-order only. Weekday sales will stay walk-in, just as they are now.

You'll need to order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. Payment is taken when you order. Collection is between 8am and 11am on Saturday. Any bread not collected by 11am will go to the Chapeltown food bank.

The reason is our 1 oven. I can bake a maximum of 64 loaves on a Saturday. More coffee does not increase that number. I've checked.

On busy mornings, people miss out. On a quiet week, we throw away about 6 loaves. Neither feels fair. Pre-ordering means we know how many loaves to bake before the oven goes on.

You can cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund if your plans change. After that, our pre-order terms don't offer a refund. That's because the bread is baked for you.

A friend said businesses with under 10 staff were exempt from the Consumer Rights Act. That isn't correct. The Act applies to small businesses too. Our pre-order terms do not remove your statutory rights.

We haven't tried this before. I don't know yet whether it will suit everyone. We'll learn from the first few Saturdays and make changes if needed.

Weekdays stay as they are. Saturday needs a little more planning. Order your Saturday bread on our website by 8pm on Thursday.
