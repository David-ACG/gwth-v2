# Four-day week pilot: assessment and options

## Does the proposal work as written?

No. Weekly contracted hours fall from **1,500 to 1,280 (-14.67%)** on unchanged pay; days lengthen from 7.5 to eight hours. Patterns A and B provide showroom cover of **6, 12, 12, 12, 6 and 0**, Monday to Saturday. Saturday therefore has a **six-person shortfall**, despite the proposal saying Saturdays continue as now.

Overtime does not rescue that version. Only S02, S07, S09 and S11 qualify: four people, not six, with one designer rather than two. Their gross cost is **£617.40 per Saturday**, or **£7,408.80 for 12 Saturdays**. Fitting volunteer F03 cannot count.

The survey’s 87% refers to respondents, not the company: **15/40 responded (37.5%)** and **13/40 answered Yes (32.5%)**. Only three showroom and three fitting staff responded. Consult all staff on contracts, longer days and individual arrangements.

## Realistic options

- **Revise the roster:** make Saturday one of four normal working days, as below. This preserves six-day opening within 32 hours without routine overtime.
- **Change the service:** retain Monday–Friday patterns but close or reduce Saturday opening/appointments. This protects the shorter week but risks the busiest appointment day.
- **Stage the trial:** test non-showroom teams first while retaining current showroom arrangements and negotiating a six-day solution. This is not a company-wide pilot.

## A workable 12-week showroom pilot

Run **4 January–28 March 2027**, with normal opening hours. Everyone works 32 hours over four days on the same pay; non-showroom teams use balanced A/B patterns subject to cover and consultation. The individual showroom patterns in `numbers.json` produce:

| Day | Staff on duty | Key holders | Designers | Minimum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mon | 9 | 2 | 2 | 5 / 1 / 1 |
| Tue | 9 | 2 | 3 | 5 / 1 / 1 |
| Wed | 8 | 2 | 2 | 5 / 1 / 1 |
| Thu | 9 | 2 | 3 | 5 / 1 / 1 |
| Fri | 7 | 2 | 2 | 5 / 1 / 1 |
| Sat | 6 | 2 | 4 | 6 / 1 / 2 |

Saturday’s team is S01, S02, S03, S04, S09 and S10, each working three weekdays plus Saturday. One paid admin hour after the seven-hour Saturday shift makes eight hours that day and 32 that week. S05 and S06 retain their no-Saturday arrangements. Confirm assignments through consultation.

Weekday surpluses allow some absence cover. Use voluntary day swaps to preserve 32 hours and reconfirm Saturday contingency cover weekly: it has no spare capacity. Overtime remains voluntary. If minima cannot be met, reduce appointments or close rather than use untrained staff or compulsory overtime. The showroom closes on **Good Friday, 26 March**; the final Saturday is 27 March.

## Measurement and review

Record the preceding 12 weeks’ baseline. Track weekly: opening and role-minimum compliance; actual hours, overtime and cost; appointments, conversion and sales per opening hour; fitting/delivery timeliness, rework and customer response times; sickness, resignations and wellbeing. Success means full cover, maintained or better productivity and service, no rise in compulsory/unpaid work, and improved wellbeing without worsening absence or retention. Note seasonality.

Hold fortnightly operational checks and all-staff pulses in weeks 6 and 12, reporting response rates. Review evidence and decide whether to retain, amend or stop the model on **Tuesday 30 March 2027**, after the pilot and Easter Monday.
