Pronunciation coach

Practise everyday Spanish and French phrases out loud, on your own, as often as you like.

How it works

  1. Choose a phrase from the list.
  2. Press Listen to the phrase to hear it said properly.
  3. Press Start recording, say the phrase, then press Stop recording.
  4. See how each word went, then try again as often as you like.

When you stop recording, your voice is sent to the company’s listening service so it can check which words it hears. This page does not keep your recording. Only your best score for each phrase is saved, on this device.
