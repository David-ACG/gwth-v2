# Four-day week pilot plan

The decision is yours; this sets out the facts and options.

## Does the proposal work as written?

**Not for the showroom.**

- **Saturdays have no cover.** Patterns A (Mon–Thu) and B (Tue–Fri) leave Saturday with 0 showroom staff against a minimum of 6. Weekdays are fine (Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6; minimum 5).
- **Overtime can't close the gap.** Four showroom staff have volunteered (the fifth is a fitter and can't cover). That gives 4 on a Saturday: 2 short, with one designer where two are required. It costs £617.40 a Saturday (£7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays), and overtime is voluntary.
- **"87% of staff" is 13 of the 15 who replied.** Only 15 of 40 replied (37.5%), so 13 yeses is 32.5% of all staff. It went round one WhatsApp group; the office answered 7 of 8, the showroom 3 of 12. Encouraging, not conclusive. And 11 of 15 want Friday off; A/B gives that to half.
- **Hours.** Same pay for 32 hours, not 37.5, cuts total weekly hours from 1,500 to 1,280 (−14.67%). Days lengthen from 7.5 to 8 hours, a worry one survey comment raised for fitters.

## Your options

1. **As written**, with or without overtime: fails Saturday cover.
2. **Four-day week for all, Saturday built into the showroom pattern** (pilot below): meets every minimum without overtime, but the Saturday team works every Saturday (12 in the pilot, about 7 in 12 weeks now).
3. **Everyone except the showroom** (28 staff): no Saturday problem, but leaves out the designers you want to recruit.
4. **Wait:** survey all 40 staff and set cover rules for every team first.

## Pilot (option 2): 4 January to 28 March 2027

12 weeks, 12 Saturdays. Rachel Byrne agrees it with each person, checking S05's and S06's childcare arrangements first.

- **Mon–Thu:** S02, S05, S06, S09, S11
- **Saturday team, Wed–Sat:** S01, S03, S04, S07, S12
- **Saturday team, Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat:** S08, S10 (no three-day break; ask for volunteers)

| Day | On duty (minimum) | Key holders | Designers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon, Tue | 7 (5) | S02, S08 | S09, S10 |
| Wed, Thu | 10 (5) | S01, S02 | S03, S04, S09 |
| Fri | 7 (5) | S01, S08 | S03, S04, S10 |
| Sat | 7 (6) | S01, S08 | S03, S04, S10 |

Every day has two key holders and at least two designers (three on Saturday, our busiest day), so one absence never breaks the minimum. Cover absences by swapping with a same-role colleague, then overtime from S02, S09 or S11 (£138.60 to £176.40 a shift).

**Other teams:** Pattern A or B as proposed, by preference. Team leads set minimum cover first (none exists), because Mondays and Fridays run at half strength.

**Good Friday (26 March)** is the only bank holiday. Anyone working Fridays loses a day that week; agree how it is treated.

## How we would know it worked

Agree pass marks first; baseline is the same 12 weeks of 2026.

- Showroom minimum met every open day; any breach triggers a review.
- Appointments kept, sales, fitting jobs and deliveries on time: each within 5% of baseline.
- Short survey of all 40 at weeks 0, 6 and 12; sickness; resignations.
- Applications per vacancy (12 weeks can't prove retention).
