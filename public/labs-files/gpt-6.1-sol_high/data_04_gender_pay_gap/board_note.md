# Thistlecombe Care: gender pay gap, 5 April 2026

**We must publish.** There were 262 relevant employees (79 men and 183 women) on the snapshot date. The provider’s method sets the threshold at 250 people, including part-time employees and employees on leave. Approximately 210 full-time equivalents does not remove the obligation. Agency workers, earlier leavers and later starters are excluded.

Hourly pay and quartiles use 248 full-pay relevant employees: 77 men and 171 women. Fourteen employees receiving reduced pay because of leave are excluded from these measures, but remain in the headcount and bonus calculations. Employees on full company sick pay remain included.

Mean hourly pay was **£19.37 for men and £15.50 for women**. The mean gender pay gap was **20.0%** and the median gap **5.0%**. Women’s mean hourly pay was therefore 20.0% below men’s; the median compares the middle hourly rate in each sex. These are workforce-wide comparisons and do not establish whether people receive equal pay for equal work.

Each quartile contains 62 people, ranked by unrounded hourly pay:

| Pay quartile | Women | Men |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lower | 77.4% | 22.6% |
| Lower middle | 71.0% | 29.0% |
| Upper middle | 74.2% | 25.8% |
| Upper | 53.2% | 46.8% |

The mean bonus gap was **78.1%** and the median bonus gap **73.5%**, comparing recipients’ total bonuses. Bonuses were received by **34.2% of men** (27 of 79) and **28.4% of women** (52 of 183). The period is 6 April 2025–5 April 2026 inclusive, determined by payment date. Bonuses are not adjusted for part-time hours.

We removed 15 exact duplicate payroll rows, standardised sex labels and matched payroll references to HR references. We used contracted hours effective on the snapshot date, ignoring later changes. Hourly pay uses the April monthly run and the specified ordinary-pay elements; negative pension sacrifice reduces pay. Overtime, expenses and bonuses are excluded. The bonus reversal cancels the erroneous payment; its recipient’s zero net bonus is excluded from recipients.

**Ethnicity pay gap: unavailable (`null`).** No ethnicity field exists in these files. Reliable employee ethnicity data and an agreed comparison method are needed; ethnicity cannot be inferred from names or other details.
