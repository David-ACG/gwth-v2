# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of the progress we have made together.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% between the April 2024 and April 2025 snapshots, and our mean gap fell by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap reports for the last two years, as required by law. At 31 March 2026, women held 41% of filled senior leadership posts, up from 35% a year earlier.

**Pay.** From 1 April, basic pay increased by 5% for grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for grades 7 to 8 and 2% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% in 2024/25 to 18.1% in 2025/26. All sickness absence was 4.2% in 2025/26, down from 4.5%, compared with the NFMA’s 3.6% sector average for 2025.

**Growing.** At 31 March 2026, Hollinsford was our largest site. Line 4 at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles in 2025/26, with 58 filled by 31 March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to the 972 people who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of those invited took part. Invitations went to employees and long-term agency workers. Our engagement score rose four percentage points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development in 2025/26. Our last induction survey, of January–June 2024 starters, found that 87% of respondents rated their induction as good or excellent, with a 38% response rate.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
