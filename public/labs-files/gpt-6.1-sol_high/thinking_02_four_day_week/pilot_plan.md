# Fernhill Kitchens: four-day week pilot for Nina's decision

**The proposal does not work as written.** Contracted hours fall from 1,500 to 1,280 weekly (14.67%), with unchanged pay. Showroom cover becomes Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6, Sat 0. Weekdays meet headcount and skill minima; Saturday is six short. Only S02, S07, S09 and S11 qualify and volunteer for showroom overtime: four staff, one key holder and only one designer, against Saturday's six staff/two designers requirement. Their seven-hour shifts cost £617.40 each Saturday, £7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays, without solving cover. Other teams' volunteers cannot count.

**Staff support is uncertain.** Fifteen responses represent 37.5% of staff; 13 yes answers represent 32.5% of all staff, or 86.7% of respondents. The optional WhatsApp survey was self-selected and office-heavy. Repeat confidentially with all 40 staff, reporting team response rates and constraints before agreeing patterns.

**Realistic options:** trial 32 hours at unchanged pay using staggered showroom days including Saturdays; phase the trial into teams whose cover is ready while retaining the showroom's current rota; compress 37.5 hours into four longer days (9 hours 22½ minutes paid daily, still needing staggered Saturday cover); or retain current arrangements. Keeping A/B for the showroom requires additional trained Saturday staff, including another designer; existing volunteers alone cannot deliver it. Recruitment or training would need a separate costed plan.
