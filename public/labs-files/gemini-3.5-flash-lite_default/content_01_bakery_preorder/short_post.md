We sold out by 9.40am last Saturday morning at Rise & Crumb. That is lovely for our books, and also a real problem for you. Three people told me they walked all the way from Headingley and found an empty shelf. You got up early for bread and we let you down. On a quiet week we also throw away about 6 loaves that nobody bought. 

I only have one oven and two hands. We can bake 64 loaves on a Saturday and not one more, however much coffee I drink. 

So we are changing how Saturday works. From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only. 

Here is how it works. You order on our website by 8pm Thursday and pay when you order. You collect your loaf between 8am and 11am on Saturday morning. If you do not collect by 11am, the loaf goes straight to the Chapeltown food bank so nothing is wasted. 

If you cannot make it, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that, no refund, because the bread is baked specifically for you. Under the Consumer Rights Act, refund rules for perishable goods apply to businesses of any size. 

Weekday sales stay walk-in as they are now. We still love a quick chat at the counter during the week when things are calmer. 

Head to our website and place your order by 8pm Thursday this week. Thank you for supporting us through every single change we make together.
