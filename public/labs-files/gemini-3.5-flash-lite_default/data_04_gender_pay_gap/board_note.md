# Gender Pay Gap Report: 5 April 2026 Snapshot

## Executive Summary & Publishing Requirement
As of the 5 April 2026 snapshot date, Thistlecombe Care Ltd has **262 relevant employees** (full-time and part-time staff). Because this exceeds the statutory threshold of 250 employees, **we must legally publish** our gender pay gap figures. While our full-time equivalent (FTE) staff count is approximately 210, UK regulations specify headcount (all individuals employed on the snapshot date), not FTE.

## Key Figures
- **Mean Gender Pay Gap:** 20.8% (men are paid higher on average).
- **Median Gender Pay Gap:** 4.1%.
- **Mean Hourly Pay:** Men £20.07 | Women £15.91.
- **Bonus Pay Gap:** Mean 77.5% | Median 77.5%.
- **Bonus Recipients:** 34.2% of men and 28.4% of women received a bonus.

## Pay Quartiles (Percentage of Women)
- **Lower Quartile (Q1):** 79.0% women
- **Lower Middle Quartile (Q2):** 66.1% women
- **Upper Middle Quartile (Q3):** 72.6% women
- **Upper Quartile (Q4):** 58.1% women

## Analysis & Context
Women make up the vast majority of our workforce across all quartiles (ranging from 58% to 79%). The mean pay gap (20.8%) is largely driven by a higher concentration of men in senior management and specialist clinical roles, whereas the median pay gap is much lower (4.1%). 

## Data Notes & Missing Information
- **Ethnicity Pay Gap:** Cannot be calculated from the provided HR and payroll exports (`inputs/`) because employee ethnicity is not recorded in our HR system.
- **Data Corrections:** Ordinary pay calculations correctly adjusted for pension salary sacrifice deductions and excluded employees on statutory maternity/sick leave or unpaid leave in April 2026, in accordance with the method note.
