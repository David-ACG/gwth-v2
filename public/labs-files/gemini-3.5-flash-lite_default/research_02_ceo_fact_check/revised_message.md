# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap report in line with statutory requirements. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a pay rise of 5.0%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. That is the lowest staff turnover of any client in TalentHarvest's food portfolio in the region. Our short-term sickness absence rate fell to 3.1%.

**Growing.** Hollinsford remains our largest site, and our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe has already created 64 new roles.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: nearly four in five of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72% (up six points since 2024).

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
