# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen from 14.2% to 7.4% in a year, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as employers with 250 or more relevant employees must do. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, grades 1 to 6 received a 5.0% pay rise, grades 7 to 8 received 3.5%, and grade 9 received 2.0%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. TalentHarvest reports that this is the lowest voluntary turnover among its 14 food-manufacturer clients. All sickness absence was 4.2%, compared with a sector average of 3.6%; our short-term absence rate was 3.1%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is now our second-largest site, with 371 employees; Hollinsford remains the largest, with 402. Our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe has created 64 permanent roles, with 58 filled and 6 being recruited.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 972 of 1,231 people invited took part, a response rate of 79%. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, and six points since 2024. Our survey package does not include an official external benchmark comparison.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. Our last onboarding survey, in 2024, found that 87% of 46 respondents rated their induction good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
