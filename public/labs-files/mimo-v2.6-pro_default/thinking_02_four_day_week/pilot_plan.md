# Fernhill Kitchens four-day week pilot

**Bottom line**

The proposal does **not work as written**. Its showroom patterns (S01-S06: Mon-Thu; S07-S12: Tue-Fri) give cover of Mon 6, Tue 12, Wed 12, Thu 12, Fri 6 and **Sat 0**. Saturday needs 6 showroom staff. The four Saturday-overtime volunteers (S02, S07, S09 and S11) could raise cover only to 4, leaving a shortfall of 2. Saying “Saturdays carry on exactly as now” is therefore incompatible with the four-day patterns. The survey is encouraging but not conclusive: 15/40 responded (37.5%); 13 “Yes” answers are 87% of respondents but only 32.5% of all staff.

**Realistic options**

1. **Run the proposal unchanged**, adding volunteers: not operationally viable. It still misses Saturday minimum cover; the four volunteer shifts cost £617.40 each, or £7,408.80 across 12 Saturdays.
2. **Keep four days but include Saturdays in the rota** (the viable pilot design below). This tests the hours and pay change without relying on routine overtime.
3. **Trial four days for Monday-Friday teams and retain the current showroom pattern**, or phase in a shorter-hours/five-day arrangement. This reduces disruption but tests less of the proposal.

**Pilot design**

Run for 12 weeks, Monday 4 January to Sunday 28 March 2027. Everyone works 32 hours over four 8-hour days at current pay. Non-showroom teams split between Mon-Thu and Tue-Fri patterns, with team leads protecting customer and fitting deadlines.

Showroom groups work these fixed days:

- **Group 1:** S01, S03, S07, S09 — Mon, Tue, Thu, Sat
- **Group 2:** S02, S04, S08, S10 — Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat
- **Group 3:** S05, S06, S11, S12 — Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri

This gives the same cover every open day:

| Day | Showroom staff | Minimum check |
|---|---:|---|
| Mon | 8 | 5; key holders S01/S02/S08; designers S03/S04/S09/S10 |
| Tue | 8 | 5; key holder S01; designers S03/S09 |
| Wed | 8 | 5; key holders S02/S08; designers S04/S10 |
| Thu | 8 | 5; key holder S01; designers S03/S09 |
| Fri | 8 | 5; key holders S02/S08; designers S04/S10 |
| Sat | 8 | 6; key holders S01/S02/S08; designers S03/S04/S09/S10 |

S05 and S06 retain no Saturday working. The volunteer list is the contingency pool: S02, S07 and S09 are already on Saturdays in this revised rota, while S11 is the reserve for absence or demand surges.

**Measures and review**

Take a four-week baseline before 4 January. Weekly, track cover breaches, appointments offered/kept and waiting time, design-to-order conversion, sales and contribution, order errors, complaints, overtime, absence, holiday use, fatigue and staff pulse. Compare output per paid hour and customer service with the baseline; flag any deterioration in fitting or delivery lead times. At the end of 12 weeks, review the operational and financial results with the staff forum and team leads. Nina's keep/change/stop decision is at the review meeting on **Monday 29 March 2027**.
