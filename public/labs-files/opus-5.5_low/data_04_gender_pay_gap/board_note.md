# Gender pay gap: 5 April 2026 snapshot

## Must we publish? Yes.
The legal test is a headcount of **relevant employees** on the snapshot date, not full-time equivalents. On 5 April 2026 we employed **262 people** (183 women, 79 men), so we are over the 250 threshold and must publish. Agency workers (14) are excluded, as their agencies count them.

## Our figures
| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Mean gender pay gap (hourly) | 20.0% |
| Median gender pay gap (hourly) | 5.0% |
| Mean hourly pay: men / women | £19.37 / £15.50 |
| Mean bonus gap | 78.1% |
| Median bonus gap | 73.5% |
| Men / women receiving a bonus | 34.2% / 28.4% |
| Women in pay quartiles, lowest to highest | 77.4%, 71.0%, 74.2%, 53.2% |

Hourly pay figures cover the 248 staff on full pay in April 2026. Fourteen people on maternity, sick or unpaid leave are excluded from them. They are still counted in the bonus figures.

**What this means.** The typical (median) woman earns 5% less per hour than the typical man. The average gap is much bigger, at 20%, because men are over-represented at the top. Women make up about 70% of our workforce but only 53% of the top pay quartile, and the five directors are all men. The bonus gap is large because men's bonuses are bigger on average, even though a similar share of men and women get one.

## Data corrections and limits
- **Sex** was recorded in eight different formats. We standardised these to women and men.
- **Duplicate payroll lines:** 15 lines appeared twice with the same line ID. (including three bonuses) We counted each line once.
- A **November bonus paid in error** and reversed in December was netted to zero.
- A long-service bonus run on 4 April 2025 falls before the bonus period (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026), so it was excluded. The run on 2 April 2026 was included.
- Hours changes agreed for May and June 2026 were ignored. We used the hours in force on 5 April.
- **Ethnicity pay gap: not calculated.** Our HR export holds no ethnicity data,. If the board wants this, we would first need to collect ethnicity data voluntarily.
