# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has almost halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We publish our gender pay gap every year, and we are open about what is behind it. Women now hold 41% of our filled senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, everyone in grades 1 to 6 received a 5% pay rise, as agreed with the union.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. That is below the median of 20.4% for food manufacturers in the North of England. Sickness absence fell from 4.5% to 4.2%, but that is still above the sector average of 3.6%, so there is more to do.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is our fastest-growing site, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new permanent jobs.

**Your voice.** Thank you to the 972 of you who filled in the PulsePoint survey: almost four in five of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
