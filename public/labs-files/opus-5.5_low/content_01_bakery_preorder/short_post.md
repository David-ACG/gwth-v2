On Saturday the shelf was empty by 9.40am. People had walked from as far as Headingley. They went home with nothing.

That keeps happening, and I've stopped pretending it's fine.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Here's how it works:

1. Order on our website by 8pm Thursday.
2. Pay when you order.
3. Collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday.

Weekday bread stays walk-in, just as now.

Why the change? I have one oven. It bakes 64 loaves on a Saturday and not one more, however much coffee I drink. Some weeks you get up early and miss out. On quiet weeks we throw away about 6 loaves. Neither feels right to me. Pre-orders mean every loaf is baked for someone who wants it, and you know it's there before you leave the house.

If your plans change, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that we can't refund, because your loaf has been baked for you. If something is actually wrong with your bread, that's different. Tell us and we'll sort it out.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Someone still gets to eat it.

We haven't done this before. Some of it will need fixing. The first few Saturdays might be a bit wobbly, mostly because of me and a new order list.

I'll be honest. I'll miss a bit of the early queue. I won't miss the faces at 9.45am.

If Saturday bread matters to you, set a reminder now for 8pm on Thursday 5 November.
