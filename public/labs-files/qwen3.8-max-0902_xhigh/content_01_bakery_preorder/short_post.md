We sold out by 9.40am on Saturday. 3 people told me they'd walked in from Headingley. The shelf was empty.

I felt awful. You got up early for bread and we let you down.

So we're changing how Saturday works.

From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

You order on our website by 8pm on the Thursday. You pay when you order. You collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday, and your name goes on the loaf.

Weekdays stay walk-in. Nothing changes there. Same bread, same recipe, same very early start.

Here's why. We have 1 oven and 2 hands. The Saturday limit is 64 loaves, however much coffee I drink. On a quiet week we still throw away about 6 loaves. That sits badly with me.

The people who miss out are the ones who travelled furthest. That's the part I can't live with. A pre-order means the shelf stops being a race.

You'll know you have bread before you leave the house. You get the loaf you asked for, and we bake what people actually want.

If we can't bake your loaf, you get your money back.

If you change your mind, cancel by 8pm Thursday and we refund you in full. After that there's no refund, because your loaf has been made for you.

Our rules sit on top of your rights, not instead of them. We're a small bakery and the Consumer Rights Act still applies to us.

Anything not collected by 11am, we plan to take to the Chapeltown food bank.

I haven't done this before. The first few Saturdays might wobble, and I'll say so here when they do.
