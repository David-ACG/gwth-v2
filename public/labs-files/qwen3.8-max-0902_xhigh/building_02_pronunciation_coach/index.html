How it works

  1. Pick a phrase from the list below.
  2. Press Hear the phrase to hear it said properly, in the right accent.
  3. Press Start recording, say the phrase out loud, then press Stop recording.
  4. Every word is coloured green, amber or red, you get a score out of 100, and you can see what the computer actually heard.
  5. Try as many times as you like. Your best score for each phrase is saved on this device.

    What the computer heard

    Your privacy, in plain English: when you press Stop recording, your recording goes only to Harbour & Vale's own listening service on this website (the address /api/listen) so it can be marked. It is not sent anywhere else, it is never saved on this device, and it is thrown away as soon as your score appears. The only thing kept is your best score for each phrase, inside this browser's own storage. No analytics, no advertising, no hidden uploads.

    Your browser will ask permission to use the microphone — that is normal and nothing is recorded until you press Start recording. Best scores belong to this browser on this device, so they will not follow you to another phone or laptop, and clearing your browser data will remove them.

    Works best in an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a phone or a laptop.
