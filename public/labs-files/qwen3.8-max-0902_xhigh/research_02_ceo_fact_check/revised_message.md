# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026
Status: revised after HR fact-check

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen from 14.2% to 7.4%, very nearly halved in a year, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter, from 16.8% to 12.6%. We published the report in March, as the law requires of an employer of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago. Two posts are empty while we recruit.

**Pay.** In April, grades 1 to 6 received a 5.0% rise, grades 7 and 8 received 3.5%, and the senior leadership team 2.0%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year, below the 20.4% median for Northern food and drink manufacturers. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%. Including long-term absence our rate was 4.2%, against a sector average of 3.6%, so there is more to do.

**Growing.** Hollinsford is still our largest site (402), and Thorncliffe grew fastest, up 59 to 371. Line 4 there has created 64 new permanent roles, 58 of them filled; a further 56 depend on a second customer contract.

**Your voice.** Thank you to the 972 of you who filled in the PulsePoint survey, a response rate of 79%. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, and six points since 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. Our last onboarding survey, of colleagues who joined in the first half of 2024, found 87% rated their induction as good or excellent. It has not run since, and we are looking at bringing it back.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
