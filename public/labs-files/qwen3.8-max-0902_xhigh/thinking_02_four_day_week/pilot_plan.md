# Four-day week pilot: facts and options

## Does your proposal work as written?

For the 28 staff outside the showroom, yes. For the showroom, no: one flaw is fatal.

**Saturday cover falls to zero.** Pattern A (Mon–Thu) and Pattern B (Tue–Fri) put nobody in on Saturday: 0 against a minimum of 6, including 1 key holder and 2 designers. Weekdays are fine (Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6; minimum 5), but Saturday is your busiest day (a third of design appointments), so "Saturdays carry on as now" cannot survive two fixed Mon–Fri patterns.

**Overtime cannot fill it.** Four showroom staff volunteered for Saturday overtime: S02, S07, S09, S11 (fitter F03 volunteered but is not showroom-trained). All four in gives 4 of the 6 needed, 1 key holder and 1 designer instead of 2, still below minimum. Gross cost: £617.40 per Saturday (7 paid hours at 1.5×), £7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays.

**The 87% will not survive scrutiny.** 15 of 40 answered (37.5%) via a self-selecting WhatsApp link; the 13 "Yes" votes are 32.5% of all staff. Only 3 of 12 showroom staff and 3 of 14 fitters responded, the teams carrying most risk. Treat support as unproven.

**Two staff are protected.** S05 and S06 have agreed flexible working and do not work Saturdays; no rota may schedule them.

Also: 37.5 to 32 hours cuts contracted hours 14.67% (1,500 to 1,280 a week) at unchanged pay, and 8-hour days are 30 minutes longer, the fitters' survey worry.

## Your options

- **A. As written.** Not viable: Saturday below minimum every week.
- **B. Overtime Saturday.** Not viable: £7,408.80, still 2 staff and 1 designer short.
- **C. Saturday-inclusive showroom rota** (below). Meets every minimum, no overtime.
- **D. Pilot the other 28 only,** showroom stays at 37.5. Simplest, but two-tier risk.
- **E. Re-run the survey properly first.** Costs the January start.

Only C and D work; the choice is yours.

## The pilot I would run (Option C)

Two showroom teams, with Saturday inside contracted hours.

- **Weekday team** (S01, S04, S05, S06, S08, S10): Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu.
- **Saturday team** (S02, S03, S07, S09, S11, S12): Fri and Sat, plus two staggered weekdays (S02 Mon/Tue, S03 Mon/Wed, S07 Mon/Thu, S09 Tue/Wed, S11 Tue/Thu, S12 Wed/Thu).

**Cover:** Mon 9, Tue 9, Wed 9, Thu 9, Fri 6, Sat 6: all at or above minimum, with a key holder and required designers each day. Other teams keep Patterns A and B (check Friday deliveries).

**Rules.** S05/S06 never on Saturday; same faces Friday and Saturday for appointment handover. Standby: S01 or S08 (key holder) and S04 or S10 (designer) on paid overtime. Saturday shifts are 7 paid hours, so that team works 31: decide whether to top up. Swaps allowed at week 6 (a designer-for-designer swap, S03/S10, keeps both teams compliant). Voluntary, pay unchanged, written opt-out.

## Measuring success

Set baselines in December. **Weekly:** Saturday cover met (6/1/2), rota compliance, sickness, overtime cost. **Monthly, against the same period last year:** appointments held and completed, Saturday revenue, quote turnaround, customer satisfaction, on-time fittings. **People:** applications, leavers, and a week-6 survey with a proper all-staff sample. Agree the pass mark first: no fall in Saturday appointments or revenue, no rise in sickness.

- Week-6 checkpoint: Fri 12 Feb 2027.
- Decision meeting: Thu 15 Apr 2027, 18 days after the end so the data is clean.
