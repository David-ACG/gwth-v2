# Board Note: Gender Pay Gap (5 April 2026 Snapshot)

## 1. Requirement to Publish
**We must publish (`must_publish: true`).** On 5 April 2026, Thistlecombe Care Ltd had **262 relevant employees** (183 women, 79 men), above the statutory 250 threshold.

Although contracted hours equal roughly **210 FTE** (209.5 FTE at 37.5 hours/week) and **248 full-pay relevant employees**, the legal threshold counts **headcount** on the snapshot date. Every part-time employee and anyone on leave counts as one person. Agency workers (14), pre-snapshot leavers (18), and post-snapshot joiners (5) are excluded.

## 2. Figures and What They Mean
* **Hourly pay (248 full-pay staff: 171 women, 77 men):** Mean hourly pay is **£19.37** for men and **£15.50** for women (**20.0% mean gap**). Median hourly pay is **£14.63** for men and **£13.91** for women (**5.0% median gap**). Fourteen relevant staff (12 women, 2 men) on statutory maternity pay, statutory/half sick pay, or unpaid leave in April 2026 are excluded from hourly pay figures.
* **Pay quartiles (62 staff per quartile):** Women make up **77.4%** of the lower quartile, **71.0%** of the lower middle, and **74.2%** of the upper middle, falling to **53.2%** in the upper quartile (men: 22.6%, 29.0%, 25.8%, 46.8%).
* **Bonuses (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026, all 262 relevant staff):** **34.2%** of men (27/79) and **28.4%** of women (52/183) received a bonus. The **mean bonus gap is 78.1%** (£3,857.41 vs £846.15) and the **median bonus gap is 73.5%** (£1,700.00 vs £450.00).
* **Meaning:** Equal pay within roles keeps the median hourly gap modest (5.0%). The wider mean hourly gap (20.0%) and bonus gaps reflect seniority: women hold most frontline care roles, while men hold all 5 Director posts and 6 of 8 Home Manager posts, where salaries and annual bonuses are highest.

## 3. Data Corrections and Missing Figures
* **Ethnicity pay gap (`null`):** Cannot be calculated because the HR and payroll exports contain no ethnicity data.
* **Data corrections:** Standardized eight `sex` text formats; deduplicated **15 repeated payroll lines** (identical `line_id`s: 12 in April 2026 ordinary pay, 3 in March 2026 bonuses); ignored 27 post-snapshot contract-hour changes; netted the December 2025 bonus reversal (`TC-0335`, `-£1,500`) against the November 2025 error payment; and excluded the 4 April 2025 off-cycle bonus run and leavers' bonuses.
