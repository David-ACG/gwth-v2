# Four-Day Week Pilot Plan

## 1. Does Your Proposal Work as Written?

**No — it leaves Saturdays uncovered and creates weekday single-point risks.**

- **Showroom Saturdays:** Putting `S01–S06` on `Mon–Thu` and `S07–S12` on `Tue–Fri` gives cover of **Mon: 6, Tue: 12, Wed: 12, Thu: 12, Fri: 6, Sat: 0**. Saturday (a third of design appointments) requires **≥6 staff (≥1 key holder, ≥2 designers)**—a **shortfall of 6**. Friday has only **1 key holder (`S08`)**, with no absence cover, while `Tue–Thu` has all 12 staff.
- **Overtime volunteers:** Five staff volunteered for Saturday overtime, but `F03` (Lewis Grant) is a fitter, not showroom-trained. Even if all **4 showroom volunteers (`S02, S07, S09, S11`)** worked every Saturday (39h/5 days weekly), cover reaches only **4 staff and 1 designer (`S09`)**—2 staff and 1 designer short—costing **£617.40/Saturday** (**£7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays**).
- **Hours, operations, and survey:** Moving 40 staff from 37.5 to 32h cuts weekly hours from **1,500 to 1,280 (-14.67%, -220h)** on full pay and lengthens shifts from 7.5 to 8h (flagged by fitters). Warehouse (`W01`) and Office (`O01`) each have **1 key holder**, and Fitting has **1 Electrician (`F08`)** and **1 Plumber (`F09`)**. Moreover, "87% support" came from **15 responses (37.5% response rate)**—only **32.5% of all 40 staff** voted "Yes" (skewed to Office 7/8 vs Showroom 3/12, Fitting 3/14, Warehouse 2/6).

## 2. Realistic Options (15 October Board Decision)

1. **Option A — 12-week 32h pilot (100% pay) with a 6-day Showroom rota:** Use `Mon–Thu` and `Tue–Fri` outside the showroom, and include Saturday as a contracted day across three 4-day Showroom patterns (**£0 routine overtime**).
2. **Option B — 9-day fortnight (~34–35h/week):** Halves the capacity cut (~7%) and keeps 7.5h shifts for fitters.
3. **Option C — Consult all staff first:** Re-survey all 40 staff and model fitting schedules before committing.

## 3. Recommended Pilot: 4 Jan – 27 Mar 2027

### Showroom Pattern (`S01–S12`, 4 days / 32h)
Protect weekday childcare arrangements for `S05` and `S06` across three 4-person patterns (Saturday is 7 paid hours vs 8h weekdays; Patterns 2–3 include 1h weekday prep/handover):
- **Pattern 1 (`Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu`):** `S01` (key holder), `S03` (designer), `S05, S06` (sales).
- **Pattern 2 (`Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat`):** `S02` (key holder), `S04` (designer), `S07, S11` (sales).
- **Pattern 3 (`Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat`):** `S08` (key holder), `S09, S10` (designers), `S12` (sales).

### Daily Cover
Every open day (`Mon–Sat`) has **8 showroom staff** (vs minimum 5 Mon–Fri, 6 Sat), absorbing leave without overtime:
- **Mon & Tue:** **8 staff** (`S01, S02` key holders; `S03, S04` designers; 4 sales).
- **Wed & Thu:** **8 staff** (`S01, S08` key holders; `S03, S09, S10` designers; 3 sales).
- **Fri & Sat:** **8 staff** (`S02, S08` key holders; `S04, S09, S10` designers; 3 sales).

**Other teams & bank holidays:** Split Office, Warehouse, and Fitting between `Mon–Thu` and `Tue–Fri` (1 Lead Fitter, 1 Apprentice, 1 Driver per pattern), add backup key holders for `W01` and `O01`, and schedule `F08` and `F09` on their working days. All teams close on **Good Friday (`2027-03-26`)**, with bank holiday hours pro-rated.

### Success Metrics & Review
- **KPIs (vs Q1 2026):** Weekly/Saturday design appointments and conversion; on-time fittings, snagging, and delivery punctuality; sickness, overtime (£0 routine), recruitment, and an **all-staff survey** (≥85% response).
- **Review:** Midpoint check **17 February 2027**. Pilot ends **27 March 2027** (`12` Saturdays). Since **29 March is Easter Monday** (closed), collate metrics **30 March** and hold the decision meeting **31 March 2027 (`2027-03-31`)**.
