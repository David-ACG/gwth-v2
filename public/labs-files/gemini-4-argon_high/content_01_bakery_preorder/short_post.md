We sold out by 9.40am on Saturday again. That's lovely, and also a problem. Soon after 9.40am, 3 people told me they'd walked from Headingley. They found an empty shelf and went home with nothing. You got up early for bread and we let you down. I felt awful for the rest of the morning.

On a quiet week, the opposite goes wrong. We throw away about 6 loaves at the end of a slow Saturday. Watching 6 good loaves go in the bin hurts. I only have 1 oven and 2 hands here in Leeds. Even when the oven hits 250 at 4.10am, 64 loaves is my limit. Drinking extra coffee at 6am hasn't improved my Saturday guesswork.

So we're changing how Saturdays work at Rise & Crumb. From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only. You choose your bread on our website by 8pm on Thursday. You pay when you order, and we bake your loaves for you.

On Saturday, you collect your order between 8am and 11am. You don't need to rush down the street before 9.40am anymore. Your bread will be waiting on the shelf with your name on it. Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Weekday sales stay walk-in, just as they are now.

Plans change sometimes. If you can't make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday for a full refund. After 8pm on Thursday, we can't give a refund. By then, the bread is already baked for you.

We haven't tried a pre-order system before. I'll still be in my coat checking the list at 8am on 7 November. Please bear with me while I learn the ropes on week 1. Order your Saturday loaf on our website by 8pm on Thursday 5 November.
