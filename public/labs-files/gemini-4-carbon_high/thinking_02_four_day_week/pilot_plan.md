# Four-Day Week Pilot Plan

## Does your proposal work as written?

**No, not as written**, for three reasons:

- **Zero Saturday showroom cover.** Putting `S01`–`S06` on Mon–Thu and `S07`–`S12` on Tue–Fri puts `6` staff on Monday and Friday, `12` midweek, and **`0` on Saturday**—a shortfall of **6** on your busiest day (a third of appointments; minimum 6 showroom staff, including 1 key holder and 2 designers). Friday also relies on one key holder (`S08`).
- **Overtime cannot fix Saturday.** Five staff volunteered for Saturday overtime, but `F03` is an untrained fitter. Even if all **4** showroom volunteers (`S02`, `S07`, `S09`, `S11`) worked every Saturday, cover reaches **4** (2 short) with **1** designer (`S09`, 1 short). Overtime is voluntary, keeps those four on five-day (39-hour) weeks, and costs **£617.40 per Saturday** (**£7,408.80** over 12 Saturdays).
- **Survey support is unproven.** Only **15 of 40 staff (37.5%)** replied, so 13 "Yes" votes equal **32.5% of all staff**, skewed to Office (7 of 8, versus 3 of 12 Showroom and 3 of 14 Fitting). Eleven wanted Fridays off, and a fitter questioned 8-hour days.

## Your realistic options

1. **Run the January pilot with a 6-day showroom rota.** Keep Mon–Thu and Tue–Fri outside the showroom, and use three 4-day showroom patterns across Mon–Sat. *Trade-offs:* £0 routine overtime and tests recruitment/retention across all 40 staff, but cuts weekly hours by **14.67%** (1,500 to 1,280), introduces 8-hour days, and schedules 8 showroom staff every Saturday.
2. **Use a smaller cut or weekend hires.** Trial a 9-day fortnight (protecting fitting hours and ~7.5-hour days) or recruit part-time Saturday cover. *Trade-offs:* Eases capacity and Saturday load, but softens recruitment appeal or raises payroll.
3. **Consult all 40 staff first (or keep five days).** Poll every team on shift lengths and Saturday rotas before deciding whether to pilot later in 2027 or retain 37.5 hours. *Trade-offs:* Avoids acting on a skewed sample, though delays or foregoes recruitment gains.

## A workable 12-week pilot (if you proceed)

- **Dates:** Monday **4 January** to Sunday **28 March 2027** (12 Saturdays; closed **Good Friday, 26 March 2027**).
- **Showroom pattern (32 hours, full pay):** Three four-person groups (adding 1 weekly prep/admin hour to Saturday's 7-hour shift):
  - **Group 1 (`Mon–Thu`):** `S02` (key holder), `S09` (designer), `S05`, `S06` (childcare kept).
  - **Group 2 (`Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat`):** `S08` (key holder), `S03` (designer), `S07`, `S11`.
  - **Group 3 (`Wed–Sat`):** `S01` (key holder), `S04`, `S10` (designers), `S12`.
- **Other teams:** Split Office, Warehouse, and Fitting across Mon–Thu and Tue–Fri; add deputy key holders for Office and Warehouse, pair apprentices with fitters, and schedule `F08` (electrician) and `F09` (plumber) phases on their shifts.
- **Daily showroom cover (£0 routine overtime; `S02`, `S09` emergency backup):**
  - **Mon & Tue:** 8 staff, 2 key holders (`S02`, `S08`), 2 designers (`S03`, `S09`) — min 5, 1, 1.
  - **Wed & Thu:** 8 staff, 2 key holders (`S01`, `S02`), 3 designers (`S04`, `S09`, `S10`) — min 5, 1, 1.
  - **Fri & Sat:** 8 staff, 2 key holders (`S01`, `S08`), 3 designers (`S03`, `S04`, `S10`) — Sat min 6, 1, 2.
- **Success measures (vs Q1 2026 and budget):** Appointments, conversion, sales; on-time fits, snagging, deliveries, complaints; margin, overtime/subcontractor spend; absence, applicants, and a Week 12 survey of all 40 staff (≥80% response).
- **Review:** Check at Week 6. After 28 March (**Monday 29 March is Easter Monday**, closed), keep pilot rotas provisionally while March closes and decide on **Thursday 8 April 2027**, when all patterns overlap.
