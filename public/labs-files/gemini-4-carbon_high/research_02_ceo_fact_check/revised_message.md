# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has nearly halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap reports for the last two years. As of April, women held nine of our 24 senior leadership posts — 37.5% overall, and 41% of the 22 filled roles — up from eight a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a 5% pay rise, with increases of 3.5% for managers in grades 7 and 8 and 2% for the senior leadership team.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. That puts us below the 20.4% sector median for food and drink manufacturers in the North of England. Short-term sickness absence fell from 3.4% to 3.1%, and overall sickness absence fell from 4.5% to 4.2%, though that remains above the 3.6% sector average.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe has grown to 371 colleagues — now our second-largest site behind Hollinsford — and the first phase of our Line 4 investment there has created 64 new permanent roles, with 58 filled by the end of March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of those invited — nearly four in five — took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, and is up six points since 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
