# Board Note: Gender Pay Gap (5 April 2026)

## Must We Publish?
**Yes.** Although our staff equate to roughly 210 full-time equivalents (209.5 FTE), the Chair's view does not apply: the statutory 250-employee threshold uses **headcount** on 5 April 2026, not FTE. Part-time staff and employees on maternity, sick, or unpaid leave each count as one person, while our 14 agency workers, 18 earlier leavers, and 5 mid-April starters are excluded. With **262 relevant employees** (183 women, 79 men), we must publish by 4 April 2027.

## Figures and Meaning
Excluding 14 staff on reduced or nil leave pay in April 2026 leaves **248 full-pay relevant employees** (171 women, 77 men; 69.0% women):

- **Hourly pay:** Men average **£19.37** vs **£15.50** for women (**20.0% mean gap**); median pay is **£14.63** vs **£13.91** (**5.0% median gap**).
- **Pay quartiles (62 each):** Women represent **77.4%** (lower), **71.0%** (lower middle), **74.2%** (upper middle), and **53.2%** (upper); men represent **22.6%**, **29.0%**, **25.8%**, and **46.8%**.
- **Bonuses (all 262 relevant staff):** **34.2%** of men (27 of 79) and **28.4%** of women (52 of 183) received bonuses, with a **78.1% mean bonus gap** (£3,857.41 vs £846.15) and **73.5% median bonus gap** (£1,700.00 vs £450.00).

Men and women in identical roles earn similar hourly rates. Both gaps reflect **seniority mix**: frontline care roles are mostly female, whereas **all 5 Directors** and **6 of 8 Home managers** are men receiving top salaries and executive annual bonuses (most female bonuses are £250–£500 retention, referral, or long-service awards).

## Data Corrections and Missing Data
- **Corrected:** Removed **15 duplicate payroll lines** (12 in April 2026 ordinary pay, 3 in March 2026 bonuses); standardised **8 sex label formats**; used contracted hours effective on/before 5 April 2026 (ignoring May/June 2026 changes); netted a reversed £1,500 error bonus to £0; and excluded bonuses outside 6 April 2025–5 April 2026 (including the 4 April 2025 off-cycle run) or paid to non-relevant staff.
- **Ethnicity pay gap (`null`):** Our files contain no ethnicity data, and inferring ethnicity from names is unreliable and inappropriate. HR recommends introducing voluntary self-reporting.
