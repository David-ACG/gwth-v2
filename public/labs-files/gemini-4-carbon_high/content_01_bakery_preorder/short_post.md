Not long ago, we sold out by 9.40am on a Saturday. That morning, 3 people told me they walked from Headingley to an empty shelf. I felt awful standing behind that bare counter. You walked a long way across Leeds for bread, and we let you down.

Quiet weeks go wrong in the other direction. On a slow week, we throw away about 6 unsold loaves. I only have 1 oven and 2 hands at Rise & Crumb. The oven hits 250 at 4.10am while I am still in my coat. However much coffee I drink, I can bake 64 Saturday loaves and not 1 more. Guessing Saturday crowds is clearly not my gift.

From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only. You order on our website by 8pm Thursday and pay when you order. Then you collect your bread between 8am and 11am on Saturday. We hold your loaf on our shelf until 11am. Turning up at 10.30am works just like turning up at 8.10am. You don't have to race a 9.40am sell-out anymore.

Plans change, and sometimes you can't make it. You can cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After 8pm Thursday, we don't refund cancelled or uncollected orders. By then, your bread is already being baked for you. Anything not collected by 11am on Saturday goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Good loaves feed neighbours instead of going in our bin.

Weekday sales stay walk-in as now, including Tuesday and Thursday rye. Only Saturday's 64 loaves are moving to pre-order. We haven't tried pre-orders at the bakery before.
