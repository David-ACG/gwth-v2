# Gender Pay Gap Report - 5 April 2026 Snapshot

## Statutory Publishing Obligation
Thistlecombe Care Ltd **must statutory publish** its gender pay gap figures. The chair's suggestion that publishing is unnecessary due to having ~210 Full-Time Equivalent (FTE) staff is incorrect under UK regulations. The statutory reporting threshold is based on total employee **headcount** on the snapshot date (5 April 2026), not FTE. With **262 relevant employees**, we exceed the 250 threshold.

## Key Pay Gap Figures
- **Headcount:** 262 relevant employees (183 women, 79 men); 248 full-pay relevant employees (171 women, 77 men).
- **Hourly Pay Gap:** 
  - **Mean:** **20.8%** (£20.07 men vs £15.91 women).
  - **Median:** **4.1%** (£14.68 men vs £14.08 women).
- **Bonus Pay Gap:**
  - **Mean Bonus Gap:** **77.5%** (£3,944.44 men vs £889.42 women).
  - **Median Bonus Gap:** **77.5%** (£2,000.00 men vs £450.00 women).
  - **Proportion Receiving Bonus:** **34.2%** of men vs **28.4%** of women.
- **Pay Quartiles (Women %):** Lower: **79.0%**, Lower Middle: **66.1%**, Upper Middle: **72.6%**, Upper: **58.1%**.

## Understanding the Results
While women represent 69.0% of full-pay staff, they comprise 79.0% of the lowest-paid quartile and only 58.1% of the highest-paid quartile. The lower median gap (4.1%) reflects similar pay rates across care staff, while the larger mean gap (20.8%) and substantial bonus gap (77.5%) stem from a higher concentration of men in senior/specialist roles eligible for larger annual bonuses.

## Data Adjustments and Limitations
1. **Ethnicity Pay Gap:** Cannot be calculated (**null**) because ethnicity is not recorded in our HR or payroll systems.
2. **Data Clean-up:** Standardised inconsistent sex labels (e.g., "female", "Woman", "male ") across records.
3. **Exclusions:** Excluded 14 agency workers (counted by agencies), 18 leavers prior to 5 April 2026, and 5 post-snapshot joiners. Excluded 14 employees receiving reduced leave pay (maternity, sick, unpaid leave) from hourly pay figures per guidance.
4. **Bonus Adjustments:** Accounted for a £1,500 bonus reversal in December 2025.
