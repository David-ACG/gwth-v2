Last Saturday at 9.40am, I turned the shop door sign to closed on Harrogate Road. The shelves were empty. I went back to sweep the floor and felt miserable.

A customer walked all the way from Headingley at 9.45am and found no sourdough left. I felt awful about that. That happens far too often on a busy weekend. On a quiet week, we throw away 6 loaves. That is a waste of good flour, gas and early morning effort.

We have 1 oven in the bakehouse. I can bake 64 loaves on a Saturday morning, and not 1 loaf more. My baking schedule starts at 4.10am. There is no time to bake extra rounds later in the day.

From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread will be pre-order only. You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday and pay when you order. You collect your loaf between 8am and 11am on Saturday. Weekday sales stay walk-in as they are now.

Any loaf left at 11am on Saturday goes to the Chapeltown food bank. A friend told me small shops with under 10 staff fall outside the Consumer Rights Act. So we can set our own refund policy. If you cancel by 8pm on Thursday, you get a full refund. After 8pm on Thursday, we give no refund. The bread is already baked specifically for your order.

We want this system to give everyone a fair chance at getting weekend sourdough. I know change takes time to get used to. We appreciate your patience while we try this out.

Visit our website today to set up your account before Thursday.
