# Four-Day Week Pilot Plan: Recommendation & Implementation

**To:** Nina Ashworth, Managing Director  
**From:** Operational Advisory  
**Date:** October 2026  

---

## 1. Evaluation of Proposal as Written

Your proposed 12-week pilot (4 January – 28 March 2027) reduces weekly contracted hours from 1,500 to 1,280 (a 14.67% reduction) on equal pay. 

However, **the proposal does not work as written**:

* **Showroom Saturday Deficit:** Fixed Patterns A (Mon–Thu) and B (Tue–Fri) leave Saturday with zero scheduled staff against a minimum requirement of 6 (shortfall of 6).
* **Unviable Overtime Reliance:** Only 4 showroom staff have volunteered for Saturday overtime (S02, S07, S09, S11). Even if all 4 work every Saturday, cover reaches 4 staff (still 2 short of the minimum). This overtime would cost £617.40 per Saturday (£7,408.80 across 12 weeks) without meeting operational requirements.
* **Survey Context:** While 87% of survey respondents favored a pilot, the survey response rate was only 37.5% (15/40 staff). Only 32.5% of total staff (13/40) explicitly voted "Yes", and only 3 of 12 showroom staff responded.

---

## 2. Realistic Options

1. **Option A: Staggered 6-Day Showroom Roster (Recommended)**  
   Maintain a 32-hour week across four 8-hour days, but stagger days off across Monday to Saturday for showroom staff. Non-showroom teams (Office, Fitting, Warehouse) remain on Patterns A and B (Mon–Thu / Tue–Fri).
2. **Option B: Voluntary Saturday Overtime + Rota Adjustments**  
   Assign 4 days Mon–Fri and rely on rotas plus temporary cover incentives. Unviable due to staff shortage and unnecessary overtime expense (£7.4k).
3. **Option C: Phased Implementation / Pilot Postponement**  
   Delay the pilot until full consultation is conducted with all 40 staff members to address Saturday cover and client appointment capacity.

---

## 3. Recommended Pilot Framework

### Showroom Working Pattern (S01–S12)
Each staff member works four 8-hour days per week:

* **S01 (Mgr / Keyholder):** Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat
* **S02 (Asst Mgr / Keyholder):** Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat
* **S03 (Designer):** Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu
* **S04 (Designer):** Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri
* **S05 (Sales / Childcare):** Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu
* **S06 (Sales / Childcare):** Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri
* **S07 (Sales):** Mon, Wed, Thu, Sat
* **S08 (Senior Sales / Keyholder):** Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri
* **S09 (Designer):** Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat
* **S10 (Designer):** Mon, Tue, Fri, Sat
* **S11 (Sales):** Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat
* **S12 (Sales):** Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri

### Daily Showroom Cover
* **Mon (8 staff):** 3 Keyholders, 2 Designers (Min: 5 staff, 1 KH, 1 Des)
* **Tue (8 staff):** 2 Keyholders, 3 Designers (Min: 5 staff, 1 KH, 1 Des)
* **Wed (9 staff):** 2 Keyholders, 3 Designers (Min: 5 staff, 1 KH, 1 Des)
* **Thu (9 staff):** 1 Keyholder, 3 Designers (Min: 5 staff, 1 KH, 1 Des)
* **Fri (8 staff):** 2 Keyholders, 3 Designers (Min: 5 staff, 1 KH, 1 Des)
* **Sat (6 staff):** 2 Keyholders (S01, S02), 2 Designers (S09, S10), 2 Sales (S07, S11) (Min: 6 staff, 1 KH, 2 Des)

## 4. Success Metrics & Review

### Key Performance Indicators
* **Customer & Revenue Metrics:** Completed design appointments and sales lead conversion rates (especially Saturdays).
* **Operational Delivery:** Fitting project completion dates and warehouse delivery turnaround.
* **Staff & Cost Metrics:** Absenteeism, turnover intent, and zero unbudgeted overtime expense.

### Review Schedule
* **Mid-Pilot Check-in:** Week 6 (12 February 2027) for operational tweaks.
* **Final Review Meeting:** **Monday 29 March 2027** (or Tuesday 30 March 2027 following the Easter bank holiday on 26–29 March) to evaluate metrics and decide on permanent adoption.
