# DRAFT: People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has nearly halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap report for the last two years. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, everyone in grades 1 to 6 received a pay rise of 5%, with management grades also receiving increases.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. That gives us the lowest staff turnover of any client in our recruitment partner portfolio. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is expanding rapidly, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new roles.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of you took part. Our engagement score jumped four points this year to 72% (up six points since 2024), a strong result for our sector.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year, and 87% of new starters rated their induction as good or excellent in our last survey.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
