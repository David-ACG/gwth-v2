# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% in 2024 to 7.4% in 2025, and our mean gap fell by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as required by law. At our March 2026 snapshot, women held 41% of filled senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, basic pay rose by 5% for grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for grades 7 to 8, and 2% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% in 2025/26. Sickness absence, including long-term absence, fell to 4.2% in 2025/26, above the sector's 3.6% average for calendar 2025.

**Growing.** At our March 2026 snapshot, Hollinsford was our largest site, and our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles in 2025/26, with 58 filled by 31 March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of the employees and long-term agency workers invited took part. Our engagement score rose four percentage points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year. In our 2024 induction survey, 87% of the 46 respondents rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
