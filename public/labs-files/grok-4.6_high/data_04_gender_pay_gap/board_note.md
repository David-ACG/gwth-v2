# Gender pay gap — 5 April 2026 snapshot

## Must we publish?

Yes. The legal test is 250 or more **relevant employees** on 5 April 2026, not full-time equivalents. That means everyone employed by Thistlecombe Care Ltd that day, including part-time staff and people on leave. Agency workers are not included: their agency reports them.

We had **262 relevant employees** (183 women, 79 men), so we must publish. The chair’s figure of about 210 FTE is a fair staffing measure (209.5 FTE at 37.5 hours) because many colleagues work part time. FTE is not the publishing test.

## Hourly pay

Fourteen people were paid less than usual in the April 2026 payroll because of statutory maternity pay, statutory sick pay, half company sick pay or unpaid leave. They stay in the headcount and bonus figures but are left out of hourly pay and quartiles, leaving **248 full-pay relevant employees**.

- Mean hourly pay: **£19.37 men**, **£15.50 women**
- Mean gender pay gap: **20.0%** (men’s mean is 20.0% higher)
- Median gender pay gap: **5.0%**

The mean is much larger than the median because highly paid roles sit mainly with men: all five directors, and six of eight home managers. Care assistants, our largest group, are mostly women. Quartiles (share of women, lowest-paid first): **77.4%, 71.0%, 74.2%, 53.2%**. Women are the majority in every band, but the top band is much more mixed.

## Bonus (6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026)

- Mean bonus gap: **78.1%**
- Median bonus gap: **73.5%**
- **34.2% of men** and **28.4% of women** received a bonus

Bonus gaps are wide because larger awards sit with senior roles. Bonuses are not scaled for part-time hours.

## Ethnicity pay gap

We cannot calculate one. The HR and payroll files do not record ethnicity, and it would be wrong to infer it from names or other fields.

## Data we corrected or could not use

Sex was stored in several labels (including “male” with a trailing space). All 262 people could be mapped to woman or man. Fifteen payroll lines were exact duplicates of the same line ID; we counted each once so a few hourly rates and three bonuses were not doubled. Contracted hours are those in force on 5 April 2026, not hours already agreed for later in 2026. A November bonus paid in error was reversed in December and nets to zero.
