# Four-day week: facts, options, and a 12-week pilot

**If you proceed:** Monday 4 January to Sunday 28 March 2027 (12 weeks). Closed Good Friday 26 March. **Review: Thursday 8 April 2027**, when you decide whether to keep it. 15 October is only whether to start.

## Does your proposal work as written?

**No. Weekdays yes; Saturdays no.**

Patterns A (S01–S06 Mon–Thu) and B (S07–S12 Tue–Fri), four contracted days only, no overtime: Mon 6, Tue 12, Wed 12, Thu 12, Fri 6, **Sat 0**. Minimum is 5 each weekday (with a key holder and a designer) and **6 on Saturday** (one key holder, two designers). Monday and Friday still clear that. **Saturday has nobody.** A third of design appointments are on Saturday.

“Saturdays carry on as now” conflicts with two weekday-only patterns. A fifth day is overtime, not a four-day week.

Overtime does not close the gap. Only four showroom staff have volunteered (Imran S02, Callum S07, Owen S09, Ben S11): four people, one key holder, one designer — still two people and one designer short. Non-showroom staff do not count. All four, every Saturday: **£617.40 a week, £7,408.80 over 12 Saturdays** (7 hours at 1.5×, gross only).

**Hours:** 1,500 a week now, 1,280 in the pilot — **14.67% fewer hours, same pay**.

**87% is of respondents, not of staff.** 15 of 40 replied (37.5%) to a three-day optional WhatsApp poll. Thirteen yes: **32.5% of all staff**. Showroom 3/12; fitting 3/14. Eleven of 15 want Friday off. Not a staff mandate.

## Options (you choose)

1. **Do not run it as written.** Saturday fails, or you close the busiest day.
2. **Four-day week with Saturday in the showroom rota.** Same 32 hours and pay. Office, fitting and warehouse on A/B. Six showroom staff have Saturday as one of four days. Keeps Saturday open without overtime.
3. **Four-day week off the shop floor only.** Fitting, warehouse and office trial it; showroom stays five-from-six. Unequal, and designers would not get it.
4. **Pause and survey all 40** (including Saturday) before you commit.

## A pilot you could run (option 2)
