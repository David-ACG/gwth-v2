# Four-Day Week Pilot Plan: Board Briefing

**To:** Nina Ashworth, Managing Director | **Date:** October 2026

---

### 1. Proposal Viability Assessment
Your proposal **does not work as written**:
- **Zero Saturday Cover:** Fixed Patterns A (Mon–Thu) and B (Tue–Fri) schedule zero showroom staff on Saturday.
- **Overtime Shortfall:** Overtime cannot bridge the gap. Only 4 showroom staff volunteered (S02, S07, S09, S11). Non-showroom staff cannot cover the showroom. At 4 staff, Saturday is 2 short of the 6-person minimum and has only 1 kitchen designer (minimum 2 required).
- **Cost and Burnout:** Using these 4 volunteers costs £617.40 weekly (£7,408.80 over 12 weeks) gross, requiring 39-hour weeks and defeating the 32-hour trial's purpose.
- **Weekday Inefficiency:** Showroom staffing peaks at 12 on Tue–Thu (vs 5 required), while Mon/Fri have 6 and Sat has 0.
- **Limited Survey Mandate:** The 87% support represents 13 of 15 respondents across 40 staff (32.5% of total workforce). Only 3 of 12 showroom staff and 3 of 14 fitters responded, citing concerns over Saturday cover and longer fitting days.

---

### 2. Realistic Options
1. **Split-Week Fixed Patterns (Recommended Pilot):** Assign showroom staff to Mon–Thu and Wed–Sat teams. Satisfies all cover rules, requires zero overtime, and provides 3 consecutive days off.
2. **Rotating Saturday Rota:** Rotate the 10 eligible showroom staff across Saturdays (~3 in 5 weeks), with S05 and S06 fixed Mon–Thu. Balances weekends, but adds administrative complexity.
3. **Weekend Hiring:** Recruit part-time weekend staff. Protects weekday patterns but adds payroll cost and cannot be hired by January 2027.
4. **Pause or Defer:** Consult showroom and fitting teams directly to resolve operational concerns before launching later in 2027.

---

### 3. Recommended Pilot Framework
All 40 staff move to 32 contracted hours over 4 days (8 hours/day, equal pay), reducing weekly company hours from 1,500 to 1,280 (-14.67%). Fitting, Warehouse, and Office split across Mon–Thu and Tue–Fri.

**Showroom Schedule:**
- **Team 1 (Mon–Thu):** S01, S02, S03, S04, S05, S06 (honours agreed childcare arrangements for S05 and S06).
- **Team 2 (Wed–Sat):** S07, S08, S09, S10, S11, S12 (all eligible for Saturday work).

**Daily Showroom Cover:**
- **Mon & Tue (6 on duty):** 2 key holders (S01, S02), 2 designers (S03, S04). *Exceeds min 5 staff, 1 key, 1 designer.*
- **Wed & Thu (12 on duty):** Full team on duty (3 keys, 4 designers). Facilitates training and handovers.
- **Fri (6 on duty):** 1 key holder (S08), 2 designers (S09, S10). *Exceeds min 5 staff, 1 key, 1 designer.*
- **Sat (6 on duty):** 1 key holder (S08), 2 designers (S09, S10). *Meets min 6 staff, 1 key, 2 designers.*
- **Sundays & Bank Holidays:** Closed (Good Friday, 26 March 2027).

---

### 4. Success Metrics & Review Timeline
**Measuring Success:**
- **Commercial:** Design appointments completed, sales conversion rate, weekly revenue vs prior year.
- **Operations:** Fitting first-time completion rate, snagging rates, customer review ratings.
- **Staff Wellbeing:** Sickness absence, staff turnover, pulse survey scores (weeks 4, 8, 12).
- **Financial:** Overtime spend (target: £0 showroom overtime).

**Review Timeline:**
- **Pilot Duration:** 12 weeks: Monday 4 January 2027 to Sunday 28 March 2027 (12 Saturdays).
- **Checkpoints:** End of Week 4 (29 January) and Week 8 (26 February) for operational reviews.
- **Board Review Meeting:** Thursday 15 April 2027 (`2027-04-15`) to decide whether to retain the four-day week.
