Last Saturday at 9.40am, the last loaf left the wooden cooling rack.

A customer walked 2 miles from Headingley and found an empty shelf. I had to tell her there was no bread left. She walked home with an empty tote bag, and I felt rotten. You make the effort to visit, and we let you down.

Right now, Saturdays are unpredictable. When the sun shines, our shelves clear out in 90 minutes. When it rains, we end up throwing away about 6 loaves. I hate seeing good bread in the bin.

I only have 1 oven and 2 hands. That means 64 loaves is the absolute limit for us on a Saturday. The oven holds what it holds, however much coffee I drink.

So from Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Weekday sales stay walk-in as now. You can pop in on Wednesday at 8.30am for a loaf without ordering.

Here is how Saturday orders will work. You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday and pay then. You collect your loaf from the counter between 8am and 11am on Saturday.

If you cannot make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday for a full refund. After 8pm on Thursday, we do not offer refunds. The dough is already mixed by then, and that loaf is baked for you.

Anything not collected by 11am on Saturday goes straight to the Chapeltown food bank. Volunteers pick it up in crates at 11.15am, so no loaf goes to waste.

I know changing a Saturday routine can be annoying. But this way, you do not have to sprint across Leeds before 9.30am. Your loaf will be waiting on the counter.

Visit our website to set up your pre-order account before Thursday.
