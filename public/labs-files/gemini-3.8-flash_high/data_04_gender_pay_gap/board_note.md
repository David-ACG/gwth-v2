# Board Briefing: Gender Pay Gap Reporting (5 April 2026 Snapshot)

### Statutory Publication Requirement
Thistlecombe Care Ltd **must publish** its gender pay gap figures. Under the Equality Act 2010 (Gender Pay Gap Information) Regulations 2017, the 250-employee threshold is determined strictly by employee **headcount**, not full-time equivalent (FTE) staffing. 

While our contracted hours equate to approximately 209.5 FTE, our actual headcount on 5 April 2026 was **262 relevant employees** (excluding agency staff). Because this headcount exceeds 250, reporting is mandatory.

### Headline Figures and Meaning
Calculations are based on 248 full-pay relevant employees (171 women, 77 men), excluding 14 staff on reduced pay due to statutory maternity, sick leave, or unpaid leave.

- **Hourly Pay Gap:** The **mean gap is 20.0%** (men £19.37, women £15.50), and the **median gap is 5.0%** (men £14.63, women £13.91). 
- **What this means:** The modest median gap (5.0%) reflects comparable hourly pay across frontline care roles. The higher mean gap (20.0%) is driven by a concentration of men in senior managerial roles.
- **Pay Quartiles:** Women make up 69.0% of full-pay relevant employees and over 70% of the lower three quartiles (Lower: 77.4%, Lower Middle: 71.0%, Upper Middle: 74.2%). However, female representation falls to 53.2% in the Upper Quartile, where men occupy 46.8% of positions despite representing only 31.0% of staff.
- **Bonus Pay Gap:** 34.2% of men and 28.4% of women received a bonus in the 12 months ending 5 April 2026. The **mean bonus gap is 78.1%** (men £3,857.41, women £846.15) and the **median bonus gap is 73.5%** (men £1,700.00, women £450.00), reflecting larger performance bonuses paid to executive and management posts.

### Data Corrections and Limitations
- **Duplicate Payroll Records:** 15 identical duplicate rows in the payroll export (12 in April 2026 ordinary pay, 3 in March 2026 bonuses) were removed to prevent artificial earnings duplication.
- **Bonus Reversal:** A £1,500 bonus paid in error in November 2025 and reversed in December 2025 was netted to zero, correctly excluding the employee from recipient counts.
- **Ethnicity Pay Gap:** Reported as **null**. Ethnicity is not recorded in HR or payroll records, preventing reliable calculation. We recommend introducing voluntary monitoring.
