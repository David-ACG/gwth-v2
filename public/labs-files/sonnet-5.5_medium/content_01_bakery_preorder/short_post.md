Some Saturdays, someone walks a long way to the bakery and goes home with nothing. I hate that.

The shelf keeps emptying by 9.40am. On a quiet week, we have the opposite problem. We throw away about 6 loaves. Neither one is fair to you, and neither one is good for the bread.

So from Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Here is how it works.

You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. You pay when you order. Then you collect your loaf on Saturday between 8am and 11am.

Weekday sales stay walk-in, exactly as now.

The reason is simple. I have 1 oven and 2 hands, and neither is getting bigger. The oven makes 64 loaves on a Saturday at most. If I know what you've asked for, I can bake that, and you know your loaf is set aside before you leave the house.

Two rules you should know now.

If you can't make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday and you get a full refund. After that, there is no refund, because the bread is baked for you.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank.

I'll be honest. We haven't done this before. It may be clumsy for the first few Saturdays. I'll probably get something wrong, and I'll tell you when I do.

I also know it's less casual than wandering in on a Saturday morning. That's a real cost, and I'm asking you to put up with it. I think it's better than a long walk and an empty shelf.

If something about the new system doesn't work for you, reply and tell me. I read every message.

Order your Saturday loaf on our website by 8pm on Thursday 5 November.
