# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has nearly halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires of a business of our size. Women now hold 41% of our filled senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, everyone in grades 1 to 6 received a 5% pay rise, managers in grades 7 and 8 received 3.5%, and our senior leadership team received 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Sickness absence fell from 4.5% to 4.2%, though that is still above the sector average of 3.6%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is our fastest-growing site, with 59 more colleagues than a year ago, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new roles, 58 of them filled.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: nearly four in five of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
