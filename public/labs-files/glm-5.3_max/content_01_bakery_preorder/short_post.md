At 9.40am last Saturday, the bread shelf was empty.

Three people told me they had walked from Headingley. You got up early, came to Rise & Crumb, and found nothing. I hated that.

So we are changing how Saturday bread works. From Saturday 7 November, it will be pre-order only.

Here is the plan.

You order on our website by 8pm Thursday. You pay when you order. Then you collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday.

If you cannot make it, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that, our policy is no refund. The bread will have been baked for you.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. I would rather it feed someone than sit here looking reproachful.

This is not a way to sell more bread. It is a way to stop the same problem repeating every week.

We have one oven. On a Saturday, I can bake 64 loaves and not one more. Last week, they had all gone by 9.40am. On a quiet week, we still throw away about 6 loaves. That is too much at both ends.

Pre-orders will tell me what to bake. They should mean fewer people leave with nothing, and fewer loaves go to waste.

Weekday sales stay walk-in as now. If you come on Tuesday or Thursday, nothing changes for you.

I know this adds a step. I have tried to keep it simple: order by 8pm Thursday, pay then, collect by 11am Saturday.

Order your Saturday bread on the website by 8pm Thursday.
