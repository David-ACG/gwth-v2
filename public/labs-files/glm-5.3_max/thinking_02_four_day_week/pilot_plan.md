# Fernhill Kitchens: Four-Day Week Pilot Plan

**Prepared for:** Nina Ashworth, Managing Director  
**Pilot:** 12 weeks, Monday 4 January to Sunday 28 March 2027.

## Does the proposal work as written?

No. The proposed showroom patterns give Monday 6, Tuesday to Thursday 12, Friday 6, and Saturday 0 staff. Saturday needs 6, so the shortfall is 6. Only four showroom staff have volunteered for Saturday overtime, giving a maximum of 4 and leaving a shortfall of 2. Using every volunteer every Saturday would cost £617.40 per Saturday, or £7,408.80 across 12 Saturdays, and still not meet minimum cover.

The survey is also weaker evidence than the headline suggests: 15 of 40 staff responded (37.5%), and 13 of all 40 staff (32.5%) said yes. Only 3 of 12 showroom staff responded, so more consultation is needed before treating it as a mandate.

## Realistic options

1. **Run a revised four-day pilot:** build Saturday into six showroom working patterns. This can meet every minimum without overtime and is described below.
2. **Run a partial pilot:** apply four-day patterns to fitting, warehouse, and office first, while the showroom retains its current rota. This is lower-risk for cover but creates fairness and communication issues.
3. **Pause and consult:** gather October–December baseline data and run a better-attended survey, especially with showroom staff. This delays learning but improves consent.

## Recommended pilot design

Run for 12 weeks from Monday 4 January 2027. Everyone contracts 32 hours over four days at current pay. Non-showroom staff use Monday–Thursday or Tuesday–Friday. Showroom staff use the following patterns:

Contracted hours fall from 1,500 to 1,280 a week (14.67%). The pilot contains 12 Saturdays and no England bank holidays.

| Staff | Working days |
|---|---|
| S01 | Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat |
| S02, S09, S11 | Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat |
| S04 | Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat |
| S07 | Mon, Wed, Thu, Sat |
| S03 | Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu |
| S05, S12 | Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri |
| S06 | Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri |
| S08 | Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri |
| S10 | Mon, Tue, Wed, Fri |

Weekday showroom shifts provide 8 paid hours. A Saturday shift provides 7 paid hours, so staff whose pattern includes Saturday also complete one flexible weekday preparation/administration hour to reach 32 hours.

| Day | Cover | Key holder | Kitchen designers |

The Saturday cohort contains only existing Saturday workers and includes all four volunteers. Keep patterns fixed for the pilot so results are comparable; discuss rotation or Saturday recognition before any permanent rollout.

## Success measures and review

Collect October–December 2026 as baseline. Track weekly: cover compliance, unplanned closures, Saturday appointments kept, design-to-quote time, quotes issued, sales conversion, installations completed, revenue per labour hour, overtime, absence, and a short wellbeing pulse. Hold a midpoint check after week six on Monday 15 February 2027. Require no missed minimum cover, no reliance on overtime, and service/productivity at least matching baseline before considering continuation.

Hold the decision meeting on Wednesday 31 March 2027, after the pilot ends on Sunday 28 March 2027.
