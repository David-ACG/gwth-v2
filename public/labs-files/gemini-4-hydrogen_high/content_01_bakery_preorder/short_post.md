We sold out by 9.40am on Saturday. 3 people told me they'd walked from Headingley and found an empty shelf. You got up early for bread, and we let you down. Nobody should walk across Leeds for nothing.

At Rise & Crumb, I only have 1 oven and 2 hands. I can bake 64 loaves on a Saturday and not 1 more, however much coffee I drink. Yet on a quiet week, we still throw away about 6 loaves. Guessing on a Thursday night is failing both ways.

From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only. Weekday sales stay walk-in, just as they are now. If you drop by during the week, nothing changes at our counter.

Here is how Saturday works. You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday and pay when you order. That gives me a real count before I light the oven at 4.10am. You collect your bread between 8am and 11am on Saturday. Anything not collected by 11am goes straight to the Chapeltown food bank. Spare loaves help someone nearby instead of going in the bin.

Plans change, and I know mornings get messy. If you can't make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday for a full refund. After 8pm on Thursday, there is no refund. Your bread is already baked for you.

We haven't tried pre-orders before. Knowing me, I'll mix up a ticket or misplace a tray on week 1. I've got things wrong before, like the Tuesday rye that came out too dark. We'll watch how the early Saturdays go and fix what isn't working.
