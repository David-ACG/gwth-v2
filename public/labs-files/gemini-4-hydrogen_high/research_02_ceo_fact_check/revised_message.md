# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap nearly halved in a year, falling from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap report for the last two years, as required for an employer of our size. In our spring snapshot, women held 9 of our 22 filled senior leadership roles (with two of our 24 established posts vacant), up from 8 of 23 a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, colleagues in grades 1 to 6 received a 5.0% pay rise, with 3.5% for managers in grades 7 to 8 and 2.0% for our senior leadership team.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%, and overall sickness absence fell to 4.2%, compared with a sector average of 3.6%.

**Growing.** Hollinsford remains our largest site, while Thorncliffe is growing: our Line 4 investment there created 64 new permanent roles in 2025/26, with 58 filled at the end of March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of invited colleagues took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72% (up six points since 2024).

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
