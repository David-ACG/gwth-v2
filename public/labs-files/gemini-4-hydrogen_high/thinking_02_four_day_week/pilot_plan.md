# Four-Day Week Pilot Plan — Briefing for Nina Ashworth

## Does Your Proposal Work as Written?

**No — it leaves Saturdays unstaffed and carries operational risks.**

- **Saturday showroom collapse:** Putting S01–S06 on Mon–Thu and S07–S12 on Tue–Fri puts 12 staff in on Tue–Thu (minimum 5), 6 on Mon and Fri, and **0 on Saturday** (shortfall of **6** on the day driving ~1/3 of design appointments).
- **Overtime cannot bridge the gap:** Only **4** showroom staff volunteered for Saturday overtime (S02, S07, S09, S11; F03 is a fitter and cannot cover the showroom). Calling in all four gives **4 staff** (2 short of 6) and **1 kitchen designer** (short of 2), costing **£617.40/Saturday** (**£7,408.80** gross across 12 Saturdays).
- **Survey mandate is overstated:** The 87% figure is 13 of 15 responses — only **32.5%** of all 40 staff (**37.5%** response rate), skewed toward Office (7/8) over Showroom (3/12) and Fitting (3/14).
- **Other bottlenecks:** Contracted hours fall **14.67%** (1,500 to 1,280/week). Office (O01) and Warehouse (W01) each have one key holder, leaving Mon or Fri uncovered unless backups are trained. Good Friday (**2027-03-26**) falls in Week 12.

## Your Realistic Options

1. **Run a 12-week, 32-hour pilot using the 3-pattern showroom rota below:** Covers Saturdays in core hours with £0 overtime; splits Office, Warehouse, and Fitting across Mon–Thu and Tue–Fri once backup key holders are trained.
2. **Stagger or adapt non-showroom hours:** Trial 32 hours in Office/Showroom while keeping Fitting/Warehouse on a 9-day fortnight (~34–35h) or 5-day rota until lead-time impact is proven — lower capacity risk, higher perceived unfairness.
3. **Defer and re-survey first:** Run a mandatory whole-staff survey (testing Saturday patterns and fitter preferences) before committing to a January start.

## Pilot Design (12 Weeks: Mon 4 Jan – Sun 28 Mar 2027)

### Showroom Pattern & Daily Cover

Split the 12 showroom staff into three 4-person groups (preserving S05 and S06's non-Saturday childcare agreements):

- **Group A (Mon–Thu):** S01, S03, S05, S06
- **Group C (Wed–Sat):** S08, S09, S10, S12
- **Group D (Mon–Tue, Fri–Sat):** S02, S04, S07, S11

| Day | Staff (Min) | Key Holders (Min) | Designers (Min) | Groups |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon, Tue | 8 (5) | 2 (1) | 2 (1) | A + D |
| Wed, Thu | 8 (5) | 2 (1) | 3 (1) | A + C |
| Fri | 8 (5) | 2 (1) | 3 (1) | C + D |
| Sat | 8 (6) | 2 (1) | 3 (2) | C + D |

Every day exceeds minimum cover by 2–3 staff and absorbs single absences without overtime. *(Saturday shifts are 7 paid hours vs 8 on weekdays; either accept 31h on full pay or add 1h midweek. You can also swap S01/S03 with Saturday-eligible staff mid-pilot for fairness.)* Train one backup key holder each in Office and Warehouse before 4 January.

### Measuring Success (vs Q1/Q4 2026 Baselines)

- **Showroom:** Weekly design appointments, conversion rate, and Saturday order value.
- **Operations:** On-time fitting completions, installation lead times, rework/snags, and overtime spend.
- **People:** Sickness absence, recruitment applications, and an end-of-pilot survey (>80% response target).
