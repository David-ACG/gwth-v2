# Gender Pay Gap Report — 5 April 2026 Snapshot

## Must we publish?
**Yes.** We must publish our statutory gender pay gap figures. On 5 April 2026, Thistlecombe Care Ltd employed **262 relevant employees** (183 women, 79 men), exceeding the 250-employee statutory threshold. The ~210 figure cited previously reflects full-time equivalent hours (7,857 contracted weekly hours ÷ 37.5 = 209.5 FTE), not headcount. Under the regulations, part-time staff and employees on leave count as one employee each; only our 14 agency workers, 18 pre-snapshot leavers, and 5 post-snapshot joiners are excluded.

## Key figures and what they mean
After excluding 14 relevant employees on reduced pay due to maternity, sick, or unpaid leave in April 2026, **248 full-pay relevant employees** (171 women, 77 men) are included in the hourly pay calculations:

- **Hourly pay gap:** Mean hourly pay is **£19.37** for men and **£15.50** for women, giving a **20.0% mean gap**. The **median gap is 5.0%** (men £14.63/hr vs women £13.91/hr).
- **Pay quartiles:** Women make up 69.8% of our relevant workforce overall, and **77.4%** of the lower quartile, **71.0%** of the lower middle, **74.2%** of the upper middle, and **53.2%** of the upper quartile.
- **Bonus pay:** In the 12 months to 5 April 2026, **34.2%** of men and **28.4%** of women received a bonus, with a **78.1% mean bonus gap** and a **73.5% median bonus gap**.

The large gap between the mean (20.0%) and median (5.0%)—and the steep bonus gap—is driven by senior representation rather than unequal pay for equal work. All 5 Directors and 6 of 8 Home Managers are men, while Care Assistants (83% women) and Domestic Assistants (60% women) are concentrated in the lower pay quartiles and receive smaller retention, referral, or long-service bonuses rather than large annual bonuses.

## Data corrections and missing information
- **Duplicate payroll lines:** Removed 15 exact duplicate export rows (same `line_id`: 12 in April 2026 ordinary pay and 3 in March 2026 bonuses).
- **Formatting and cut-offs:** Standardised inconsistent `sex` labels, used contracted hours effective on or before 5 April 2026 (ignoring 26 later changes), excluded the 4 April 2025 off-cycle bonus run, included the 2 April 2026 off-cycle run, and netted out one £1,500 December 2025 bonus reversal.
- **Ethnicity pay gap (`null`):** Could not be calculated because the HR and payroll exports contain no ethnicity data.
