# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has almost halved in a year, from 14.2% to 7.4%, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We publish our pay gap report every year, as the law requires of employers of our size, and you can read it on our website. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, everyone at Hollins Brook received a pay rise: 5% for colleagues in grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for our managers, and 2% for the senior leadership team, including me.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year, well below the industry median of 20.4% for food manufacturers in the North of England. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1%, from 3.4% the year before.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe grew by 59 people last year and is now our second-largest site, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new jobs, 58 of them filled.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: nearly four in five of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%, up six points on 2024.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year, and in our last onboarding survey 87% of new starters rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
