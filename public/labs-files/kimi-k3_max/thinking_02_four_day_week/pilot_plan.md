# Four-day week pilot: plan for Fernhill Kitchens

Prepared for Nina Ashworth, 21 October 2026. Figures in `out/numbers.json`.

## Does your proposal work as written?

No — one gap breaks it. Patterns A (S01–S06, Mon–Thu) and B (S07–S12, Tue–Fri) put **nobody** in the showroom on Saturdays, which needs 6 staff including a key holder and 2 designers. "Saturdays carry on exactly as now" cannot hold alongside fixed Mon–Fri patterns. Weekday cover would be fine (6–12 against a minimum of 5), and office, fitting and warehouse teams work well on A/B.

Saturday overtime does not rescue it: only four showroom staff have volunteered (S02, S07, S09, S11), so even with all four every Saturday you get 4 people and 1 designer — below the 6-and-2 minimum — at £617.40 per Saturday (£7,408.80 over the pilot), relying on the same four people weekly.

Treat "87% support" carefully: only 15 of 40 staff responded (37.5%), so the 13 yeses are 32.5% of all staff — and only 3 of 12 showroom staff, the team most affected, answered. Re-survey everyone before committing.

## Your options

1. **As written, plus Saturday overtime.** Fails the Saturday minimum even with every volunteer. Not workable.
2. **Showroom rota (recommended below).** Showroom staff each work 4 of Mon–Sat within 32 hours; a six-strong Saturday crew covers Saturdays at no overtime cost. Other teams keep A/B.
3. **Non-showroom first.** Run A/B for the 28 office/fitting/warehouse staff from January; showroom joins from April once its rota beds in. Lower risk, slower learning.
4. **Different model.** Five shorter days or a four-and-a-half-day week keeps Saturdays simple but dilutes the recruitment benefit.

## Recommended pilot (option 2)

**When:** Monday 4 January – Saturday 27 March 2027 (12 weeks, 12 Saturdays); closed Good Friday 26 March as normal.

**Hours and pay:** 32 hours (4 × 8-hour days), same pay. Days lengthen slightly (8 vs 7.5 hours) — watch this for fitters, as one survey comment flagged.

**Showroom pattern** (S05 and S06 keep their agreed no-Saturday arrangements):

- S01 Wed–Sat · S02 Mon–Thu · S03 Mon/Tue/Fri/Sat · S04 Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri
- S05 Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri · S06 Tue–Fri · S07 Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat · S08 Tue–Fri
- S09 Mon/Tue/Wed/Sat · S10 Mon/Wed/Thu/Fri · S11 Tue/Thu/Fri/Sat · S12 Mon/Tue/Thu/Sat

The Saturday crew (S01, S03, S07, S09, S11, S12 — covering the key holder and both designers, and including three of the four overtime volunteers) takes three weekdays off. Swap them with S02, S04, S08, S10 halfway through if you want Saturdays shared more widely.

**Cover each day** (minimum 5 Mon–Fri, 6 Sat, with key-holder and designer rules): Mon 8, Tue 9, Wed 8, Thu 9, Fri 8, Sat 6 — every day meets it. Saturday overtime stays a voluntary backstop for sickness and holidays.

**Other teams:** half on Pattern A, half on Pattern B, as you proposed.

## Measuring success

Baseline: autumn 2026. Track weekly:

- **Cover:** days meeting the showroom minimum (target 100%); overtime hours.
- **Trading:** design appointments (Saturdays are a third of them), sales, conversion.
- **Customers:** appointment waiting times; complaints.
- **People:** sickness/absence, turnover; anonymous survey of all 40 staff at weeks 1, 6 and 12 — not just the WhatsApp group.
- **Output:** installations on time (fitting), deliveries (warehouse), orders and payroll processed (office).
