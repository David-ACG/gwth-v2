We sold out by 9.40am last Saturday. Again.

Three people told me they had walked from Headingley and found an empty shelf. I felt awful. You got up early for bread and we let you down.

So we're changing how Saturdays work. From Saturday 7 November 2026, Saturday bread is pre-order only.

Here is how it works.

You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. You pay when you order. You collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. When you arrive, your loaf will be waiting for you. No queue at the till, because you've already paid.

If your plans change, cancel by 8pm Thursday for a full refund. After that we can't refund. By then the flour is weighed and your loaf is baked. It exists because you asked for it.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank. Good bread shouldn't end up in a bin.

Why the change. I only have one oven and two hands. I can bake 64 loaves on a Saturday, and not one more, however much coffee I drink. A bigger oven would mean a different shop, and I like this one. At the moment we run out early, and on a quiet week we throw away about 6 loaves. Neither sits right with me.

If you usually get here at 10am, this is for you most of all. Your loaf will be waiting, not sold from under you.

Weekdays stay as they are. Monday to Friday, walk in as normal.

We haven't done this before, so the first few weeks may be wobbly. Tell me when I get it wrong. I read every message.

Order your Saturday loaf on our website by 8pm on Thursday.
