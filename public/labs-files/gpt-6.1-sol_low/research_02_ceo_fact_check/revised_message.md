# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap fell from 14.2% to 7.4% in a year, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires. At the March 2026 snapshot, women held 41% of our filled senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year earlier.

**Pay.** In April, basic pay rose by 5% for grades 1 to 6, 3.5% for grades 7 to 8 and 2% for grade 9.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. All sickness absence fell from 4.5% to 4.2%.

**Growing.** At the March snapshot, Hollinsford remained our largest site, with 402 employees, while Thorncliffe had 371. Our Line 4 investment at Thorncliffe created 64 new permanent roles last year, with 58 filled at the end of March.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of those invited, including employees and long-term agency workers, took part. Our engagement score rose four percentage points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
