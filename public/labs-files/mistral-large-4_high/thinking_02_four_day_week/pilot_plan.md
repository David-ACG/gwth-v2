# Four-day week pilot: one-page plan

For Nina Ashworth, Managing Director — decision at the board meeting, 15 October 2026.

## Does the proposal work as written? No.

- **Hours.** 1,500 contracted hours a week now (40 staff × 37.5); 1,280 as proposed (40 × 32) — down 14.67%.
- **Weekdays are fine.** Under Patterns A and B, cover is Mon 6, Tue–Thu 12, Fri 6: every weekday meets the minimum of 5 with a key holder and kitchen designer on duty.
- **Saturdays collapse.** No four-day pattern includes Saturday, so Saturday cover is 0 against a minimum of 6 (1 key holder, 2 designers) on your busiest day (a third of design appointments).
- **Overtime cannot rescue it.** Only four showroom staff volunteered for Saturday overtime (S02, S07, S09, S11): cover of 4, one key holder, one designer. Cost: £617.40 every Saturday, £7,408.80 over the pilot, and still short.
- **The survey is weaker than "87%."** 15 of 40 staff responded (37.5%); 13 "yes" = 32.5% of all staff — indicative, not a mandate (11 of 15 wanted Friday off, 4 Monday).

## Your realistic options

1. **As written.** Not viable: Saturdays fail; paid overtime still fails and costs £7,408.80.
2. **Pilot with a redesigned showroom rota (recommended).** Saturdays staffed within contracted hours; no overtime; wage bill unchanged.
3. **Do not pilot.** Keep the five-day week: no risk, but no progress on retention and recruitment.

## Recommended pilot: 12 weeks, Mon 4 Jan to Sun 28 Mar 2027

**All staff:** four days a week, same weekly pay (32 hours; Saturday rota 31 — see note).

**Showroom — two fixed groups:**

- **Saturday rota (6):** S02, S07, S09, S10, S11, S12 work **Tue, Wed, Fri, Sat**. All four Saturday-overtime volunteers are on it, plus S10 and S12, who work Saturdays today (confirm with them); Mon, Thu and Sun off.
- **Weekday group (6):** S01, S03, S04, S05, S06, S08 work **Mon–Thu**, off Fri–Sun. S05 and S06 keep their no-Saturday childcare arrangements.

**Cover each day (minimum in brackets):**

- Mon 6 (5), Thu 6 (5): key holders S01, S08; designers S03, S04
- Tue 12 (5), Wed 12 (5): everyone in
- Fri 6 (5): key holder S02; designers S09, S10
- Sat 6 (6): key holder S02; designers S09, S10 — exactly the Saturday minimum

**Hours note:** the Saturday shift is 7 paid hours, so this rota works 31-hour weeks (3 × 8 + 7); anyone wanting 32 can work one longer weekday by arrangement.

**Other 28 staff:** half Mon–Thu, half Tue–Fri, as you proposed.

**Bank holiday:** Good Friday 26 March 2027 is the only one in the pilot: showroom closed, showroom staff have Friday off anyway, and the 14 Tue–Fri staff elsewhere get a paid day off.

**Fairness:** the Saturday rota works every Saturday for 12 weeks; if you keep the four-day week, rotate the groups so nobody works Saturdays permanently.

## How we would measure success

- **Cover:** 100% of days at or above minimum, checked weekly by the showroom manager.
- **Trading:** Saturday sales and design appointments within 5% of last year's same 12 Saturdays.
- **Productivity:** sales per staff-hour within 5% of baseline — hours fall 14.67% at the same wage bill.
- **People:** sickness absence, regretted leavers and recruitment enquiries tracked; pulse survey at weeks 6 and 12.
- **Cost:** unplanned overtime £0.

## Review

The pilot ends Sunday 28 March 2027; results are collated in the week of 5 April. **Review meeting: Monday 12 April 2027**, where you decide to keep, adjust or stop.
