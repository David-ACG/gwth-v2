# People update from Martin

To: All staff (email and noticeboards at all four sites)
From: Martin Ashdown, Chief Executive, Hollins Brook Foods Ltd
Planned send date: Monday 5 October 2026

---

Hello everyone,

Before the busy season starts, I want to share how we are doing as an employer. I am proud of these numbers, and they are yours.

**Fair pay.** Our median gender pay gap has fallen from 14.2% to 7.4% in a year, and our mean gap has fallen by a quarter. We have published our gender pay gap for the last two years, as the law requires of a business of our size. Women now hold 41% of our senior leadership roles, up from 35% a year ago.

**Pay.** In April, everyone covered by our collective agreement received a pay rise of 5%. Our managers received 3.5%, and our senior leadership team received 2%.

**A place people stay.** Voluntary staff turnover fell from 22.7% to 18.1% last year. That is below the 20.4% median for voluntary turnover across food and drink manufacturers in the North of England. Short-term sickness absence fell to 3.1% last year, and our overall sickness absence rate fell to 4.2%.

**Growing.** Thorncliffe is now our second-largest site, and our Line 4 investment there has already created 64 new permanent jobs, with up to 120 expected over three years, subject to a second customer contract.

**Your voice.** Thank you to everyone who filled in the PulsePoint survey: 79% of you took part. Our engagement score rose four points this year to 72%.

**Learning.** We invested £362,500 in training and development last year, and in our most recent onboarding survey, 87% of new starters rated their induction as good or excellent.

Thank you for everything you do.

Martin
