Last Saturday we sold out by 9.40am. Again. The Saturday before, the same.

3 people told me they had walked from Headingley and found an empty shelf. I felt awful. You got up early for bread, and we let you down. I hate that more than almost anything in this shop.

I have 1 oven and 2 hands. On a Saturday I can bake 64 loaves and not one more. On a quiet week we throw away about 6 loaves. Both of those things are daft, and I am tired of them. It is not stubbornness. It is the size of the oven.

So from Saturday 7 November, Saturday bread is pre-order only. There will be no walk-in bread on Saturdays. Weekday sales stay walk-in, exactly as they are now.

Here is how it works. You order on our website by 8pm on Thursday. You pay when you order, because we bake your loaf for you. We only bake what has been ordered. You collect between 8am and 11am on Saturday. Your loaf is baked on Saturday morning.

If you cannot make it, cancel by 8pm on Thursday. We will refund you in full. After that there is no refund. The bread is baked for you, and nobody else can eat it. I know some of you will not like that. The bread cannot be unbaked.

Anything not collected by 11am goes to the Chapeltown food bank, not the bin. The bread is still good at 11am. The food bank takes it from there.

I know "pre-order" sounds like a big-chain word. It is just a website and a deadline. The deadline is 8pm on Thursday. We have not tried this before. We do not know yet how many of you will order. If it is clumsy, tell me and I will fix it.

Order your Saturday loaf on the website before 8pm on Thursday.
