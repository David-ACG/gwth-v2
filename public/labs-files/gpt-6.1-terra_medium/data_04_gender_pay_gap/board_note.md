# Gender pay gap: 5 April 2026

Thistlecombe Care must publish its gender pay gap figures. It had 262 relevant employees on 5 April 2026, above the 250-employee threshold. The test is a headcount of employees, including part-time employees and those on leave, rather than full-time equivalents. Agency workers are counted by their agency. The hourly figures use 248 full-pay relevant employees; 14 were excluded because their April pay was reduced due to leave.

Men's mean hourly pay was £20.07 and women's was £15.91. The mean gender pay gap was 20.8% and the median gap was 4.1%, both in favour of men. These compare average and middle hourly rates across the workforce; they do not by themselves establish whether women and men receive equal pay for equal work.

The mean bonus gap was 77.5% and the median bonus gap was 77.5%, in favour of men. Bonuses were received by 34.2% of men and 28.4% of women. These gaps compare recipients' total bonuses in the year to 5 April 2026 and are not adjusted for part-time hours.

Women made up 79.0% of the lower hourly pay quartile, 66.1% of the lower middle, 72.6% of the upper middle and 58.1% of the upper. Each quartile contains 62 people.

We applied the recorded reversal of an annual bonus, cancelling the payment made in error. We also standardised the different sex labels in the HR export. An ethnicity pay gap cannot be calculated from these files because they contain no ethnicity data.
