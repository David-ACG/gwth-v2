# PwC 2026 AI Performance Study — The 20% Who Win

**Primary source:** PwC press release and study, Apr 2026.
**URL:** https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/news-room/press-releases/2026/pwc-2026-ai-performance-study.html

## Headline

> *Three-quarters (74%) of AI's economic gains are being captured by just 20% of companies.*

## Methodology

- **1,217 senior executives** surveyed.
- Primarily **large, publicly listed** companies.
- **25 sectors** represented globally.
- Questions cover AI-driven revenue, efficiency gains, deployment practices, and governance.

## What separates leaders from laggards

### 1. Growth, not productivity

Leaders think about AI as a growth technology, not an efficiency technology. They are:
- **2–3× more likely** to say they use AI to identify and pursue growth opportunities.
- **2.6× more likely** to report AI has improved their ability to reinvent the business model.

### 2. Scale of return

- Leaders generate **7.2× more AI-driven revenue and efficiency gains** than the average competitor.

### 3. Advanced deployment practices

Leaders run AI in fundamentally different ways:
- **1.8×** more likely to execute multiple tasks within guardrails.
- **1.9×** more likely to operate AI in autonomous, self-optimising ways.
- Increasing the number of decisions made without human intervention at **2.8×** the rate of peers.

### 4. Trust and governance

- **1.7×** more likely to have a Responsible AI framework.
- **1.5×** more likely to have a cross-functional AI governance board.

Trust isn't a brake on adoption — it's a *precondition* for scale.

## Why the leaders win (PwC's thesis)

The winning 20% are not just using more AI tools. They:
1. Use AI as a **catalyst for business reinvention**, not a faster horse.
2. Pursue **new revenue opportunities** emerging from industry convergence.
3. Build **strong foundations** around data, governance and trust *before* scaling.
4. Deploy AI with more autonomy because they have the governance to permit it.

## Key quotes

> *"The winning 20% aren't simply using more AI tools. They are using AI as a catalyst for growth and business reinvention."* — PwC 2026 study.

## How to use this in GWTH

- **Opening hook for Month 3.** Statistic-driven wake-up: *"three quarters of the AI gains are going to one in five companies — here's what's different about them."*
- **Self-diagnostic prompt.** Participants rate themselves: are we in the 20% or the 80%? What would it take to move?
- **Debunks "productivity" as the only frame.** Gives leaders permission to think about AI as a *revenue* project, not a cost-saving project.
