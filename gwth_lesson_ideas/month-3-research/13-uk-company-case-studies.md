# UK Company Case Studies — Matched to Each Month 3 Lesson

*Added 2026-04-20. For every US / non-UK company cited in the lesson ideas, this file identifies the UK company doing something materially similar, so UK students can point to a domestic reference.*

**Important:** nothing in this file replaces the existing US / global examples. It **adds** UK examples alongside them. Students benefit from seeing both.

## Financial services — banks

Pair with DBS Bank (Singapore). UK peers span all six capabilities.

### Lloyds Banking Group — the clearest UK parallel to DBS

- **Scale claim:** £50m AI value in 2025 → **£100m+ expected in 2026** — a DBS-style year-on-year compounding trajectory.
- **Agentic rollout:** agentic AI financial assistant going live early 2026, scaled to **21 million customer accounts**.
- **Investment envelope:** $5bn digital transformation programme.
- **Infrastructure:** migrated ML/AI workloads to Google Cloud Vertex AI (15 modelling systems / hundreds of models moved from on-prem).
- **Talent capability build:** **Lloyds AI Academy** launched to train teams across the organisation. **200 senior executives** trained in 2026 — this is the UK equivalent of DBS's culture-at-the-top approach.

### HSBC — organisational structure parallel

- Appointed **David Rice as first Chief AI Officer** (Mar 2026) — promoted from COO of corporate & institutional banking.
- Same move was made by **NatWest, Barclays, and Lloyds** in 2026 — UK banking norm is now to have a named C-level owner of AI.
- HSBC's three domains of AI dominance: international trade finance, cross-border wealth management, multi-jurisdictional risk mitigation.

### NatWest — customer-journey AI

- **Cora / Cora+** (enhanced by GenAI, OpenAI partnership, 2024): **150% increase in customer satisfaction scores** for chatbot interactions.
- Always-on fraud reporting via AI (no waiting on hold).

### Barclays — employee productivity at scale

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot deployed to 100,000 employees globally** — one of the largest UK enterprise agentic-AI rollouts.
- AI-driven wealth-management platform for personalised investment advice.

## Retail — supermarkets and high street

Global reference: no single Rewired retail case. UK has three distinct retail archetypes.

### Tesco — the Rewired-in-retail archetype

- **£500m productivity savings** in FY2026 from AI + automation.
- **Three-year strategic partnership with Mistral AI** (French foundation model lab).
- AI demand forecasting → inventory optimisation, reduced food waste, higher on-shelf availability.
- **Whoosh rapid delivery**: now from 1,400+ stores, covering 60%+ of UK population, AI-optimised logistics and automated micro-fulfilment.
- Trialling an **AI shopping assistant with 280,000 colleagues** before customer rollout.
- **£1.5bn capex** specifically on digital / AI transformation.

### Marks & Spencer — agentic AI in-store

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot deployed to 11,000 store managers** — one of the largest enterprise Copilot rollouts in retail.
- **Sparks loyalty relaunch** with ML + GenAI for personalised offers.
- **Multi-year TCS partnership** to modernise tech foundations and embed AI.

### Ocado Group — the UK harness-engineering-in-physical-systems parallel

- **Ocado Smart Platform (OSP)** — over **100 applications of AI embedded end-to-end** across the platform.
- **3,000+ patents** granted and filed.
- **2,500+ technologists** in the team.
- **AI "air traffic control"** orchestrates robot fleet, talking to each bot **10 times/second**.
- MODEX 2026: **Ocado IQ** launched — cloud-based AI software suite with Smart Bypass, orchestration across every warehouse workflow.
- **OGRP robotic arms** deployed in 120+ warehouses worldwide. Chuck AMR implementations: 2–3× efficiency gains, 50% lower labour costs than manual pick.
- Ocado is the physical-world counterpart to Ramp's Glass: a moat built from data + AI that eventually becomes a product.

## Energy — the UK's "internal tool became the moat" story

### Octopus Energy / Kraken — the UK's closest analogue to Ramp Glass

- Built **Kraken** as an internal operations platform; now an **$8.65bn independent company** after Dec 2025 spin-out ($1bn raised).
- Operates utilities for **70M+ accounts worldwide** via licensing — EDF, E.ON, Origin, Good Energy, Plenitude (Eni), Severn Trent (4.6m UK water customers).
- **15 billion data points/day** processed.
- The internal harness *became* the external product. Directly validates Seb Goddijn's / Eric Glyman's argument (from Ramp): **internal AI infrastructure is a moat — and sometimes the entire business.**
- For GWTH: the single best UK example of "raise the floor, don't lower the ceiling" at scale.

## Manufacturing & engineering

### Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) — UK counterpart to Toyota

- **NVIDIA DRIVE AGX** AI platform goes into every new Range Rover, Defender, Discovery, Jaguar from 2026.
- **Tata Communications partnership**: connected services across **120 countries**.
- **2.5TB of vehicle data generated daily** across the connected fleet.
- **500,000 ECU updates per month** on average.
- JLR's Reimagine strategy: electrification + AI + circularity → 20% emissions cut, net-zero by 2039.
- **Partnership with Ericsson**: private 5G + AI-driven smart manufacturing at UK plants.
- JLR uses **Anaplan**-based planning transformation as the operational spine for AI-supported decisions.

### Rolls-Royce — the UK prior-art for Freeport-McMoRan-scale AI

- **R² Data Labs** — industrial AI + ML across engine design, manufacturing, operations; data innovation cells built on DevOps principles.
- Hubs in **UK, US, Germany, Singapore, India, New Zealand**.
- **30 years of data-led services** — the definition of proprietary data advantage.
- Commercial "IntelligentEngine" proposition turned R² Data Labs output into customer value.

### Dyson — AI-in-product-development archetype

- **£2.75bn** investment plan over five years (Singapore, UK, Philippines).
- Deep investment in robotics + machine learning research on the **Hullavington Airfield Campus** (UK).
- AI optimises cyclone air paths and other product systems — a worked example of "AI in R&D" rather than "AI in ops".

## Aviation

### British Airways / IAG — UK counterpart to LATAM Airlines

- **£100m biometric + AI infrastructure** investment.
- **£7bn** overall transformation plan (BA).
- **600 AI use cases** value-mapped — then consolidated into a focused AI strategy, live from **January 2026**.
- **IAG.ai labs** in **London and Barcelona** (Rewired-style in-house Labs).
- **100+ data scientists** across the group; 80 at BA.
- **Dr Ben Dias** appointed as IAG's first Chief AI Scientist.
- **EOS (Engine Optimisation System)** — in-house AI designed at Barcelona IAG.ai lab; saves millions on engine maintenance scheduling.
- Heathrow **86% on-time departure rate** (record) attributed to ML optimisation of landing slots, air-traffic congestion simulation, weather forecasting.

## Telecoms — workforce and agentic transformation

### BT Group / Openreach

- **Up to 55,000 jobs cut by 2030** (CEO warns the real number may be higher once AI reaches maturity).
- **Openreach NiCE Cognigy AI agents**: redesigned **15M customer journeys** in the UK's biggest fibre rollout.
- **2,800 new digital roles** added to BT Digital — product, software, cloud, design, data, AI, ML.
- On track for **25M fibre-to-the-premises** homes by 2026.

## Fintech — native AI native companies

### Starling, Monzo, Revolut — UK fintech trio

- **Starling Bank**:
  - **8,000 hours/month saved** on admin via AI-generated chat summaries.
  - **Google Gemini for Workspace** rolled out company-wide.
  - Agentic AI 'assistant' (Gemini-backed) for personalised financial insights.
- **Monzo**:
  - **42% of simpler customer queries** resolved via AI self-service.
  - Dedicated AI for financial crime and fraud.
- **Revolut**:
  - **AIR (AI assistant)** — customer rollout from April 2026.
  - Conversational finance management (spending, investments, subscriptions, card freezing).
- Together these three show UK-native AI-first product thinking at SME → mid-cap scale.

## Public sector — the UK's Rewired-scale transformation

### UK Civil Service — Humphrey

- Suite: **Consult** (consultation analysis), **Parlex** (Hansard search), **Minute** (transcription + summaries), **Lex** (legal research), **Redbox** (day-to-day policy tasks).
- Built **in-house by i.AI** (Incubator for Artificial Intelligence).
- Target: **£80m+/year** savings on consultation analysis alone.
- Adjacent data: 26 min/person/day saved in AI-using civil-service trial × ~550k civil servants = **1M+ weeks of effort saved per year**.
- Directly validates Rewired's 30/70 in-house-talent rule **at government scale**.

### NHS

- Diagnostic AI deployed across **66 NHS trusts** (chest imaging).
- Cut stroke treatment times in half; sped prostate cancer diagnosis.
- NHS AI trust rating **63% net positive** — highest of any UK organisation for AI use (public or private).
- Cautionary tale too: many diagnostic tools struggled with integration due to insufficient clinical engagement — perfect material for L18 *midstream adjustments* lesson.

## Deep-tech / AI-core UK

### DeepMind, Wayve, Synthesia, Stability AI

- **DeepMind** (London): AlphaFold Nobel; Gemini for Education pilots in England curriculum + Northern Ireland teachers (10 hrs/week saved).
- **Wayve** (London): end-to-end learning for autonomous mobility; $1.2bn raised, $4–8bn valuation, robotaxi trials 2026.
- **Synthesia** (London): $4bn valuation, $100m+ ARR (2025).
- **Stability AI** (London): Stable Diffusion — democratising generative-AI access.
- **UK Sovereign AI Fund** £500m backing first cohort (2026).

These are the UK answer to "who are our OpenAI / Anthropic". Use for sovereignty / moat conversations.
