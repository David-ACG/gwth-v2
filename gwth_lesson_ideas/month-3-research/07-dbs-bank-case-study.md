# DBS Bank — The Flagship AI Transformation Case

**Primary sources:** McKinsey, Forrester, Harvard Business School, IMD, DBS Newsroom.

## Why this case matters

DBS (Singapore-headquartered, multinational bank) has been McKinsey's exemplar of end-to-end AI transformation for over a decade. It appears as **Chapter 39** (expanded) in *Rewired* 2nd edition. It is the single most-cited corporate case in the McKinsey playbook because it spans **all six capabilities** (roadmap, talent, operating model, technology, data, adoption).

## Headline numbers

- **800+ AI/ML models** deployed.
- **350+ use cases** in production.
- **SGD 180M economic value** captured from AI/ML in 2022 (SGD 150M revenue uplift + SGD 30M cost avoidance and productivity).
- **SGD 1B+ expected economic impact** in 2025.
- **AI deployment time reduced from 18 months to <5 months** after programme launch (with McKinsey support).
- **~30% of all job vacancies** filled by internal mobility using AI-powered upskilling.
- **83% increase** in consumer savings adoption (customer-segment AI).
- **95% prediction accuracy** for at-risk small-business loans.

## The DBS transformation framework (three pillars)

1. **Digital to the core.** Cloud- and open-source-first stack; APIs and microservices; in-house tech capability.
2. **Embed yourself in the customer journey.** Don't build the bank as a destination — build it into the moments where customers need banking.
3. **Operate like a 22,000-person startup.** Platform teams, squads, product ownership, experimentation culture.

## The PURE framework (responsible data use at DBS)

All AI use of customer data must be:
- **P**urposeful
- **U**nsurprising
- **R**espectful
- **E**xplainable

This is a clean, memorable customer-facing framing of responsible AI — much shorter than most "Responsible AI" lists and explicitly anchored in customer trust.

## Key lessons for Month 3

- **Talent-first.** DBS famously invested in in-house capability before AI was hot; that paid off enormously when AI arrived.
- **Sustained multi-year programme.** Not a sprint; not a pilot. DBS has been transforming continuously since ~2014.
- **Economic value, not just tech deployment.** Every programme has a revenue or cost-saving line attached.
- **Human + AI, not human replaced by AI.** DBS frames staff as beneficiaries of AI-enabled upskilling.

## How to use this in GWTH

- **Week on measuring impact.** DBS is the archetype for "what does a measured AI programme look like". Participants build their own version of the SGD 180M → SGD 1B progression, scaled to their size.
- **PURE as a takeaway artefact.** Participants adapt PURE for their own organisation — four words, memorable, customer-facing.
- **Talent-first precedent.** Closes the argument that >70% of AI talent should be in-house. DBS is the proof.
- **Counter-example to "we're too big / too regulated / too old".** DBS is all three and still did it.
