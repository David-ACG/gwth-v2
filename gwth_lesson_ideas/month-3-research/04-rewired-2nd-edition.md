# Rewired — 2nd Edition (McKinsey, Apr 2026)

**Full title:** *Rewired: McKinsey's Playbook on How Leading Companies Win with Technology and AI*, 2nd edition.
**Authors:** Eric Lamarre, Kate Smaje, Robert Levin, Alex Singla, Alexander Sukharevsky.
**Publisher:** Wiley. ISBN 9781394381906. Published 20 Apr 2026.
**Length:** 39 chapters, seven sections, plus four case studies.

## What changed in the 2nd edition

The 1st edition (2023) predated the agentic era. The 2nd edition is substantially rewritten around:

- **Agentic AI** as a spine — runs across multiple chapters (Ch 5 roadmap, Ch 11 talent, Ch 22 technology).
- **The new economics of AI transformations** (Ch 2, new) — unit economics, payback curves, value-at-stake for the gen-AI era.
- **Tech muscle of business leaders** (Ch 8, new) — a full chapter on upgrading *non-technical* leaders, not just IT.
- **Reimagining workflows with agents** (Ch 5, new).
- **Proprietary data advantage** (Ch 29, new).
- **Right to deploy AI** (Ch 34, expanded) — governance reframed for autonomous-action AI.
- **Midstream adjustments** (Ch 33, new) — the realism chapter: programmes pivot.
- **New case studies:** LATAM Airlines, Toyota.
- **Expanded case studies:** Freeport-McMoRan, DBS Bank. (The 1st-edition LEGO case is dropped.)

## The six capabilities (unchanged spine)

| # | Capability | Book section |
|---|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Transformation roadmap that targets real value | §1 |
| 2 | Talent bench of highly skilled experts | §2 |
| 3 | Operating model that moves at pace | §3 |
| 4 | Distributed, flexible technology environment | §4 |
| 5 | Data embedded throughout the organisation | §5 |
| 6 | Adoption and scaling that realises meaningful gains | §6 |

All six are required. You cannot skip one. *"A good operating model can't work without the right talent."*

## Key quantitative claims (from the introduction / manifesto section)

- 20% EBITDA uplift on average for AI leaders.
- 1–2 year payback.
- $3 incremental EBITDA per $1 invested.
- >70% of AI talent should be in-house.
- *"For every $1 spent on developing digital and AI solutions, plan to spend at least another $1 to ensure full user adoption and scaling."*
