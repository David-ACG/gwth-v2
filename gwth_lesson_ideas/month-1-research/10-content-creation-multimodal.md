## Audio — April 2026

- **ElevenLabs v3.** Starter $6/mo (best student entry). Creator $22. Pro $99. Voice cloning requires consent (recorded consent phrase).
- **ElevenLabs Audiobook Platform** (Feb 2026) — 60% royalty on direct sales + Spotify distribution.
- **AI avatar video:**
  - **Synthesia** (UK-founded, London) — **70%+ of FTSE 100** use for L&D. Starter ~$22 (10-min cap); Enterprise for full translation. Clients include NatWest, Lloyds, NHS, UN.
  - **HeyGen Avatar IV** — Creator $29 unlimited videos, 175+ languages. UK invoices include VAT.

## Video — IMPORTANT 2026 reality

- **OpenAI Sora is shutting down 26 April 2026** (app; API 24 Sep). Codenamed "Spud" signalled but not public. **Do not teach Sora.**
- **Google Veo 3.1 / 3.1 Fast / Lite** — **only mainstream model with synced audio**. In AI Pro $19.99 (~£16). Integrated into Gemini app, Flow, Google Vids (April 2026). **Month 1 default.**
- **Runway Gen-4.5** — cinematic control; Standard $12/mo.
- **Kling 3.0** — budget pick; ~$0.50/clip via fal.ai; up to 2-min clips.
- **Seedance 2.0** (ByteDance) — topped 2026 quality benchmarks.

## Presentations

- ChatGPT or Claude to draft a deck; export to PowerPoint/Google Slides.
- Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Tome for polished decks.

## UK context

- **Synthesia** is the UK's standout AI company for video — widely adopted by UK corporates.
- **UK Music** actively lobbying on AI training-data licensing.
- **ASA** (Advertising Standards Authority) has guidance on AI-generated endorsements.
- UK Copyright: IPO consultation on text-and-data-mining exception still ongoing; commercial use of AI-generated output is generally fine but never generate real-person likenesses commercially without consent.
- **Reform UK sausage-finger image (9 April 2026)** — flagship UK example of what not to do in political/brand image.
