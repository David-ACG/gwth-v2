# Portfolio & Presentation (L20)

## Why the portfolio matters

Students leave Month 1 with real evidence they can do things their colleagues, family and employer can't. That evidence is the **career-accelerator differentiator** mentioned in L1. The portfolio *is* the transformation receipt.

## What goes in the portfolio

1. **Your Tool Log** — honest table of which tool you reached for when.
2. **Your Prompt Library** — 10–20 best prompts, categorised.
3. **Your Life Research Project** (L6 output — PDF or page).
4. **Your Content Package** (L7 + L8 — email, social, long-form, image, deck, TTS, optionally video).
5. **Your apps** (L9, L10 — screenshots + live demo links if published).
6. **Your website** (L11 — live URL).
7. **Your dashboard** (L13 — screenshots + Artifact link).
8. **Your Custom Assistant** (L15 — Custom GPT/Claude Project link, 10 test queries).
9. **Your automation(s)** (L16, L19 — Zap/Make screenshots + description).
10. **Your Family AI Bot demo** (L19–L20 — 2-minute video, demo link, instructions page).
11. **Personal statement** — "Before GWTH, I… Now I…"

## Where the portfolio lives

- **Lovable** single site (recommended — they built one in L11).
- **Notion** public page.
- **Claude Artifact** with persistent storage as a hub.
- PDF export for emailing.

## The 2-minute demo

1. **10 sec** — context: "This is my Family AI Bot. It turns our Sunday family meeting into the week's plan."
2. **30 sec** — input: drop the recording into the folder / upload to the app.
3. **60 sec** — watch the outputs appear (tasks, events, meals, shopping).
4. **20 sec** — one distribution channel firing (email or calendar).
5. **10 sec** — sign off: "That took 90 minutes a week off my wife's shoulders."
