# UK Beginner AI Use Cases — Jan–Apr 2026

The single most important research file for keeping the course UK-first. Every Month 1 lesson should cite at least one point from here.

## Headline UK statistics

- **54% of UK adults use ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini** (Ofcom Adults' Media Use and Attitudes Report, 2 Apr 2026, n=7,533). Teens 16–24: **79%**; 25–34: **74%**.
- **12% of AI users use them for companionship** (19% of 25–34s). Specific non-technical uses Ofcom flagged: **wedding speeches, room-layout planning, breakup advice, DIY research, artwork**.
- **54% of UK firms using AI**, only **11% deeply** (British Chambers of Commerce, March 2026).
- **UK AI investment: £2.6 bn (2023) → £4.0 bn (2024) → £4.7 bn (2025).**
- **LinkedIn 2026 Skills on the Rise (UK):** demand for AI literacy **up 6× year-on-year**.
- **£90 bn productivity swing** on whether AI delivers or fails (OBR Spring 2026 forecast).
- **OBR Spring 2026:** AI seen as decisive to UK productivity trajectory.

## 15 topic-by-topic UK anchors

### 1. Everyday personal AI (L1, L5)
- **UK example:** Ofcom's own 2026 case studies — wedding speeches, DIY planning, breakup advice.
- **Global contrast:** **OpenAI ChatGPT Health** launched 7 Jan 2026 — excluded from UK/EEA/Switzerland. Teaching point: "US tutorial ≠ UK reality."

### 2. Prompting skills spread (L4)
- **Skills England + DSIT** expanded (28 Jan 2026): free AI training for **10 M UK workers by 2030**; NHS + techUK partners.
- **UK training providers:** London School of Innovation (5-week online), Happy Ltd (Aldgate half-day), The Knowledge Academy (1-day ChatGPT Prompt Engineering Certification).
- **Gap flag:** no clear viral UK prompt library found — use Skills England free benchmark as the substitute.

### 3. Research with AI (L6)
- **IPPR (2 Feb 2026):** ChatGPT cites **Guardian in 58%** of responses; **BBC absent** from ChatGPT and Gemini (BBC blocks them); **Perplexity uses BBC in 36%** of news queries. Brilliant UK teaching moment — tool choice = citation mix.
- **Press Gazette** tracker of which journalists are most cited by AI engines (BBC, Guardian, Reuters, FT, Independent).

### 4. Content creation — written (L7)
- **Gap flag:** no equivalent UK creator to the US "0-to-100k in 65 days" growth story.
- **Closest-fit UK voices:** Azeem Azhar (Exponential View), Benedict Evans, Ian Leslie (The Ruffian), Daniel Priestley, Tom Goodwin — all on Substack / LinkedIn.

### 5. Visual + video (L8)
- **UK example (warning):** **Reform UK AI sausage-finger supporters image (9 April 2026)** — what not to do.
- **UK example (liar's dividend):** **RSPCA real 250-dogs photo (10 April 2026)** denied as AI.
- **UK winner:** **Synthesia** (London) — **70%+ FTSE 100** customers; NHS, NatWest, Lloyds, UN.
- **Video reality:** Sora shuts 26 Apr 2026; use **Veo 3.1**.
- **ElevenLabs Audiobook Platform** Feb 2026 — 60% royalty on direct, Spotify distribution.

### 6. Vibe coding / building (L9, L10, L11)
- **Lovable**: 8 M users, $200 M ARR (Nov 2025). **UK ≈ 5% of traffic** (#2 or #3 non-US market).
- **Claude Cowork** was itself built *"almost entirely by Claude Code in ~1.5 weeks"* per Anthropic's Felix Rieseberg (Axios, Jan 2026).

### 7. Data analysis for non-coders (L12)
- **Claude for Excel** (sidebar add-in, 2025–26) — UK SMEs building 3-statement models, DCF templates from natural language. Requires Claude Pro ($20/£16).
- **UK practitioner:** The Data School UK (Tableau-focused) publishing dashboard-week posts with ChatGPT.
- **Gap flag:** no discrete named UK SME dashboard case study surfaced.

### 8. Dashboard building (L13)
- **Gap flag:** no strong UK case. Use **CSL UK** (csl-uk.com/future-of-ai-and-dashboards/) as closest-fit.
- **Teaching substitute:** live-demo a UK-public-data dashboard (ONS, TfL, NHS appointments) from a CSV.

### 9. Agents — Cowork / Chrome / OpenClaw (L14)
- **Claude for Chrome** now on all paid plans.
- **Microsoft disables Claude-in-Copilot by default for UK and EU tenants** pending DPIA.
- **BCC:** 54% using, 11% deeply — the agent gap.
- **OpenClaw:** no reliable UK adoption data. **Do not claim UK case studies.**

### 10. Custom GPTs / Claude Projects (L15)
- **UK examples:** **Illuminate AI** (illuminateai.co.uk) + **Writearm** (writearm.co.uk) published public walk-throughs of their Custom GPTs.
- **UK directory:** e-innovate.co.uk "Best Custom GPT Software for UK Businesses 2026".
- **Global asset:** OpenAI Academy *Four GPT templates for small businesses* (5 March 2026).

### 11. Automation (L16)
- **toptenaiagents.co.uk** UK TCO: **n8n £1,560 · Make £1,070 · Zapier £6,670+** for 500 k ops/year.
- **n8n on AWS London / OVHcloud Erith** → 100% UK data sovereignty.
- **n8n 2.0 (Jan 2026):** AI Agent Tool Node, persistent memory, RAG support.

### 12. Family Bot / transcription style (L18)
- **Gap flag:** no UK family-meeting AI story.
- **Practical tools in UK:** Fathom free unlimited, Otter Pro, Zoom AI Companion (host-only retention).
- **UK legal caveat:** recording children under 13 raises **UK GDPR Age Appropriate Design Code** — mention briefly.

### 13. UK regulatory context (60-second version for L5)
- **ICO · NCSC · FCA · AISI (AI Security Institute — renamed 2025) · AI Opportunities Action Plan (38 of 50 delivered).**
- **Bank of England FPC April 2026:** advanced AI not yet systemic risk in UK finance, but escalating fast.
- **EU AI Act Article 50 disclosure** (Aug 2026 milestone): UK SMEs with EU customers need to disclose.

### 14. UK safety / hallucination cases (L5)
- **Ayinde / Al-Haroun, Parsons, M v F, Re A,B,C,D, Brightwaters** — ~60 UK hallucination cases on record.
- **Reform UK** sausage-finger image; **RSPCA** liar's dividend.
- **Amelia AI schoolgirl far-right meme (31 Jan 2026)** — CNN covered; contextual awareness piece.

### 15. UK AI training boom (L20 close)
- **£27 M government funding** (Jan 2026) for tech jobs pipeline.
- **10 M UK workers lack essential digital skills; 7.3 M (18%) lack workplace digital essentials; 49% had no AI learning at work.**
- **QA Ltd** trains 270 k UK learners/yr, serves 96% of FTSE. **Corndel** 30%+ of FTSE 100. **Synthesia** 70%+ FTSE 100 for AI training video.
- **Databricks $850 M UK investment (2026).**
