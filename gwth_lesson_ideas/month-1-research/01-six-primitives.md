# The Six OpenAI Primitives — a beginner-facing guide

*Source: OpenAI — "Identifying and Scaling AI Use Cases: How Early Adopters Focus Their AI Efforts" (PDF, April 2025). Download: https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/identifying-and-scaling-ai-use-cases.pdf*

## Origin

OpenAI analysed **600+ use cases from 300 enterprise implementations and 2 million business users** and distilled them into six primitives. The report says: *"Most use cases fall into one of six 'primitives' — fundamental use case types that apply across all departments and disciplines"* (p. 10). The list on p. 11 is:

1. **Content Creation**
2. **Research**
3. **Coding** (aka Building)
4. **Data Analysis**
5. **Ideation & Strategy**
6. **Automation**

By April 2026 the framework is the de-facto corporate-AI taxonomy (BCG's "Use Case Olympics", McKinsey's *Superagency in the Workplace* Jan 2025). Two 2026 extensions to know:
- **Workflow mapping** — stringing primitives end-to-end (OpenAI p. 29–30).
- **Agentic primitives** — the same categories executed autonomously by agents.

## Per-primitive plain-English brief for beginners

### 1. Content Creation
AI helps you stop facing a blank page. Draft, polish, repurpose, translate, or visualise words and media.
**Beginner examples:** a birthday speech in your own voice; a book-club summary of a long PDF; a logo and cover for a homemade recipe book.
**Tools (April 2026):** ChatGPT-5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, Ideogram v3 (text in images), Midjourney V8, ElevenLabs v3 (TTS + voice cloning).

### 2. Research
Gather, synthesise, structure information faster than Googling.
**Beginner examples:** compare three car-insurance PDFs; plan a holiday with Perplexity citations; "best primary schools near SW11" with sources.
**Tools:** Perplexity (92% factual accuracy, always cited; **Comet browser now free worldwide**), ChatGPT Deep Research, Gemini 3 Pro, Claude Projects for document corpora.

### 3. Coding / Building 🔨
Describe software in plain English → get working code. The one primitive that compounds — it gives you back *software*, not just time.
**Beginner examples:** family shopping-list web app in Lovable in 20 min; "what's in the fridge?" meal suggester in Claude Artifacts; a birthday countdown page in Bolt.
**Tools:** Lovable (60% of users are non-developers), Bolt.new, v0, Replit Agent, Claude Artifacts (now persistent up to 20 MB), Claude Code, Google AI Studio vibe-coding mode.

### 4. Data Analysis
Get insights from spreadsheets, PDFs, and screenshots without Excel skills.
**Beginner examples:** a year of bank statements → "where is my money going?"; photo of an energy bill → "am I on a good tariff?"; spreadsheet of kids' football scores → charts.
**Tools:** ChatGPT Advanced Data Analysis / Code Interpreter, Claude with Analysis tool, Gemini 3 in Google Sheets, Claude Desktop with file access.

### 5. Ideation & Strategy
AI as a thinking partner — brainstorm, stress-test, simulate stakeholders, roleplay hard conversations.
**Beginner examples:** voice-mode birthday-party brainstorm while doing the washing up; roleplay a screen-time chat with a teenager; "poke holes in my small-business plan as a banker."
**Tools:** ChatGPT Voice, Claude Opus 4.7 (deep reasoning), Gemini 3 Pro (multimodal plan critique).

### 6. Automation
Hand off repeatable tasks. AI runs them on a schedule or trigger.
**Beginner examples:** every Sunday summarise the family calendar and email it to both parents; Gmail label → accounting folder with summary; morning Cowork routine — open browser, check diary, draft daily-plan emails.
**Tools:** Zapier (Copilot + AI Agents, 8,000+ apps), Make.com (Maia AI assistant), n8n 2.0 (UK self-hosted for GDPR), Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Scheduled Tasks, Custom GPTs.

## Why Building is the leverage primitive for individuals

- **Market:** vibe-coding market ~$4.7 B in 2026, projected $25 B by 2030.
- **Adoption:** Stack Overflow 2025 — **84% of developers** use or plan to use AI tools; GitHub March 2026 — 82% actively using.
- **Non-developers:** **60% of Lovable's users are non-developers**; Taskade Genesis 63%.
- **YC:** 25% of W25 batch codebases ≥95% AI-generated; 40% of 2026 SaaS MVPs primarily vibe-coded.
- **Language:** Karpathy — *"the hottest new programming language is English"* (2023); Collins named **"vibe coding" Word of the Year 2025**.

Other primitives give you back hours. **Building gives you back software that keeps working.** For a UK adult whose AI experience is "asked ChatGPT a few things," learning to build is the single highest-ROI skill in Month 1.

## The Family AI Bot capstone embodies all six primitives

- **Research:** finding recipes for the meal plan
- **Content:** writing the tasks / emails / shopping list
- **Coding:** building the processing engine and hub page
- **Data Analysis:** transforming an unstructured transcript into structured outputs
- **Ideation:** designing the system in L17
- **Automation:** the scheduled pipeline in L19

That's why it's the capstone.
