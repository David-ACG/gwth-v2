# Content Creation — Writing (L7)

## The three moves that matter most

1. **Voice matching.** Paste three samples of your own writing and say *"match this style."* Single biggest quality lever for everyday content.
2. **The 70/30 rule.** Let AI do 70% of heavy lifting; you add the 30% that makes it yours. Students often try 0/100 or 100/0 — both wrong.
3. **System prompts.** Some tools (Claude Projects, Custom GPTs) let you set permanent rules: *"always write in British English"*, *"never use em-dashes"*, *"never use the word 'delve'"*. Set once, forget.

## The workflow

Rough draft → AI polish → your final touches. **Never:** blank-page → AI final. **Always:** you own the last read-through.

## Content types for L7's "Content Sprint"

- One **email** you actually have to send today.
- One **social media post** for LinkedIn / X / Instagram you actually want to publish.
- One **longer piece** (blog post, article, letter, report).

## British English defaults

Teach from day one: British spelling (colour, realise, centre), British dates (DD/MM/YYYY), British currency (£), British idioms. Claude Opus/Sonnet and GPT-5.4 both respect "write in British English" system prompts reliably.
