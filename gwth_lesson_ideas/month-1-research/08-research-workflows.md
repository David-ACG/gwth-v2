# Research with AI — Beginner Workflows (L6)

## The research workflow to teach

**Broad scan → dig deeper → verify → synthesise.**

1. **Broad scan** with Perplexity to map the landscape and get citations.
2. **Dig deeper** with ChatGPT Deep Research or Claude Research for narrative synthesis.
3. **Verify** by checking one citation from each claim you'll use.
4. **Synthesise** into a one-page summary with five verified sources.

## Upload-and-ask patterns

Drag a PDF / image / spreadsheet / audio file into AI and ask questions:

- "Summarise this 200-page report in 10 bullet points."
- "Compare the fee structures across these 3 mortgage offers."
- "What does this blood-test result mean?" (with standard verify caveat).
- "Extract all company names and dates from this document."

## Multi-source triangulation (L6 build)

Ask the same research question to two different tools. Compare. Tool choice is not neutral — Perplexity cites BBC (36% of news queries); ChatGPT doesn't (BBC blocks it). IPPR's Feb 2026 analysis makes this visible.

## Context-window discipline (from L4)

Long PDFs: break into sections. Uploading a 500-page report and asking "summarise" gives worse results than iterating section by section.
