[TODO-VERIFY] Pairs with Ch 3: if the unit of transformation is a domain, the owner of the redesign must be the business leader of that domain. ## Key concepts

- The reimagination, not the implementation, is the business leader's job.
- Time investment and decision rights of the domain leader.
