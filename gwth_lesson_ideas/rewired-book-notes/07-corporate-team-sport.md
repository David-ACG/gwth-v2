# Ch 7 — The ultimate corporate team sport

*Pages 111–124. Expanded from the 1st edition.*

## Summary

[TODO-VERIFY] The chapter argues that digital and AI transformation is not owned by any one function — not IT, not digital, not the CTO. It requires active participation from every part of the business: line leaders, HR, finance, risk, procurement, the CEO. In the 1st edition this sat at the front of the Talent section; in the 2nd edition it closes Section One as a capstone on the people side of the roadmap.

## Key concepts

- Multi-function participation as a precondition for scale.
- Roles of non-technology functions (HR, finance, risk) in transformation.
- The CEO as active participant, not sponsor.
