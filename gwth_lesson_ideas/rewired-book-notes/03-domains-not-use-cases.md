# Ch 3 — Focus on business domains, not use cases

*Pages 49–62. Expanded from the 1st edition.*

## Summary

[TODO-VERIFY] One of the book's defining arguments: the unit of transformation is a **business domain** (a customer journey, a process, or a product line), not a "use case". Use-case-hunting produces a portfolio of disconnected pilots; domain work produces sequenced, cumulative value. The 2nd edition expands the chapter, likely with reinforced contrast between the two modes and updated examples in the gen-AI era where use-case proliferation is a particular risk.

## Key concepts

- Domain as the unit of transformation.
- Use-case-hunting as an anti-pattern.
- Domain prioritisation and sequencing on the roadmap.
