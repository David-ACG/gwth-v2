# Talent

*Rewired*'s talent section is substantially rewritten in the 2nd edition. The 1st-edition aspiration of 70–80 percent of digital talent in-house remains a useful anchor, but the section now opens with a **business-leader capability** chapter (Ch 8, new: "Building the tech muscle of your business leaders") before turning to IT talent. Ch 11 (new) explicitly reframes the whole talent model for an "agentic world" in which humans and agents share the work.

## Core framework (2nd edition)

1. **Tech muscle of business leaders** (Ch 8, new) — the talent chapter that isn't about engineers. Business leaders have to be literate and fluent enough in tech and AI to lead domain redesign. This is new to the 2nd edition and likely fills a gap the 1st edition was criticised for.
2. **IT talent "surgery"** (Ch 9, expanded) — a structural re-sort of the IT organisation, not a headcount pass. "Surgery" suggests deliberate reshaping toward the skills and seniority the roadmap demands.
3. **Environment where tech talent thrives** (Ch 10) — the EVP, tools, growth, culture. Carry-over from the 1st edition.
4. **Talent model for an agentic world** (Ch 11, new) — the big 2nd-edition bet: what team shapes and roles look like when agents are team members. Likely revisits the 70/80–20/30 split.

## 2nd-edition-specific watchouts

- **Tech muscle** (Ch 8): look for a diagnostic or competency model for business leaders; this would be directly reusable in the GWTH curriculum. Flag any "levels" framing or assessment tool.
- **Agentic talent model** (Ch 11): highlight anything on role redefinition, agent-human ratio, performance measurement under shared work. This is the chapter most likely to date fast — anchor analytical frames, not specific tool claims.
- The 1st edition's "distinctive technologists / pay for skills" content (1st-ed Ch 11) appears to have been absorbed or moved; verify where it now lives.

## GWTH course application

- Ch 8 is a direct fit for GWTH — the course's core audience *is* non-technical business leaders trying to build tech muscle. Plan to anchor several course modules on this chapter.
- Ch 11's agentic talent model is a natural closer for a talent module — starts with "what your team should learn" and ends with "how the team will look when agents join".
- Skip granular IT-talent content (Ch 9, Ch 10) for the main GWTH track; keep for the optional leader track.
