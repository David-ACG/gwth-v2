# Adoption and scaling

Section Six is renamed in the 2nd edition to **"Adoption and Scaling: Your Force Multipliers"** — the framing makes explicit that adoption and scaling are the multipliers that turn a working solution into enterprise value. Two new chapters appear: **Plan for midstream adjustments** (Ch 33) and **So, what about culture?** (Ch 35, restated as new — likely substantially rewritten from the 1st-edition Ch 32). The risk chapter (Ch 34) is renamed to "Risk, trust, and the right to deploy AI" — an explicit gate-keeping framing for AI-specific deployment.

## Core framework (2nd edition)

1. **Make adoption stick** (Ch 30, expanded) — adoption is a business-process redesign problem, not a UX problem. Carries forward 1st-edition framing ("nail user adoption and underlying business").
2. **Design for scale and reuse** (Ch 31, expanded) — solutions built once should lift into other domains with limited rework.
3. **Tracking impact** (Ch 32) — KPI discipline; measure what was promised in the roadmap.
4. **Plan for midstream adjustments** (Ch 33, new) — the realism chapter. Programmes pivot; budget for it. New to the 2nd edition.
5. **Risk, trust, and the right to deploy AI** (Ch 34, expanded) — model risk, fairness, governance, with an explicit "right to deploy" gate.
6. **So, what about culture?** (Ch 35, new) — the closer; rewritten for the agentic era.

## 2nd-edition-specific watchouts

- **Ch 33** (midstream adjustments): probably the most candid chapter in the book. Look for failure-mode patterns, decision criteria for pivoting vs persisting, and concrete examples of programmes that adjusted course. High value for the GWTH course as cautionary material.
- **Ch 34** (right to deploy AI): the "right to deploy" framing is sharper than the 1st edition's "managing risk and digital trust". Look for concrete deployment gates, pre-deployment checks, model-cards or similar artefacts.
- **Ch 35** (culture): if the 1st edition's culture chapter was a wrap-up, the 2nd edition's may be more substantive given the agentic-era reframe. Highlight any concrete cultural anti-patterns or interventions.

## GWTH course application

- Ch 30 ("make adoption stick") is the single most important chapter for the GWTH curriculum's value-realisation track. Many SME AI failures are adoption failures, not model failures.
- Ch 33 (midstream adjustments) is a natural fit for a GWTH module on programme management and expectation-setting — gives course participants permission to pivot.
- Ch 34 ("right to deploy") gives the curriculum a concrete decision artefact: a pre-deployment gate. Could become a downloadable course artefact.
- Ch 35 (culture) is the natural closing module — mirrors the book's own structure.
