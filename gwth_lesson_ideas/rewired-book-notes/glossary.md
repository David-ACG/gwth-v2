# Glossary

McKinsey-specific terms that recur across *Rewired*. Definitions drafted from public McKinsey framing and the 2nd edition's Wiley description; treat all as `[TODO-VERIFY]` against the text until highlights arrive.

**Agentic AI** — AI systems built around autonomous or semi-autonomous agents that can plan, use tools, and take actions. The 2nd edition treats agentic AI as a spine running through the roadmap (Ch 5), talent (Ch 11), and technology (Ch 22) sections rather than a single chapter. Public McKinsey framing references "agentic capabilities doubling every seven months" — a Moore's-law analogue for AI progress. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Agentic world (talent model for an)** — Ch 11 framing: the team-design and role-design implications of agents joining human teams as collaborators. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Bite size (transformation)** — A manageable segment of the business used as the unit of transformation. The 2nd edition reframes this as **domains** (Ch 3) — coherent slices like a customer journey, a process, or a product line. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Core modernisation, surgical** — Ch 23 framing: focused, value-backed modernisation of core/legacy systems rather than wholesale lift-and-shift. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Data architecture** — The system of data "pipes" that moves data from source to consumer. Ch 25 of the 2nd edition: "No data architecture, no AI advantage". [TODO-VERIFY]

**Data product** — A reusable, packaged set of data with ownership, SLAs, documentation and consumers, built by a data-product pod. The "reusable building block for scaling" per Ch 26. Cross-link: `capabilities/data.md`.

**Domain (business)** — The unit of transformation in *Rewired*: a coherent slice of the business — a customer journey, a process, or a product line. The 2nd edition explicitly demotes "use cases" in favour of domains (Ch 3). [TODO-VERIFY]

**Economics of Tech & AI transformations** — Ch 2 (new) framing: the financial frame for transformation programmes, including unit economics, payback, value-at-stake. The Wiley description calls out "the new economics of Tech & AI transformations" as a 2nd-edition headline addition. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Embedded engineering** — Ch 13 framing: moving engineers out of central IT and into business domains that own outcomes. The 2nd edition's "revolutionary idea". [TODO-VERIFY]

**Employee value proposition (EVP)** — The package of reasons digital and tech talent join and stay. Touched on in Ch 10 ("Creating an environment where tech talent thrives"). [TODO-VERIFY]

**Force multipliers** — Section Six's 2nd-edition framing: adoption and scaling as the multipliers that turn working solutions into enterprise value. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Industrialising AI** — The practice of making AI reliably deployable at scale: AI platforms, MLOps, monitoring, governance. A 2nd-edition headline theme on the Wiley description. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Midstream adjustments** — Ch 33 framing: the explicit, planned-for adjustments a transformation programme will need as it learns. New chapter in the 2nd edition. [TODO-VERIFY]

**MLOps** — Operational practices for deploying, monitoring and maintaining ML models in production. The 1st edition had a dedicated chapter; 2nd-edition equivalent material likely sits in Ch 21 (AI platform) and Ch 34 (right to deploy). [TODO-VERIFY]

**Pod** — A small, multidisciplinary, persistent agile team. *Rewired*'s preferred unit of software delivery.

- **Product pod** — Pod focused on a customer or user-facing outcome.
- **Platform pod** — Pod focused on reusable services consumed by other pods.
- **Data-product pod** — Pod that owns a data product end-to-end.

**Proprietary data advantage** — Ch 29 (new) framing: in an era of commoditised foundation models, proprietary data is the strategic differentiator. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Reusable building block** — Pattern applied across data products (Ch 26), platform services (Ch 17), and solution templates (Ch 31): build once, consume many times. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Right to deploy AI** — Ch 34 (expanded) framing: the explicit gate that a solution passes through before being deployed. Shift from "managing risk" to "earning the right to ship". [TODO-VERIFY]

**Talent bench** — The internal population of digital and AI talent a company can draw on. Public 1st-edition framing: 70–80 percent in-house, 20–30 percent external. The 2nd edition (Ch 11) likely revises this for the agentic era. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Tech muscle (of business leaders)** — Ch 8 (new) framing: the tech and AI literacy and fluency required of non-technical business leaders to lead domain redesign. New 2nd-edition concept. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Three operating models** — From the 1st edition: **digital factory, product and platform, enterprise-wide agile**. The 2nd edition's restructured Section Three may not foreground these as the backbone — verify whether they still appear (likely in Ch 14 or Ch 16). [TODO-VERIFY]

**Transformation manifesto** — Section in the Introduction (p. 12). Likely a high-level statement of principles before Ch 1. [TODO-VERIFY]

**Use case (avoidance of)** — *Rewired*'s 2nd edition explicitly demotes use-case-hunting in favour of domain-led transformation (Ch 3). Useful term to flag any time the book argues *against* a default practice. [TODO-VERIFY]
