# Ch 13 — Embedding engineering talent in the business

*Pages 217–236. New chapter in the 2nd edition.*

## Summary

[TODO-VERIFY] The "revolutionary idea" framing is notable — McKinsey rarely uses that word. The argument: move engineers out of central IT and into the business domains that own outcomes. A material reversal (or at least sharpening) of the 1st edition's digital-factory emphasis. For domain-led transformation to work, the engineering talent has to sit inside the domain.

## Key concepts

- Engineers embedded in business domains, not centralised in IT.
- Reporting lines, funding, and career paths for embedded engineers.
- The trade-offs vs centralisation (consistency, platforming, career development).
