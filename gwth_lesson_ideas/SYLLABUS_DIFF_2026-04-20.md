## Key rationale

**Why add a new L2 (Mindset)?**
The current syllabus implies the colleague-not-Google shift but never installs it as a dedicated lesson. April 2026 research (Mollick, Karpathy, NLW, Priestley) converges strongly on this being *the* first lesson that matters. It's the highest-ROI 60 minutes in the whole month.

**Why merge L17/L18 into L19 and L19/L20 into L20?**
The current syllabus spreads capstone across 5 lessons (ideation, transcription, processing, distribution, polish). In practice L17+L18 (processing + distribution) run together in one working session, as do L19+L20 (polish + review/portfolio). Merging frees two lesson slots for the mindset lesson and deeper tooling.

**Why move Week 5 to Month 2?**
OpenClaw security is too high-stakes for Month 1 beginners given the CVEs surfaced in early 2026 (CVE-2026-25253 RCE, CVE-2026-32922 privilege escalation, ClawHavoc incident). The "advanced building" and "content pipelines" modules are more naturally Month 2 industry material.
