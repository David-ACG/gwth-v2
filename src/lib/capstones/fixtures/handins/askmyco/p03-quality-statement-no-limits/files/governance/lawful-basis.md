# Lawful basis: AskMyCo for Riverside Plumbing Ltd

Basis: legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Legitimate Interests Assessment

1. Purpose: let Riverside's six staff find answers in the company's own
   handbook and policies without asking the office manager.
2. Necessity: the documents name a few staff (for example who to book leave
   with) and suppliers' contact details. AskMyCo only stores what is already
   in those documents; no new personal data is collected.
3. Balance: the people named are staff and business suppliers who would
   expect their work contact details to be in company documents. Answers are
   only shown to signed-in Riverside staff. Customer job sheets are not
   uploaded.
   Checked against the ICO's legitimate interests guidance on 2 November 2026.
