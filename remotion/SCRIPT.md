## The one promise

**If you can describe what you want, you can begin to build it.** (Lesson M1 L01.)
Everything else in the script serves that line.

## Who it is for

Hand-picked beta learners and adults in the UK who use AI but suspect there is
more to it: reskillers, small-business owners, parents, and team leads.

## Soft CTA

Start free with real labs and no card; join when the work is worth it. `gwth.ai`.
