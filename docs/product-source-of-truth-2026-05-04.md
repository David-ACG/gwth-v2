## Positioning

GWTH is a UK-focused, beginner-to-advanced applied AI course. It starts with people who mostly use ChatGPT like Google and moves them toward serious applied AI capability: researching, building, coding, evaluating tools, completing projects, and staying current.

Public copy should say:

- Beginner-to-advanced applied AI.
- From ChatGPT basics to top 1% applied AI capability.
- Anyone can join, but the course is UK-focused and priced in GBP.
- Other countries launching soon.

## Audience

Primary starting audience:

- People who use ChatGPT mostly to ask questions.
- People who have not built much with AI.
- People who have not used AI deeply for research, coding, workflow creation, or practical projects.

Month-specific progression:

- Month 1: beginners and everyday ChatGPT users.
- Month 2: builders, small-business users, and people interested in becoming AI consultants.
- Month 3: enterprise operators, leaders, and consultants helping larger organisations transform with AI.

## Course Structure

Working structure:

- Month 1: 24 lessons.
- Month 2: 20 core lessons plus 15 optional lessons.
- Month 3: 20 core lessons plus 15 optional lessons.

This is the current product direction, not a permanent lock. The syllabus may change as the course settles.

Optional lessons are "go deeper" material that helps learners move from strong to exceptional.

## Month Outcomes

Month 1:

- Foundations.
- What AI is and what it can do.
- Basic practical use.
- Six primitives, with coding/building as the main spine.
- Target outcome: top 30% for applied AI skill.

Month 2:

- Deeper coding and building.
- Apps and workflows.
- Small-business use cases.
- AI consultant capability.
- Target outcome: top 10% with mandatory lessons, top 5% with most optional lessons.

Month 3:

- Enterprise transformation.
- Larger-company consulting.
- Organisational AI strategy and implementation.
- Target outcome: top 2% with mandatory lessons, top 1% with most optional lessons.

## Pricing And Access

Starter/beta pricing:

- `£29/month` per course month.
- Do not prominently show `£87 total`.
- Learners pay monthly and unlock one month at a time.
- Learners can stop after Month 1, Month 2, or Month 3.
- After stopping or finishing, learners can optionally pay `£7.50/month` for Stay Current.

Future pricing intent:

- The initial `£29/month` price may rise to `£39` or `£49`.
- The `£7.50/month` Stay Current price is intended to remain stable.

There are no cohorts. Learners join whenever they want and progress at their own pace.

## Lessons

Normal lesson completion requires:

- Intro video watched to at least 80%.
- Three-question multiple-choice Q&A passed.

Learners can retry Q&A immediately until passed. Lesson text reading is not tracked for completion. Small lesson projects are trust-based and not manually checked.

Every normal lesson should have:

- Short intro video.
- Pre-generated Kokoro audio.
- Project walkthrough where relevant.

## Labs

Labs are public marketing assets and bonus learning material. They do not affect GWTH Score.

Beta labs should focus on high-interest bake-offs between AI platforms and tools, such as:

- ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini.
- Codex vs Claude Code.

Each lab should include:

- Written guide.
- Embedded video.
- Pre-generated Kokoro audio where useful.

Labs may also be published on YouTube.

## Portfolio And Admin Review

Students submit capstone evidence from within lessons. Submissions feed a central portfolio dashboard for later career/LinkedIn use.

For beta:

- Portfolio is private by default.
- No sharing yet.
- David manually reviews capstone evidence in a simple admin page.

Submission statuses:

- Pending.
- Approved.
- Needs changes.

Needs-changes feedback includes a checklist of missing requirements plus an optional free-text note.

## GWTH Score And Credential

The score is called GWTH Score.

It represents:

- Completion.
- Demonstrated applied skill.
- Freshness/currentness.

Public percentile language:

- Top 30% for applied AI skill.
- Top 10% for applied AI skill.
- Top 5% for applied AI skill.
- Top 1% for applied AI skill.

Evidence model:

- Transparent GWTH benchmark rubric.
- External AI adoption/skill-gap research mapped onto GWTH milestones.

Score decay is update-driven. When a lesson changes significantly enough to affect practical skill, safety, or currentness, learners are notified and given roughly one to two weeks to retake it. If they do not, they lose the relevant lesson points.

Score remains visible to everyone. Only Stay Current users can retake updated lessons and prevent decay after course access ends.

The beta credential should include:

- Shareable/downloadable credential page.
- Public verification URL suitable for LinkedIn.
- QR code.
- Learner-controlled sharing.

Credential page fields:

- Learner name.
- Current GWTH Score.
- Score trajectory over roughly the last three months.
- Applied-AI skill percentile.

Do not show selected project evidence or project count on the credential page by default.
