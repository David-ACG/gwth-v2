### Your edition (What an institution gets)

Core, optional and exclusive tiers. Every lesson sits in a tier. You choose the core your members must complete, the optional lessons they may pick, and the exclusive lessons only your edition carries.

Exclusive lessons you ratify. Draft lessons written to your titles wait in your admin screen until a tutor ratifies them. Nothing reaches a member unratified.

A pass mark you set. Quizzes are graded on our server with the answer key kept off the browser. The pass mark is yours, per edition.

A tutor baseline view. Before a room starts, your tutors see who has met the baseline and who has not, so an advanced course is taught at the level it says.

A verified record. Each member gets a record they can add to LinkedIn and a verification page anyone can open. It decays if they stop, so it says what they can do now.

CPD ready. Completions and scores arrive shaped as CPD records, ready for whatever your scheme asks members to log.

### Worth asking first (Common questions)

Is it tied to one AI vendor? No. GWTH is independent: no sponsors, no vendor partnerships, and lessons compare tools rather than sell one.

Who grades the quizzes? Our server does, with the answer key never sent to the browser, against the pass mark you set for your edition.

What does a member's record show? Completed lessons, the project from each, quiz results against your pass mark, and how recently they refreshed. It has a public verification page.

How much of the content is yours? As much as you want. The standard course is the foundation. Exclusive lessons written to your titles are yours and stay behind your edition.

How quickly can we start? A staff pilot can start on the standard course within days. An edition takes as long as your curation does.
